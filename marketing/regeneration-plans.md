Public source of truth for launch positioning.

## The Rose Method(TM)

The method is the clinical rhythm behind the plan.

1. Analyze
AI-assisted skin imaging creates a visible baseline for texture, pores, pigment, hydration cues, fine lines, and other trackable concerns.

2. Evaluate
The provider examines the client, reviews goals and candidacy, and considers tolerance, home care, lifestyle, and timing.

3. Sequence
Care is phased instead of stacked randomly. The right service happens at the right time so results can build safely.

4. Track
Repeat skin scans and provider check-ins compare progress and guide adjustments.

## Why Six Months

A 6-month minimum commitment is required. Results do not happen overnight, and House of Rose is not selling gimmicks. We are selling true working custom plans that give the client enough time for healthy visible change to evolve.

Nothing carries over as unused public credit because the Rose Circle is not a punch card. It is a guided treatment plan at member rates. If a monthly benefit is not used, the plan still continues because the membership is built around consistency, progress, and provider guidance.

## The Rose Circle(TM)

The Rose Circle is organized by provider/service lane, not public tiers.

### IV Hydration with Diana

A monthly wellness lane for hydration, recovery, and internal support. Exact drip selection and cadence are confirmed after consultation and candidacy review.

### Basic Feel-Good Facials with Brandy

Relaxing monthly facial care. These are comfort-focused facials only: no machines, no invasive treatments, and no chemical peels.

### Wax Pass with Brooke

A monthly waxing lane for consistent upkeep, preferred access, and predictable scheduling.

### Advanced Facials with Amber

Corrective skin care for clients who need a true plan. Eligible services may include dermaplaning, BioRePeel, other chemical peels, body peels for areas such as back, arms, and legs, Face Reality acne support, and oxygen infusion when appropriate.
