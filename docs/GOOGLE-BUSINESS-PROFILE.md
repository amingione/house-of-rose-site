- **Monthly:** 3–5 fresh geo-named photos. Re-check hours (holidays/season). Add/answer 1 new Q&A. Scan
  competitor profiles (Sweet Spot, Carlisa, Nuview) for changes.
- **Quarterly:** audit categories vs. your live menu; confirm NAP still consistent; review which services
  drive calls (Insights → calls/directions/searches) and re-order services accordingly.
- **Always:** when a state-of-emergency/hurricane closure hits, post hours + reassurance immediately.
