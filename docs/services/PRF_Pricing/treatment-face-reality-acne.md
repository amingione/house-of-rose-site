### Why a program works where a single facial stalls

A good acne facial can leave you cleaner and brighter that afternoon, and it's a fine place to start. But persistent breakouts rarely hold from one visit, because nothing about your daily inputs has changed and the skin hasn't been guided as it shifts. The program adds the three things a stand-alone treatment can't: a **home-care routine that does most of the work**, a **check-in every two weeks** where Amber strengthens that routine in measured steps, and **coaching** on the pore-clogging products and triggers quietly working against you. Consistency is the mechanism — the whole program is built to protect it.

### How the program runs

- **Visit one — consultation and skin analysis.** Amber reviews your skin and history, grades what's happening, walks you through reading ingredient labels, and sets you up with your first home-care routine.
- **Home care, every day.** A Face Reality routine chosen for your skin type, tone, sensitivity, and breakout pattern. This is the part that carries the results between visits.
- **In-studio, every two weeks.** Cleanse, an enzyme treatment or acne peel, expert extractions, high-frequency and/or LED, a progress check — then your routine is adjusted for the next two weeks.
- **Coaching throughout.** Trigger guidance, product course-corrections, and steady reassurance through the adjustment phase.

The structured arc runs about **twelve weeks** (the Acne Bootcamp), after which Amber shifts you to a lighter maintenance routine.

### What it's built for — and honest expectations

The program is designed for the **appearance of** persistent, recurring, or moderate breakouts — congestion and blackheads, whiteheads, and the inflamed bumps that keep resurfacing — plus body breakouts on the back and chest. It leans on a gentle, mandelic-forward approach that tends to suit sensitive and melanin-rich skin prone to lingering dark marks.

Timelines are individual, and we won't promise a date. What we'll tell you plainly is that this is a months-long process, not a quick fix: with consistent home care, meaningful clearing tends to build over **three to six months**, and the daily routine matters far more than the calendar. One thing to expect and not fear: an early **purge**, where skin can look busier before it calms. It's a normal part of the process, and Amber will coach you through it. We'll never spin purging as proof it's "working" — just as something that can happen.

### Who it's for — and who we'll send elsewhere

**A strong fit if you:**
- have breakouts that one-off facials haven't kept ahead of
- want a plan, accountability, and someone adjusting it as your skin changes
- are willing to do daily home care and keep your biweekly visits for about twelve weeks
- have sensitive or deeper skin tones prone to post-breakout marks, or bacne on the back and chest

**Not the right path if you:**
- have deep, painful cystic or nodular acne or widespread, actively scarring acne — that deserves a **dermatologist's** evaluation first, and Amber will tell you so and coordinate rather than overreach
- are on, recently on, or considering **isotretinoin (Accutane)** — timing is a physician's call and certain steps pause around it
- aren't able to commit to the daily routine right now — the program only delivers if the routine is followed

Amber confirms whether you're a candidate at consultation. Not every client is a fit, and outcomes are individual.

### Questions people ask

**Is this just a facial with a fancy name?**
No — the treatments matter, but the value is the structure around them: the adapting home routine, the every-two-weeks adjustment, and the coaching. That's what actually moves stubborn breakouts.

**Do I have to buy the products?**
Yes — the home routine is the engine of the program, and Amber selects Face Reality products for your specific skin. It's a separate spend from the treatments, and it's not optional if you want the results.

**What if my skin looks worse at first?**
That can be purging, and it's usually temporary. Amber expects it, watches it, and adjusts. You won't be left guessing.

**My acne is severe — can you still help?**
If it's deep, cystic, or scarring, the responsible first step is a dermatologist, and Amber will say so. Once that's managed, the program can support your skin alongside medical care.
