## ProCell MD vs. ProCell Pro — serum tiers

Both are ProCell Therapies two-step growth-factor systems built on conditioned media from human bone-marrow mesenchymal stem cells (a Cellular Renewal Serum + a Healing Accelerator Serum). The difference is potency and target skin:

- **ProCell Pro** — the professional-grade formula. Bone-marrow-derived growth factors and cytokines to help **maintain and build** collagen and elastin. Commonly positioned for preventative or under-40 skin goals. *(Entry tier.)*
- **ProCell MD** — the medical-grade formula. Contains roughly **double the growth factors/cytokines** of Pro, plus supplemental **TGF-ß3 and IL-1** for enhanced pro-healing and anti-inflammatory benefit. Formulated for more significant **collagen restoration** and faster recovery, commonly positioned for 40+ or age-related concerns. *(Premium tier — priced above Pro.)*
- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)** — not a ProCell serum. This uses **your own** platelet-rich fibrin, drawn and applied topically, delivering autologous growth factors. Positioned as the top tier. *(At House of Rose, PRF with Amber is topical only — no injections.)*

## What's In It — Active Ingredients Explained

Plain-English reference for the front desk and for answering client questions. Keep it educational — final suitability is always the provider's call.

- **Growth factors** — Naturally occurring proteins that act as "messengers," signaling skin cells to repair, renew, and produce fresh collagen and elastin. They're the core actives that make microchanneling serums work.
- **Cytokines** — A broader family of cell-signaling proteins (growth factors are one type). They help coordinate the skin's repair-and-renewal response after treatment.
- **Bone-marrow stem-cell conditioned media** — The "hero" base of the ProCell serums. It's not stem cells themselves, but the nutrient-rich fluid *cultured from* human bone-marrow mesenchymal stem cells — concentrated with the growth factors and cytokines those cells release.
- **TGF-ß3 (Transforming Growth Factor beta-3)** — A growth factor tied to **smooth, even, low-inflammation healing**. It helps new collagen lay down in an organized pattern (the way healthy skin heals) rather than the thick, disorganized pattern of scar tissue. Client-friendly phrasing: *"helps skin heal smoothly and evenly with less inflammation."*
- **IL-1 (Interleukin-1)** — An immune-signaling protein that helps kick off the early repair-and-renewal cascade after microchanneling. Part of what makes MD the "more advanced correction / faster recovery" tier.
- **IGF-1 (Insulin-like Growth Factor-1)** — A growth factor that supports cell renewal and collagen production, included in the MD formula.
- **Hyaluronic acid** — A hydrating molecule (naturally present in skin) that holds moisture and helps carry the serum's actives into the freshly microchanneled skin.
- **PRF (Platelet-Rich Fibrin)** — *Your own* growth factors. A small blood draw is spun down to concentrate your platelets and fibrin, then applied **topically** into the microchannels. Because it's autologous (from your own body), it's positioned as the top tier. *(At House of Rose, PRF with Amber is topical only — no injections.)*
- **Daxxify** — A neurotoxin (used in the GlowTox treatment) delivered superficially via microchanneling to smooth texture and minimize the look of pores for an event-ready finish.
