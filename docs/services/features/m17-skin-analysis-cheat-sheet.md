# M17 AI Skin Analyzer — Provider Cheat Sheet

**Audience:** Amber, Diana, Brandy (and any future provider running the scan)
**Purpose:** how to run it, what every reading means, how to read the report out loud, and how to turn it into a plan.
**Status:** internal SOP. Not client-facing copy. Client-facing language rules live in §9 and in `docs/COMPLIANCE-COPY-RULES.md`.

> **The one line that governs everything below:** the M17 is a **planning and tracking tool**, not a diagnostic device. It shows patterns. *You* interpret. The client leaves with a plan, not a verdict.

---

## 1. What the machine actually is

| | |
|---|---|
| Device | M17 Intelligent Skin & Hair Analyzer |
| Camera | 20MP UHD, light-blocking hood, 18 high-CRI LEDs for reproducible lighting |
| Light modes | 10-spectrum — RGB (white), UV (Wood's-lamp range ~320–400nm), parallel-polarized, cross-polarized |
| Screen | 15.6" touchscreen; cloud + offline reports; SD card |
| Modes | Full-face + **spot mode** (up to ~50× magnification) |
| Extras | 3D facial map w/ zoom + slicing, future-aging simulation, **scalp/hair handpiece** |
| Report | Generated in seconds; shareable to phone or print |

**The three light modes, in one sentence each:**
- **RGB / white light** → the surface. Fine lines, visible pores, acne, surface spots — what you'd see, but sharper and standardized.
- **Cross-polarized** → *under* the surface. Kills glare so pigment and blood vessels/redness read clearly. **Parallel-polarized** does the opposite — it exaggerates surface texture.
- **UV** → fluorescence. Melanin absorbs UV and reads **dark** (sun-related pigment you can't see yet); porphyrins from pore bacteria **fluoresce** (congestion/oil).

## 2. Capture SOP — do it the same way every time

Reproducibility is the entire value. A sloppy re-scan makes the before/after worthless.

**Before they arrive (booking note / reminder text):**
- Come with a **clean face** — no makeup, no SPF, no tinted moisturizer.
- No self-tanner within ~1 week (skews UV + pigment).
- Ideally no exfoliation/actives the night before (transient redness).

**In the room:**
1. **Talk first, scan second.** Concerns, history, budget, timeline, what they've tried. The scan serves the conversation.
2. **Full cleanse.** Double cleanse if any makeup/SPF residue — residue **fluoresces under UV** and fakes porphyrins/UV spots. This is the #1 source of garbage data.
3. **Wait 5–10 min after cleansing** if you can. Fresh cleansing = transient redness that inflates the sensitive/red readings.
4. **Position:** chin in rest, forehead to bar, hood fully closed. Eyes closed for UV. **Neutral expression** — no smiling, no brow raise (kills wrinkle comparability).
5. **Hair fully back.** Stray hair reads as wrinkles/texture.
6. **Capture full-face**, then **spot mode** on the client's stated concern zone (this is the "wow" moment — let them see it).
7. **Log it.** Same name, same lighting, hood closed, so the follow-up is a true comparison.

**Re-scan cadence:** baseline before starting → then at series milestones or **every 3–6 months** during active corrective work. Same conditions, same time of day if possible.

**Downtime/aftercare:** none. Re-apply their SPF before they walk out — you took it off.

## 3. The readings — what each one is, what it means, what to do

The M17 reports five families. Treat scores as **relative and directional** (vs. same age/gender cohort), never as clinical values.

### A. Pores & congestion group
| Reading | What it actually is | Reads high when… | Your move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pores** | Visible pore openings, size + count | Oily/thick skin, sun-damaged laxity, chronic congestion | Microchanneling, BioRePeel, hydrodermabrasion; retinoid + niacinamide at home |
| **Comedo / Clogged pore** | Blocked follicles (open + closed) | Occlusive products, poor cleansing, oil | Extraction-forward facial, enzyme/light peel, Face Reality protocol |
| **Porphyrins** | Fluorescence from *C. acnes* bacteria in follicles — a **congestion/oil proxy** | Active oil + bacteria in pores. **⚠️ Also fakes high if makeup/SPF wasn't fully removed** | Acne-directed protocol, Face Reality program; talk cleansing habits |
| **Sebum** | Surface oil level | Genuinely oily/combination skin; also post-cleanse rebound | Balance, don't strip. Correct home care before anything aggressive |
| **Acne** | Active lesion count/area | Active breakout | **If inflammatory/cystic → Face Reality program or refer for medical eval.** Don't microneedle over active acne |

### B. Pigment group
| Reading | What it actually is | Reads high when… | Your move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Epidermis pigment** | Surface-level melanin | Sun exposure, PIH, freckling | Most responsive: peels (BioRePeel), microchanneling, tyrosinase-inhibitor home care + SPF |
| **Dermis pigment** | Deeper melanin | Long-term photodamage, deeper melasma-type patterning | Slower, more stubborn. Set expectations **hard**. Conservative, gradual protocols |
| **Brown area** | Visible brown discoloration overall | General photoaging | Peels + brightening regimen + SPF (SPF is non-negotiable or you're wasting their money) |
| **UV damage** | Sun-related pigment **not yet visible** to the naked eye | Cumulative UV. Almost everyone in SW Florida | This is the most persuasive image in the whole report. Use it to sell **SPF and prevention**, honestly |
| **Melasma** | Melasma-style patterning (symmetric, patchy) | Hormonal + heat + UV | **Heat and aggressive treatment can make this worse.** Go gentle. This is a "slow, managed, lifelong" conversation, not a fix |

### C. Redness / vascular group
| Reading | What it actually is | Reads high when… | Your move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sensitive area** | Diffuse erythema / compromised barrier appearance | Barrier damage, over-exfoliation, rosacea-appearance, recent actives | **Barrier repair first.** Do NOT stack actives. Postpone aggressive treatment |
| **Spider vein** | Visible vascularity/telangiectasia | Photoaging, rosacea-appearance, alcohol/heat | Manage expectations — this lane is mostly referral/light-based, not our core menu |
| **Thermal map** | Relative heat/inflammation distribution | Inflammation | Corroborating signal only — never the headline |

### D. Aging / structure group
| Reading | What it actually is | Reads high when… | Your move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wrinkle** | Line depth + count | Photoaging, expression, dehydration (dehydration exaggerates it) | Static vs. dynamic matters: **dynamic → Diana (tox). Static/texture → Amber (microchanneling, PRF microneedling)** |
| **Texture** | Surface smoothness — peaks and valleys | Scarring, congestion, dehydration, buildup | Microchanneling/microneedling, resurfacing peels, dermaplaning |
| **Collagen** | Estimated collagen density/support | Low w/ age, sun, laxity | Regenerative series — microchanneling, PRF microneedling. **Series, not one-off** |
| **Moisture** | Hydration cues | Low w/ barrier damage, dehydration, over-exfoliation | Hydrating protocol + home care. **Fix this before judging wrinkles/texture** |
| **Future aging simulation** | Projection, not a prediction | — | Use gently. It's a motivator, not evidence. Never present it as fact |

### E. Scalp/hair (handpiece)
Follicle density, scalp condition, oil. Routes to the regenerative hair pathway — **Amber topical only; injectable PRF is Diana's.**

## 4. Reading order — how to work the report without overwhelming them

**Read it in this sequence. Always.**

1. **Moisture / Sensitive first.** If barrier is compromised or hydration is low, *every other reading is inflated and unreliable*. This is the most common rookie error: treating texture/wrinkles/redness that are actually dehydration and barrier damage.
2. **Redness / sensitivity.** Is this skin calm enough to treat at all?
3. **Congestion + acne.** Active inflammatory acne gates almost everything else.
4. **Pigment.** Epidermal vs. dermal — this determines the timeline you promise.
5. **Texture / wrinkles / collagen.** The "improvement" lane.
6. **UV damage last** — it's the closer. It's what makes prevention land.

**The 3-finding rule.** Never present more than **three** findings. Pick the one that bothers *them* most + the one blocking progress + one win. More than three and they buy nothing because they feel broken.

## 5. Confounders — when the data is lying to you

| You see | It might just be |
|---|---|
| Sky-high porphyrins | **Makeup/SPF residue** fluorescing. Re-cleanse and re-shoot |
| Elevated UV spots / weird glow | Self-tanner, sunscreen residue, some moisturizers |
| Heavy "wrinkles" in odd places | **Stray hair** in frame |
| Diffuse redness everywhere | Just-cleansed skin, recent actives, hot car, hot flash, crying |
| Awful texture score | Dehydration + product buildup, not scarring |
| Big score swings between visits | Different expression, different position, hood not sealed → **your capture, not their skin** |

If you suspect a confounder, **say so out loud.** "That reading's likely picking up residue — let me re-shoot." Clients trust the honesty more than the number.

| Dominant finding | Lead treatment | Support | Lane | Concern page |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Wrinkles — **dynamic** (expression) | Injectable consult | — | **Diana** | `/concerns/fine-lines-laxity/` |
| Texture + acne scarring | Microchanneling / PRF microneedling; BioRePeel advanced | Series of 4 | Amber | `/concerns/acne-scarring/` |
| Active acne + porphyrins + comedo | **Face Reality 12-week program** | Acne peel, extractions, home care compliance | Amber / Face Reality protocol | — |
| Sensitive / low moisture / barrier | **Barrier repair + hydration only.** Nothing aggressive | Glo2Facial, hydrodermabrasion, gentle home care | Amber · Brandy | (proposed `/concerns/dehydration-barrier/`) |
| Pores + sebum, calm skin | Hydrodermabrasion, enzyme, microchanneling | Niacinamide, retinoid | Brandy (maintenance) · Amber (corrective) | (proposed `/concerns/pores-congestion/`) |
| Under-eye darkness | Distinguish **pigment vs. vascular vs. hollow/texture** on the scan first | PRF series | Amber (topical) · **Diana (injectable PRF / EZ-Gel)** | `/concerns/dark-circles/` |
| Scalp / thinning | Regenerative hair pathway | — | Amber (topical) · Diana (injectable) | `/concerns/hair-thinning/` |
| Body — stretch marks | Microneedling / ProCell body | Series | Amber | `/concerns/stretch-marks/` |

**Sequencing law:** *Calm → Clear → Correct → Maintain.*
Barrier and inflammation first. Congestion second. Pigment and texture correction third. Maintenance and prevention forever. Never sell step 3 to skin that's failing step 1.

## 8. Scripts — what to actually say

**Opening the scan:**
> "Before we talk about any treatment, let's look at what your skin is actually showing today. This isn't a diagnosis — it's a map, so we're planning from evidence instead of guessing."

**Presenting a finding:**
> "Here's what I'm seeing — [finding]. What that means for you is [consequence]. That's why I'd start with [treatment], not [what they assumed]."

**UV damage (the closer):**
> "This is sun exposure your skin has already banked but hasn't shown you yet. We can't undo all of it — but the ones that haven't surfaced, we can keep from surfacing."

**When barrier is compromised (the honest no):**
> "Your skin's asking me for a break, not a treatment. If I resurface this today I'd set you back. Let's repair for four weeks and re-scan — then we go."

**When the answer is "nothing":**
> "Honestly? Keep doing what you're doing. I'll re-scan you in six months. That's the whole recommendation."
> *(This is the brand. Say it when it's true.)*

**Handling "how long?":**
> "Epidermal pigment — weeks to a few months. Deeper pigment — that's a longer, slower project and I want you to hear that now, not later."

**Never say:** "the machine says you have…" — say **"here's what we're seeing."** You interpret; the machine images.

## 9. Compliance guardrails — non-negotiable

**The line to include on any client-facing summary (verbatim):**
> *"This assessment is intended to support cosmetic consultation and treatment planning. It is not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for evaluation by a licensed medical provider."*

**Never say / never write:**
- ❌ "diagnoses," "detects disease," "screens for skin cancer / melanoma"
- ❌ "medical imaging," "medical-grade diagnostic," "clinical diagnosis"
- ❌ "guaranteed," "erases," "eliminates," "anti-aging," "turn back the clock"
- ❌ "FDA-approved," "clinically proven" (not verified for this device)
- ❌ A hard indicator count in **marketing copy** (public sources disagree). Internally, "18 indicators" is fine — publicly, say **"multiple skin concerns across multiple imaging modes."**
- ❌ "about a minute" as a timing claim → say **"only a few minutes."**
- ❌ Any AI "database size" claim.

**Always say:** "helps us see / evaluate," "designed to," "supports personalized recommendations," "helps guide your plan," "for the appearance of," "many clients notice."

**Hard referral line:** suspicious lesion, changing mole, non-healing spot, suspected medical skin disease → **stop, refer to a physician/dermatologist.** The M17 is not a screening tool and we never imply otherwise.

**Photos:** private, used only for the client's plan. **Never posted without explicit written permission.**

**Pricing:** the scan is the front-door planning step — **no standalone price is published anywhere.** Do not quote or invent one. Treatment pricing is confirmed by the provider after consultation.

## 10. 60-second pre-client checklist

- [ ] Face fully cleansed — **no makeup, no SPF residue**
- [ ] Hair fully back
- [ ] 5–10 min settle after cleansing
- [ ] Hood sealed, chin + forehead in rest, **neutral expression**, eyes closed for UV
- [ ] Full-face capture → then spot mode on *their* concern
- [ ] Read in order: **Moisture/Sensitive → Redness → Congestion → Pigment → Texture/Collagen → UV**
- [ ] Present **max 3 findings**
- [ ] Plan = Calm → Clear → Correct → Maintain
- [ ] Right lane: Amber (advanced facials, PRF **topical**) · Diana (injectables, IV, GLP-1, injectable PRF) · Brandy (basic facials, facial waxing)
- [ ] Compliance line on anything they take home
- [ ] Book the re-scan
