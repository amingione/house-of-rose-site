The M17 AI Skin Scanner is a professional, 10-spectrum diagnostic tool widely used in aesthetic clinics and medi-spas. It maps 18 key skin indicators—including pores, wrinkles, and pigmentation—using a 20MP HD camera to generate accurate, data-driven patient reports and personalized treatment plans in under a minute.  
Core Features & Specifications 

• Multi-Spectrum Imaging: Utilizes RGB, Polarized, and UV light to reveal both surface-level and deep, subsurface skin issues. 
• 3D Skin Mapping: Creates a comprehensive 3D visualization of the face with localized zoom analysis and slicing features. 
• Predictive Analysis: Simulates future skin aging trends to recommend proactive, preventative skincare regimens. 
• Hardware & Software: Features a 15.6-inch touchscreen and an Android/Windows-based operating system with built-in cloud data storage and multi-device synchronization. 
• Dual Functionality: Also features an advanced handpiece attachment to conduct scalp and hair follicle analysis.
