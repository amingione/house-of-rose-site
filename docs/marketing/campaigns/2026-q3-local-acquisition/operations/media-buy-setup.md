## UTM standard

- `utm_source`: `google`, `facebook`, `instagram`, or `nextdoor`.
- `utm_medium`: `cpc`, `paid_social`, or `organic_social`.
- `utm_campaign`: `hor_skin_consult_q3`, `hor_advanced_skin_q3`, or `hor_injectables_q3`.
- `utm_content`: `{concept_id}_{copy_variant}_{format}`.
- `utm_term`: use Google ValueTrack `{keyword}` for Search; omit from organic social.

Example:

`https://houseofrosefl.com/skin-analysis/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=hor_skin_consult_q3&utm_content=rsa_consultation&utm_term={keyword}`
