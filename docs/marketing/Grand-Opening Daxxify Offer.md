# Grand-Opening Daxxify Offer

## Overview
**Daxxify Free Units Promotion** — A limited-time grand opening offer designed to encourage first-time customers to book their first Daxxify treatment during their appointment.

---

## Offer Details

**What You Get:**
- 20 free units of Daxxify with your first  appointment booking for injectables or an advanced facial

**Offer Type:**
- Grand Opening Promotion (limited-time offer)

---

## Eligibility

**Who Qualifies:**
- First-time Daxxify customers only
- Must be in-store at time of booking
- Must book DURING their current appointment visit before they leave or its not availabkle

---

## Important Terms & Conditions

**Booking Window:**
- Customers must complete booking **before leaving the store**
- If not booked during the appointment, the offer expires immediately
- No exceptions — offer is only valid in-store, in-person

**Offer Status:**
- Void if not claimed before customer departure
- Non-transferable
- Cannot be applied retroactively
