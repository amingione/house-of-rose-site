## 📐 How Semrush Measures AI Visibility (Methodology)

**AI Visibility Score** — A proprietary Semrush metric combining how often a brand appears in AI responses AND how consistently that appears relative to other brands.

**Mentions** — A raw count of how often a brand name appears inside AI-generated answers across prompts.

**Citations** — How often a brand's domain is cited as a *source* that the AI is pulling from to formulate its answer. Expressed both as raw count and as share of all prompts. A citation share above 100% means the source appears more than once per prompt on average.

**Brand Performance Bubble Chart** — Maps brands on two axes:
- High Visibility + Low Authority = AI talks *about* you but doesn't cite you as a source
- High Authority + Low Visibility = AI trusts you as a reference but doesn't mention you in conversations

**Co-occurrence / Brand Pairs** — Tracks how often two brands are mentioned *together* in the same AI response — the competitive proximity metric.

**Cross-platform source similarity** — Measures how much the top 100 cited sources overlap between platforms. The 4 AI platforms behave differently enough that they require *separate* optimization strategies, not a one-size-fits-all approach.

## 💡 Strategic Takeaways (implied from public index data)

- **Being cited as a source ≠ being mentioned as a brand.** You need to optimize for both separately.
- The **4 AI platforms don't agree on sources** — you can't assume ranking well on ChatGPT means you'll appear on Google AI Mode. Platform-specific strategies are required.
- **Brands with high mentions but low citations** are likely winning PR/content but haven't earned the "trusted source" status that makes AI cite their domain directly.
- **The prompt dataset is real user behavior** — not synthetic — which means the patterns reflect actual customer discovery journeys.
