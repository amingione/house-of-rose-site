## Revised concepts — grounded in the above

### A. "Color Match + Wax" — two tracks: everyday booking + an Open House finale
Same offer, two ways to get it: book it privately any day in the window, or come to the one-time open house where the raffle gets drawn live. Keep the pricing/discount logic identical across both tracks — the open house isn't a deeper discount, it's the same offer plus a live event on top.

**Track 1 — Everyday bookable (private, appointment-only, July 13 – Aug 8, 2026):**
- **Color match:** a proper Jane Iredale color-match + mini application, one-on-one at a reserved time slot — mom, teen, or both. Positioned as a **complimentary consultation when paired with a Jane Iredale product purchase** (department-store-counter model) — a value-add tied to a sale, not a standalone giveaway.
- **Discounted wax:** **20% off any wax service** (brow $25→$20, lip $15→$12, brow+lip combo $35→$28, or a body zone) **when booked in the same appointment as the color match.** A pairing incentive, not a public price cut — keeps it out of a race-to-the-bottom against Premier Wax Center or Mermaid Body Waxing.
- **Who does what:** Amber or a Jane Iredale rep runs color-match; Brandy (facial zones) or Aundrea (body zones) runs the wax.
- CTA: call/text or Book Online.

**Track 2 — Open House finale event (one-time, posted hours, Saturday Aug 8, 2026 — two days before school starts):**
- Format: walk-in welcome during a posted window (e.g. 10am–1pm). This is a one-time event exception, not a standing hours change — hours are otherwise Mon–Fri 9–5, Sat–Sun closed.
- Same color-match table + on-site wax stations, same 20%-off-when-paired pricing as Track 1 — no separate event-only discount tier.
- **Raffle drawn live at the open house** (e.g. 12:30pm) so kids take the backpack + school supplies home that day. Entry = booking a color-match + wax slot anytime during the July 13 – Aug 8 window (either track) OR attending the open house OR following @houseofroseaesthetics on Instagram — no purchase necessary, so it stays a simple giveaway rather than a regulated sweepstakes. Cap the backpack count to control spend.
- **Staffing:** Aundrea (body wax) + Brandy (facial-zone wax) run stations concurrently to avoid a bottleneck; Amber or a Jane Iredale rep runs the color-match table; Diana isn't a natural fit for the kids' side (optional parent IV add-on, see Idea 5 below).
- **Minors & consent:** signed parental consent at check-in for anyone getting waxed — don't rely on implied consent from attendance. Suggested age floors: 12+ brow/lip, 14+ body, parent present. Color-matching itself has no age restriction.
- **Scheduling:** requires a one-time posted Saturday exception — announce it as event-only, not a new standing walk-in policy.

### B. "Clear Skin Before the First Bell" — lead with the Acne Bootcamp
Market the Face Reality 12-week program ($899) directly at teens/parents. This is the one genuinely differentiated, high-ticket back-to-school offer nobody else locally runs. Be honest in the messaging: a 12-week program started in late July won't be "done" by Aug 10 — frame it as "start the plan now, walk into the semester already on track," not an instant fix. This also naturally pulls in Amber's actual specialty instead of routing the whole event through wax/makeup, which are her lowest-margin, least-differentiated lanes.

### C. Grandparent Gift Angle
Simple gift-booking push: "Send them off with confidence — gift a back-to-school skin or makeup session." Targets the 65+ population that's the real local wallet, rather than competing for the thin 11–24% household-with-kids segment directly. No new service needed — just a gift-booking CTA aimed at a different buyer than the parent.

### Geographic + timing fix for whichever concept you run
- Promote explicitly into Port Charlotte/Murdock Facebook parent groups and IG geo-targeting, not just Punta Gorda.
- Bookable window: **July 13 – Aug 8, 2026** (~4 weeks, pre-Aug 10 start). Open House finale: **Saturday, Aug 8, 2026**.

## Idea 2 — "Picture Day Ready" Mini Package
Single bundled visit (not a membership): brow shape + lip wax + gentle facial, sold as a one-time back-to-school appointment slot for anyone who can't make the open-house date. Bookable by phone/text only, same as everything else on the site.

## Idea 3 — Mom + Kid Paired Booking
Book mom's advanced facial (Amber) and the kid's gentle facial or wax (Brandy/Aundrea) in adjacent time slots so they come and go together. No new service — just a "book together" prompt in the phone/text booking script.

## Idea 4 — Bring-a-Friend Referral
Two moms book same-day back-to-school appointments together, each gets a flat one-time discount. No points, no recurring credit — stays compliant with the no-membership rule.

## Idea 5 — Parent Recharge Add-On
While kids are at the makeup table or in a wax chair, offer Diana's IV hydration as a 30-minute add-on for the accompanying parent. Turns the kids' event into adult-side revenue without folding injectables into a family event.
