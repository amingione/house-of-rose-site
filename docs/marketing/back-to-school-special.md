# Back to School Marketing Draft

## Market & brand research (2026-07-10) — read before picking a concept
Pulled real numbers instead of guessing. These change the plan:

1. **School start date:** Charlotte County Public Schools' first day is **Aug 10, 2026**. Event window should sit Aug 1–8 — the two weekends before, not the scramble week itself.
2. **Punta Gorda is not a family market.** Median age 66.3, only **11% of households have kids under 18** ([florida-demographics.com](https://www.florida-demographics.com/punta-gorda-demographics)). Port Charlotte is better but still older-skewing — median age 55.4, ~20–24% of households have kids ([florida-demographics.com](https://www.florida-demographics.com/port-charlotte-demographics)). Any promotion needs to explicitly reach Port Charlotte / Murdock, not just Punta Gorda proper — HoR's own `/areas/` page already claims Charlotte County broadly, so use that reach.
3. **The live site's actual positioning contradicts an "open house bash."** Tagline is *"Private, appointment-only regenerative aesthetics."* The internal rate card (`docs/services/pricing/PROFESSIONAL-MAKEUP-PRICING-COMP.md`) states the brand explicitly: **"a destination, never a deal."** A walk-in event with raffles and blanket discounts fights the brand you're actually running. Reserved private appointment slots fit better than an open house.
4. **Real pricing changes what's "free."** Makeup application is **$85 (everyday) to $150 (event)** per face — not something to give away wholesale at an open house. Advanced facials/channeling run **$185–$525+ per visit**. Waxing is cheap by comparison (**brow $25, lip $15, brow+lip combo $35** — `docs/services/waxing/face.md`), which makes wax the only piece of the original idea that's actually inexpensive enough to comp as a value-add.
5. **Genuine local differentiation is the Acne Bootcamp, not wax or makeup.** The master pricing doc states plainly: *"No local competitor offers ProCell growth-factor or PRF channeling."* Nobody else locally runs a structured Face Reality 12-week acne program ($899) either. That's an actual back-to-school pain point (skin + school photos + teen self-esteem) that's differentiated, not a commodity wax-bar promo competing with Premier Wax Center & Facial Bar and Mermaid Body Waxing on price.
6. **The local wallet is older, not younger.** 53.6%+ of the Punta Gorda population is 65+ (cited directly in the pricing doc as the core market). A grandparent-gifting angle taps the actual dominant local demographic instead of fighting for the thin under-18-household slice.
7. **Correction to the original draft:** the site does have a "Book Online" button (GlossGenius) in addition to call/text — the call/text-only CTA rule is specific to the Google Business Profile listing, not the website.
8. **Stale docs found in passing (not this task, but flag it):** `docs/services/waxing/*.md` and `docs/marketing/pricing-research/` still reference "Smooth Ritual memberships" and the "Rose Circle Wax Pass" — both retired 2026-07-07 per CLAUDE.md. Don't pull copy from those files as-is; worth a cleanup pass separately.

## Revised concepts — grounded in the above

### A. "Picture-Day Consult" (replaces the open-house Bash)
Private, appointment-only reserved slots, Aug 1–8 — matches actual brand positioning instead of fighting it. Mom books a real paid service (Glo2Facial $185, or a makeup application), and while she's in her appointment, her teen gets a **complimentary brow shape + lip wax** ($35 value) as an add-on — cheap enough to comp without contradicting "never a deal," because it's a value-add on a paid booking, not a standalone freebie or open raffle. No blanket discount, no walk-in bash. CTA: call/text or Book Online.

### B. "Clear Skin Before the First Bell" — lead with the Acne Bootcamp
Market the Face Reality 12-week program ($899) directly at teens/parents. This is the one genuinely differentiated, high-ticket back-to-school offer nobody else locally runs. Be honest in the messaging: a 12-week program started in late July won't be "done" by Aug 10 — frame it as "start the plan now, walk into the semester already on track," not an instant fix. This also naturally pulls in Amber's actual specialty instead of routing the whole event through wax/makeup, which are her lowest-margin, least-differentiated lanes.

### C. Grandparent Gift Angle
Simple gift-booking push: "Send them off with confidence — gift a back-to-school skin or makeup session." Targets the 65+ population that's the real local wallet, rather than competing for the thin 11–24% household-with-kids segment directly. No new service needed — just a gift-booking CTA aimed at a different buyer than the parent.

### Geographic + timing fix for whichever concept you run
- Promote explicitly into Port Charlotte/Murdock Facebook parent groups and IG geo-targeting, not just Punta Gorda.
- Lock the window to **Aug 1–8, 2026** (pre-Aug 10 start), not "late July–early August" loosely.

## Idea 1
> Back to School Bash:
 - An open house framed event where the kids and parents can shop makeup from jane iredale, get their makeup done to color match and show kids how to apply makeup and there is backpacks with school supplies raffled + discounted wax appointments available for face and body.
  - facial zones
  - underarms
  - lower legs(knees and under)

### Scheduling conflict — resolve first
Hours are Mon–Fri 9–5, Sat–Sun closed. A family event needs a time parents can actually make. Pick one:
1. One-time posted Saturday exception (event-only, not a standing hours change)
2. Thursday/Friday evening extension (5–7pm) — smaller lift than a full weekend day
3. Two sessions: weekday after-school slot (~3–5pm) for younger kids + a Saturday slot for teens whose parents work

### Staffing / lane assignments
- **Aundrea (Brooke)** — body waxing (underarms, lower legs/knees-and-under) — her lane
- **Brandy** — facial-zone waxing (brow, lip); can also run a gentle "first facial" for younger kids/teens from her Basic Facials lane (enzyme exfoliation only — no peels/actives on minors)
- **Amber** — Jane Iredale color-match/application demo table, or floats to parent conversations; her Advanced Facials lane (microchanneling, PRF, peels) isn't appropriate for minors, so keep her event role makeup-side
- **Diana** — not a natural fit for the kids' side; see Idea 5 below for a way to work her in on the adult side

### Minors & consent — must-solve before promoting
- Signed parental consent at check-in for any service performed on a minor — don't rely on implied consent from attendance
- Suggested age floors: 12+ for brow/lip waxing, 14+ for body waxing, parent present
- Event-day kids/teens menu = waxing + gentle facial + makeup only. No actives, peels, PRF, or microchanneling.

### Discount & raffle mechanics
- Wax discount: a flat % off (e.g. 20%) on any single zone booked same-day — a one-time event discount, not a recurring plan or bundle
- Raffle: backpack + school supplies. Entry = booking any service that day OR following @houseofroseaesthetics on Instagram — "no purchase necessary" entry keeps it a simple giveaway rather than a regulated sweepstakes
- Cap backpack count to a fixed number per session to control giveaway spend

### Promo copy angle (med-spa-allowed framing, plain naming)
- Lead: "Get Picture-Day Ready: Back-to-School Bash at House of Rose"
- Subhead: "Bring the whole crew — moms shop Jane Iredale and get color-matched, kids get brow/lip waxing and a gentle facial, and everyone's entered to win a school-supplies backpack."
- CTA: call/text (844) 941-7673 or Book Online (GlossGenius) — call/text-only is a GBP-listing rule, the site itself has a Book Online button

### Run of show (draft)
1. Check-in + consent forms for minors
2. Jane Iredale table — color match + mini application demo
3. Wax stations (Brandy: facial, Aundrea: body) running concurrently to avoid a bottleneck
4. Raffle drawing at close

## Idea 2 — "Picture Day Ready" Mini Package
Single bundled visit (not a membership): brow shape + lip wax + gentle facial, sold as a one-time back-to-school appointment slot for anyone who can't make the open-house date. Bookable by phone/text only, same as everything else on the site.

## Idea 3 — Mom + Kid Paired Booking
Book mom's advanced facial (Amber) and the kid's gentle facial or wax (Brandy/Aundrea) in adjacent time slots so they come and go together. No new service — just a "book together" prompt in the phone/text booking script.

## Idea 4 — Bring-a-Friend Referral
Two moms book same-day back-to-school appointments together, each gets a flat one-time discount. No points, no recurring credit — stays compliant with the no-membership rule.

## Idea 5 — Parent Recharge Add-On
While kids are at the makeup table or in a wax chair, offer Diana's IV hydration as a 30-minute add-on for the accompanying parent. Turns the kids' event into adult-side revenue without folding injectables into a family event.
