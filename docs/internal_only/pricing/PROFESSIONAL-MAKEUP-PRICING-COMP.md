# Professional Makeup — Pricing Comp & House of Rose Rate Card

> Competitive scan of Southwest Florida makeup pricing (2025–2026) and the resulting House of Rose
> price decisions. Sources are first-party artist/salon menus and vendor-entered
> WeddingWire/Zola/GlossGenius listings — national aggregator "averages" excluded from the math.
> Positioning: House of Rose is a **Medical Aesthetics Practice** — "a destination, never a deal."
> Price at the upper-middle of the corridor, below the Naples $250+ ceiling.
> _("Premium" as self-description is retired language — Creative System Book 1 §12. The pricing
> strategy is unchanged; only the way it is described is.)_

**Punta Gorda / Charlotte County specifically:** a *published-pricing desert* — nearly every local
artist/salon is "call for a quote." Reliable live comps come from the corridor (Sarasota/Venice to the
north; Fort Myers/Cape Coral/Bonita/Naples to the south). **Publishing clear, itemized makeup pricing
is itself a local differentiator.**

## House of Rose rate card (decisions)

Positioned at bridal-median / upper-event, single skin-first mineral finish (Jane Iredale — no airbrush
upcharge, which is a quiet differentiator). Prices are "starting at."

| Service | Price | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Event Makeup** (per face — prom, wedding, photography, special outing) | **From $150** | At bridal median ($140–150), above event median ($125–130); premium studio + Jane Iredale, below the $250 ceiling. |
| **Trial Run** | **Same as event application ($150)** | Market norm: separate paid session at ≈ day-of rate. Page copy keeps it as "same price as your event application" (single source of truth). |
| **Everyday / General Application** | **From $85** | Below event tier; an accessible entry for a day-to-day look. |
| **Event — In-House Individual Block Times** | **From $150 / person** | Single member to the studio; = event application rate, no minimum. |
| **Event — Spa Rental (Pre-Party Package)** | **From $175 / person · 4-guest minimum** | Per-person premium over base covers the hosted extras — private rental, hors d'oeuvres/pastries & mimosas, and a complimentary "Party Planner Infusion" IV per attendee (a hosted-event-only special, not sold à la carte). 4×$175 = $700, inside the $600–$1,250 party-floor band. |
| **Event — Travel (we come to you)** | **From $150 / person + travel fee · 4-guest minimum** | Matches corridor norm (per-face + travel + party minimum); travel fee quoted by distance. |

**Notes for the site:** the IV perk is described only as an included feature of the Spa Rental package —
**no standalone IV link or price** (hosted-event special only). Airbrush is intentionally not offered/needed
(mineral finish). Prices render as "From $…" and remain editable in Sanity.
