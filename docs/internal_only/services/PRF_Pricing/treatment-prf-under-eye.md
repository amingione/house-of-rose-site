### What PRF actually is

PRF stands for platelet-rich fibrin. It is drawn from you, spun in a centrifuge with nothing added, and used the same day — fully autologous, which is the clinical word for "made from your own body." Because it starts and ends as you, the risk of an allergic reaction or rejection is very low.

The part worth understanding is the fibrin. With no anticoagulant in the tube, your blood forms a soft mesh that holds onto platelets and growth factors and lets them out slowly, over roughly one to two weeks. That slow release is the whole point. It is a steady renewal signal to the collagen-building cells under your eyes, not a one-hour flood — and it is why PRF is called the second-generation version of the older PRP.

### Why the under-eye specifically

The skin here is the thinnest on your face, which is exactly why it shows fatigue first and why it is the wrong place to be aggressive. PRF suits it because it works *with* that thinness instead of fighting it. As your own growth factors go to work, many clients see the under-eye look less shadowed, less crepey, and more rested, with a quiet lift in skin quality rather than an obvious "filled" result.

That subtlety is deliberate. Heavier hyaluronic-acid filler can sit oddly under such fine skin — trapping water and reading puffy or bluish. PRF sidesteps that trade-off by improving the skin itself. If a true, deep hollow is the real issue, Diana will tell you straight: PRF alone may not be enough, and a small, precise amount of the right filler — or a combination — may serve you better. You will hear that at consultation, not after you have paid for the wrong plan.

### Who it's a good fit for — and who it isn't

**A strong fit if you:**
- read as tired even when you're rested — shadowed, dull, or crepey under-eyes
- want something derived from your own biology rather than a synthetic product
- are looking for a gradual, natural-looking improvement and are comfortable with a short series

**Probably not the right first step if you:**
- have a deep, structural hollow that really needs volume (we'll talk about filler instead)
- need a single-visit, dramatic change before an event next week
- are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a bleeding or platelet disorder, are on blood thinners, or have an active infection near the eye — these are deferred or ruled out at consultation

Candidacy is a real gate here, not a formality. Diana confirms whether PRF is right for you — and whether it's right *now* — based on your health history and what your under-eye actually needs.

### What a visit looks like

Plan on about **45 minutes**. Diana reviews your goals and candidacy, draws a small amount of blood the way a routine lab does, spins it while the area is numbed for comfort, then places the concentrate under the eye with fine needles or a cannula. Most plans run as a **short series of about three sessions, four to six weeks apart**, with a refresh usually every six to twelve months once you're where you want to be.

### When you'll see it — and how long it lasts

This is a grower, not a switch. Early changes tend to show around **two to six weeks**, and collagen remodeling keeps building for roughly **three to six months**. Results commonly hold **six to twelve months** and vary with your skin, age, and lifestyle — which is precisely why we build a series with maintenance rather than selling you a single session and calling it done. We'd rather set the expectation honestly than oversell the first visit.

### Downtime

Light, compared with more aggressive procedures. Expect some redness, mild swelling, and the chance of bruising for a few days; under-eye puffiness is often most noticeable the next morning and settles over one to three days. Diana sends you home with specific aftercare — including which supplements and blood-thinning habits to pause beforehand, when it's medically appropriate.

### Questions people ask

**Is PRF the same as the "vampire facial"?**
Same broad family — your own blood — but this is targeted injectable work under the eye, done by a registered nurse. No theatrics, no branded gimmick.

**Does it hurt?**
The area is numbed for comfort, and most clients describe pressure more than pain. The blood draw feels like routine lab work.

**PRF or under-eye filler — which do I need?**
It depends on whether your issue is skin quality and shadowing (PRF's strength) or true lost volume (often better served by a precise amount of HA filler). Diana makes that call with you; sometimes the honest answer is a combination.

**Can I do this with my other treatments?**
Often, yes. Because House of Rose also offers topical PRF microchanneling with Amber for surface glow, your under-eye plan can sit inside a broader regenerative sequence — Diana coordinates it so the pieces don't fight each other.
