## 1. The 3-sentence answer

Waxing at House of Rose Aesthetics in Punta Gorda is professional hard-wax hair removal for the face and body, pulling hair out at the root so skin stays smooth for weeks — not days. We wax by zone: facial areas (brows, lip, chin, cheeks, sideburns, neck, nose, ears) from $15, and body areas (underarms, arms, legs, back, chest, stomach, bikini, Brazilian) from $28. Facial waxing is offered by both Aundrea Pedigo and Brandy; body waxing is Aundrea's lane, and every wax uses gentle hard wax for a more comfortable, precise result on sensitive skin.

## 2. How it works

**Plain English.** Warm wax is applied over the hair, allowed to grip, then removed against the direction of growth. Because the wax bonds to the hair itself, it lifts each strand out from the root rather than cutting it at the surface the way a razor does. With the follicle emptied, the area stays visibly smooth until new hair grows back up through the skin — typically several weeks, versus a day or two for shaving.

**One level deeper.** House of Rose uses **hard wax** exclusively (confirmed in both the facial and body waxing menus). Hard wax is applied thicker, hardens as it cools, and shrink-wraps around the hair so it adheres to the *hair, not the surrounding skin*. Soft (strip) wax, by contrast, adheres to both hair and the top layer of skin — which is why hard wax is the standard for sensitive zones like the face, underarms, and bikini: less pull on the skin, less irritation, and it can be re-applied to stubborn spots without re-stripping live skin.

Hair grows in cycles — **anagen** (active growth), **catagen** (transition), and **telogen** (rest). Waxing is most effective on anagen hairs that are still anchored in the follicle, which is why a **4–6 week cadence** matters: it re-catches hair as a fresh cohort enters active growth. Over repeated sessions many clients notice regrowth coming in **finer and sparser**, because repeatedly pulling from the follicle weakens (but never destroys) the root. Regrowth from waxing also comes back with a **tapered tip** rather than the blunt, stubbly end a razor leaves — so it feels softer as it returns. Ingrown hairs are managed, not by the wax itself, but by keeping follicles clear: gentle exfoliation in the weeks *between* appointments prevents dead skin from trapping the new hair as it pushes through.

## 3. Who it's for / not for

**A good fit for:** anyone who wants weeks of smoothness instead of daily shaving; clients cleaning up brow shape, upper-lip or chin hair, or a full-face tidy; and body clients doing underarms, arms, legs, back/chest, stomach, bikini, or Brazilian. Hard wax makes it a reasonable choice for sensitive skin and coarse or fine hair alike.

**Not for / talk to us first (final candidacy confirmed by the provider at the appointment):**
- **Isotretinoin (Accutane) users** — waxing is contraindicated during treatment and for a period after stopping, because the medication thins the skin and raises the risk of the wax lifting live skin ("skin lifting"). The exact wait is a provider/prescriber decision; this is a hard safety line, not a preference.
- **Topical or oral retinoids** (tretinoin/Retin-A, retinol, adapalene, etc.) on the area to be waxed — these thin and sensitize the skin and should be paused ahead of a wax per provider guidance.
- **Sunburned, broken, irritated, or actively breaking-out skin** on the area; recent chemical peel, laser, or microneedling on the same zone.
- **Hair too short to grip** — hair generally needs to be about a grain-of-rice length (~2 weeks of growth) for the wax to catch.
- Certain medications and conditions (blood thinners, some skin conditions) warrant a conversation first.

**Honest limits.** Waxing is not permanent hair removal — the follicle is emptied, not destroyed, so hair returns. Regrowth *tends* to get finer and sparser with consistency, but results vary person to person, and not every client is a candidate for every zone. Individual outcomes vary.

## 4. The visit

**Protocol (typical, by zone):**
1. Quick intake — recent retinoid/Accutane use, sun exposure, area check, and (for brows) mapping and shaping the desired shape.
2. Skin is cleansed and prepped; hard wax is warmed to a comfortable working temperature.
3. Wax is applied in the direction of hair growth, allowed to set, then removed against the growth.
4. Pressure is applied to soothe; tweezing tidies any strays; a calming, soothing finish is applied.

**Sensation.** There's a brief sting on removal — most pronounced on the first visit and on coarse/dense areas (Brazilian, underarms). Hard wax's hair-only grip keeps it more comfortable than strip wax, and discomfort typically lessens as hair grows in finer over a series.

**Time.** By zone: **facial 5–30 min**, **body 10–45 min** (Sanity durations). A single small area (lip, chin) is quick; a Full Face (9 zones) or Full Legs runs longer.

**Downtime & aftercare (first 24–48 hrs):** skin may look pink and feel warm. Avoid heat and friction — no hot showers, saunas, tanning, heavy workouts/sweating, or tight clothing for ~24 hours; skip active/exfoliating products (retinoids, acids, scrubs) on the area for a couple of days. Keep the area clean and moisturize gently with a fragrance-free product.

**Between appointments:** begin **gentle exfoliation** a few days out and keep it up 2–3×/week to prevent ingrown hairs as new hair breaks through (the day-7–14 window is when trapped hairs turn ingrown).

**Results timeline & cadence:** smooth immediately; fine stubble reappears in 1–2 weeks; most hair returns in **4–6 weeks**. The menu's own guidance: **"Rebook every 4–6 weeks — consistency brings finer, sparser regrowth."**

## 5. Comparisons (honest)

**Waxing vs. dermaplaning — different jobs, not competitors.** Dermaplaning (Amber's lane, `/services/dermaplaning/`, from $135, 50 min) uses a sterile blade to shave off *vellus* hair ("peach fuzz") plus dead surface skin for a smooth, makeup-gripping "glass-skin" finish — it's a **skin-renewal exfoliation treatment**, and the fine hair grows straight back at the surface within days. Waxing *removes* terminal hair from the **root** for weeks of smoothness and is about hair removal, not exfoliation. Rule of thumb: **dermaplaning for peach fuzz + glow; waxing for actual hair you want gone for weeks.** They can complement each other on different areas — but you would not dermaplane a coarse-haired zone expecting waxing's longevity, and you would not wax to get dermaplaning's exfoliating finish.

**Waxing vs. shaving (and other at-home removal).** Shaving cuts hair at the surface — regrowth in 1–3 days, blunt/stubbly tips, and no cumulative thinning. Waxing pulls from the root — **3–6 weeks smooth**, tapered (softer-feeling) regrowth, and often **finer, sparser hair over a series**. Trade-offs to be honest about: waxing needs a little growth to work (you can't wax daily), and there's a brief sting shaving doesn't have. For clients on retinoids/Accutane or with very reactive skin, shaving is the safer interim option and we'll say so.

**Soft wax vs. hard wax — and why we use hard.** Soft (strip) wax grips skin *and* hair and exfoliates as it pulls, which suits large, less-sensitive expanses (some legs/arms/back protocols). Hard wax grips hair only, so it's gentler and more precise — the standard for **face, underarms, and bikini/Brazilian**. House of Rose uses **hard wax across the whole menu** (face and body), prioritizing comfort and skin protection on every zone.

## 9. FAQ bank

1. **What's the difference between waxing and shaving?** Shaving cuts hair at the surface, so it's back in 1–3 days with a blunt, stubbly tip. Waxing pulls hair from the root, so skin stays smooth for weeks and regrowth comes back softer and tapered — and often finer over a series.
2. **Does waxing hurt?** There's a brief sting when the wax is removed. It's usually strongest at the first visit and eases as hair grows in finer over time. Hard wax grips only the hair, not the skin, which keeps it more comfortable — especially on the face and bikini.
3. **Why hard wax and not strip wax?** Hard wax bonds to the hair rather than the surrounding skin, so it's gentler and more precise. House of Rose uses hard wax across the entire face and body menu.
4. **How long does a wax last?** Most clients stay smooth for about 4–6 weeks. Fine stubble usually reappears within 1–2 weeks, with most hair back by week 4–6 — which is why we recommend rebooking in that window.
5. **How long does my hair need to be?** Generally about the length of a grain of rice — roughly two weeks of growth — so the wax can grip it. Too short and it won't catch; too long can be tidied first.
6. **Will the hair grow back thicker or darker?** No — that's a myth. Because waxing pulls from the root, many clients actually notice regrowth coming in finer and sparser over a series. Individual results vary.
7. **Can I get waxed if I use retinol or Retin-A?** Not on the area being waxed without pausing it first. Retinoids thin and sensitize the skin and raise the risk of the wax lifting live skin. Let us know what you use and we'll advise a timeline at booking.
8. **Can I be waxed on Accutane (isotretinoin)?** No — waxing is not safe during Accutane treatment or for a period after, because the medication thins your skin. This is a firm safety line; your provider will confirm timing based on your prescriber's guidance.
9. **How do I prevent ingrown hairs?** Gentle, regular exfoliation between appointments (a few days after your wax, then 2–3×/week) keeps follicles clear so new hair can surface cleanly. We can recommend an at-home exfoliant.
10. **What should I do — and avoid — right after?** For about 24 hours skip hot showers, saunas, tanning, heavy sweating, and tight clothing, and avoid active products on the area. Keep skin clean and lightly moisturized with something fragrance-free.
11. **Who does my wax?** Facial waxing is offered by both Aundrea Pedigo and Brandy; body waxing (arms, legs, back/chest, bikini, Brazilian) is done by Aundrea. We'll match you to the right provider when you book.
12. **Do you wax bikini and Brazilian?** Yes — Bikini Line, Full Bikini, and Brazilian are all on the menu, all with hard wax for comfort. These are Aundrea's lane.
13. **Is the "Buy 5, Get 1" a membership?** No. It's a **prepaid Brazilian series** — you buy a pack up front and the sixth wax is included. There's no membership, no recurring fee, and nothing to cancel.
14. **Can I get a wax before an event or vacation?** Yes — book a few days ahead (not the same day) so any pinkness settles. Consistent 4–6 week clients get the smoothest, quickest results.
15. **Can I combine areas in one visit?** Yes — we offer combos (e.g., Brows + Lip, Full Bikini + Underarms, Full Legs + Brazilian + Underarms) at bundle pricing that saves versus booking each area separately.
