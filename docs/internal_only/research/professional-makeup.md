## 1. The 3-sentence answer

Professional makeup at House of Rose Aesthetics in Punta Gorda, FL is skin-first, event-ready application by Aundrea Pedigo, built entirely on **Jane Iredale mineral makeup** — "The Skincare Makeup" — so your skin still looks like skin on camera and through a full day. ## 2. How it works

**Plain English.** "Professional makeup" here is two related things: (1) a **service** — a trained artist applies makeup for you, tailored to your features, coloring, outfit, and the occasion; and (2) a **product line** — Jane Iredale, the mineral makeup House of Rose has standardized on. The service builds a look step by step (prime → base/foundation → conceal/correct → set → bronze/blush/highlight → eyes → brows → lip), and for events the priorities are longevity and a finish that photographs cleanly.

**One level deeper — why mineral, and why Jane Iredale specifically.** Jane Iredale (founded 1994, trademarked as "The Skincare Makeup") is a clean mineral line built on micronized minerals and formulated **without talc, parabens, phthalates, synthetic fragrance, or GMO ingredients** (per janeiredale.com). Mineral bases use pigments like iron oxides plus mineral filters (titanium dioxide / zinc oxide), which is why many base products carry a **physical (mineral) broad-spectrum SPF** — the same minerals that give coverage also reflect UV. Two practical consequences the pages lean on: (a) mineral formulas are **non-comedogenic and breathable**, so they sit better on freshly-treated or sensitive skin; and (b) mineral pigments **do not cause the harsh white "flashback"** that some light-reflecting conventional formulas throw under a camera flash — so the finish reads clean in photos. The line spans three pillars: **Makeup** (PurePressed Base, Amazing Base loose powder, Glow Time BB, Skintuition liquid, HydroPure lip), **Skincare** (the BeautyPrep system — cleanser, toner, hyaluronic serum, moisturizer — plus mineral SPF), and **Supplements** (the ingestible Advanced Nutrition Programme range).

## 3. Who it's for / not for

**A great fit for:**
- Anyone with a big moment — a bride and bridal party, prom, engagement/family/professional photography, a milestone event, or simply wanting to feel polished.
- Clients who want their **skin to still look like skin** (enhancement, not a mask) and prefer a clean, mineral-based product.
- Someone who wants to **learn** their own routine (a private lesson) rather than only be done up once.
- Clients pairing makeup with a treatment journey — e.g. a finish after dermaplaning or a Glo2Facial before an event, or a skin-safe base after a peel/needling once the provider clears it.

**Not the right fit / honest limits:**
- Makeup does not treat skin conditions — it is cosmetic. Active breakouts, very reactive skin, or a recent clinical treatment may mean we adjust timing or approach, or wait.
- **Post-treatment timing is treatment-dependent.** After microchanneling/microneedling or a peel, skin needs a compromised-barrier recovery window; makeup goes on only when your **advanced-facial provider (Amber) or injector (Diana) clears it** — not automatically the same day. Mineral makeup is often the *first* makeup allowed back, but "often" is not "always."
- Airbrush is **intentionally not offered** — House of Rose uses a hand-applied mineral finish (a quiet differentiator, and no airbrush upcharge). If a client specifically requires airbrush, we are not their studio.
- Final suitability, product choices, and post-treatment timing are confirmed by the provider at booking/consultation. Not every client is a candidate for same-day post-treatment makeup, and individual outcomes vary.

## 5. Comparisons (honest)

**Jane Iredale mineral makeup vs. conventional makeup — for post-treatment / sensitive skin.**
Jane Iredale is **non-comedogenic, talc-free, and mineral-based**, and many bases carry a physical SPF; conventional makeup varies widely and can include talc, silicones, synthetic fragrance, or comedogenic emollients. For freshly-treated or reactive skin, the mineral line's lighter, breathable profile is why it's a common choice for **"makeup you can wear soon after a facial or peel"** — described factually as formulation/tolerability, **not** as a treatment for any skin condition. Conventional makeup wins when a client wants a specific finish or brand the mineral line doesn't replicate, or when there's no skin-sensitivity consideration and they simply prefer another product.
*Compliance note: state the ingredient/format facts and "often chosen for post-treatment wear" — never "treats acne/rosacea," never "clinically proven," and always defer post-treatment timing to the provider.*

**Event/bridal application vs. a makeup lesson.**
An **application** is done *for* you for a specific occasion — the priority is a flawless, long-wearing, camera-ready result on the day. A **lesson** teaches you to do it yourself — the priority is skills, product knowledge, and a routine you own. Choose application for weddings, prom, and photos where the result matters most and you don't want to think about it; choose a lesson when you want independence, a routine reset, or a giftable experience. Many clients do both (a lesson to learn, applications for the big days).

**In-studio vs. travel / on-location events.**
- **In-studio — individual block times (from $150/person, no minimum):** each person comes to the Punta Gorda studio at a set time. Calm, private, one-at-a-time; best for smaller groups.
- **In-studio — Spa Rental "Pre-Party Package" (from $175/person, 4-guest minimum):** the whole party rents the studio privately, with hosted extras (hors d'oeuvres/pastries & mimosas and a complimentary "Party Planner Infusion" IV Hydration session per attendee getting makeup). Best when the party wants to get ready *together* as part of the celebration.
- **Travel — we come to you (from $150/person + travel fee, 4-guest minimum):** House of Rose travels to your venue and works your timeline. Best when the party is getting ready on site.
Travel wins on convenience and venue-day logistics but carries a travel fee and a party minimum; in-studio wins on the controlled environment and (for the Pre-Party Package) the hosted experience.

## 7. Pairings & pathways

**Treatment → makeup (the journey loop):**
- **Dermaplaning → event makeup.** Dermaplaning removes peach fuzz and dead surface skin, so foundation sits smoother — a natural pre-event pairing. Book dermaplaning a few days ahead, makeup on the day.
- **Glo2Facial → event makeup.** A radiance-forward facial before a big day; makeup finishes the look once skin has settled.
- **Bridal Prep Package (existing `package` — `bridal-prep-package`) → trial run → event application.** The prep package readies the skin over time; the makeup trial locks the look; the application delivers on the day. This is the strongest cross-lane pathway on the site.
- **Microchanneling / peel → (recovery window) → mineral makeup.** Only once the provider clears it; mineral makeup is typically the first makeup back.

**What makeup stacks with in the same visit / occasion:** waxing (Aundrea's own lane — brows/facial for a clean canvas) and **permanent jewelry** (`/services/[slug]/permanent-jewelry/`, a "get-ready-together" add for bridal parties). These are all Aundrea's lane, which makes bundling natural.

**Retail follow-through (the shop tie-in).** House of Rose applies and can retail Jane Iredale, so a lesson or application converts into a take-home routine via the **this-for-that swap guide** on the Jane Iredale page (primer → Smooth Affair; pressed foundation → PurePressed Base SPF 20/15; loose → Amazing Base SPF 20; liquid → Skintuition SPF 30; concealer → Enlighten Plus / Circle\Delete; setting → Amazing Matte / POMMISST; bronzer → PureBronze; blush → ColorLuxe Liquid Blush; highlight → Glow Time stick; brows → PureBrow; eyes → PurePressed Eye Shadow; mascara → Longest Lash / Lash Fixation; lip → Lip Pencil + ColorLuxe + HydroPure). **Jane Iredale is NOT yet a `shopBrand` in Sanity** (current retail brands: Procell, GlyMed+, Skin Script, Face Reality, House of Rose) — see GAPS; adding it would let the makeup pages funnel directly to `/shop/`.
