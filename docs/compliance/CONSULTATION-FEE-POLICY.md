## 1. Why this policy exists

Two distinct obligations converge on the consultation fee.

**The regulatory one.** Fla. Admin. Code R. 64B8-11.001(2)(g) makes it deceptive to represent that
services will be competently performed for a stated fee when that is not the case, and requires
disclosure of all variables and material factors affecting the actual fee charged. A fee advertised
without its conditions — expiry, combinability, what happens if the client is declined — is an
incomplete fee representation.

**The practical one, which is larger.** The campaign's strongest differentiator is a promise to tell
people the truth, including *"you are not a candidate."* That promise is only credible if the money
follows it. A client who pays for an assessment and is then declined has paid for a refusal. If the fee
is also described as *non-refundable, no cash value*, she has an obvious grievance, a receipt, and a
Google account. **This is the single most likely source of a complaint in the entire campaign** — not
because the practice did anything wrong, but because the paperwork contradicted the pitch.

The resolution is not softer copy. It is this policy, adopted before anything runs.

## 2. The core terms

### 2.1 Structural distinction that makes the whole policy work

**The fee and the credit are two different things and are governed differently.** Collapsing them is
what produces the complaint.

| | **The consultation fee** | **The treatment credit** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Payment for a service actually rendered — imaging, provider review, and a written plan | A stored value applied against a subsequent treatment |
| Refundable in cash? | **Yes, in the circumstances at §2.3** | **No** — a credit has no cash value, ever |
| Expires? | No | **Yes** — `[CREDIT VALIDITY WINDOW, e.g. 90 days]` from the consultation date |

Say it this way and both statements are true at once: *the fee can be refunded; the credit has no cash
value.* Say it the other way — one blanket "non-refundable, no cash value" over both — and the
not-a-candidate promise becomes a misrepresentation.

### 2.2 The terms

| Variable | Term | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Consultation fee | `[AMOUNT]` | State inclusive of tax status: `[TAXABLE / NOT TAXABLE]` |
| What the fee purchases | M17 AI skin analysis imaging, provider review of the imaging, candidacy discussion, and a written, sequenced treatment plan issued in the client's name | The plan is the client's to keep whether or not she books |
| Credit toward first treatment | Full fee amount, `[AMOUNT]` | Applied to the client's first treatment at House of Rose |
| Credit validity | `[WINDOW, e.g. 90 days]` from consultation date | Single, clearly stated window. Avoid rolling or discretionary extensions — discretion is a variable and becomes disclosable |
| Cash value of credit | None | Standard, and lawful, provided the *fee* refund terms at §2.3 are stated with equal prominence |
| Combinability | `[COMBINABLE / NOT COMBINABLE]` with treatment packages and series pricing | 64B8-11.001(2)(g) requires this be stated, not left to the front desk |
| Transferability | `[TRANSFERABLE / NON-TRANSFERABLE]` | If non-transferable, say so — it is a material limitation |
| Frequency | One credited consultation per client | State whether a repeat assessment after `[PERIOD]` is eligible again |
| Cancellation | Reschedule or cancel at no charge with at least `[NOTICE, e.g. 24 hours]` notice | |
| Late cancellation / no-show | `[FORFEITED / RETAINED AS CREDIT / REBOOKABLE ONCE]` | Choose one and apply it consistently. Inconsistent application is its own exposure |

### 2.3 Refund of the fee — the not-a-candidate case

**The fee is refunded in full, in the original payment method, where House of Rose declines to treat.**
Specifically:

1. **Not a candidate for any service.** The provider determines, at or after the assessment, that the
   client is not a candidate for any treatment House of Rose offers. **Full refund.** The client keeps
   the written plan and the imaging.
2. **Contraindication or medical referral.** The client is referred out — to a physician, dermatologist,
   or for evaluation of a suspicious lesion or medical skin condition. **Full refund.** House of Rose
   does not diagnose; a referral is the correct outcome, and the client should not pay for being
   correctly redirected.
3. **House of Rose cannot deliver the recommended plan** — equipment, scheduling, scope of practice, or
   provider availability. **Full refund.**
4. **Consultation not delivered as described** — imaging not captured, or no written plan issued.
   **Full refund.**

**Refund window:** processed within `[N, e.g. 10]` business days of the determination, without the
client having to ask.

**Where the fee is *not* refunded in cash:** the client is a candidate and simply chooses not to proceed.
The fee remains available as a treatment credit for the validity window. This is the ordinary case, and
it is defensible precisely because the client received the service she paid for — imaging, a provider
review, and a written plan she keeps.

**Do not create discretionary exceptions.** "Case by case" is a variable affecting the fee and would
have to be disclosed as one. Decide the rule, write it, apply it.

## 3. Customer-facing disclosure text

Publish this wherever the consultation is offered — alongside, not instead of, the § 456.062 block
(`FL-ADVERTISING-LAW.md` §5.1). The two are separate requirements and neither substitutes for the other.

**Long form** — landing page, service page, intake paperwork:

```
Your [AMOUNT] skin analysis includes imaging, a provider review, and a written treatment plan issued in
your name. The plan is yours to keep whether or not you book a treatment.

The full [AMOUNT] is credited toward your first treatment at House of Rose, valid for [WINDOW] from the
date of your analysis. The credit has no cash value and is [TRANSFERABLE / non-transferable]. It [may /
may not] be combined with package or series pricing. One credited analysis per client.

If your provider determines you are not a candidate for treatment with us — or refers you elsewhere for
care — your [AMOUNT] is refunded in full, within [N] business days. You keep your plan either way.

Reschedule or cancel at no charge with at least [NOTICE] notice. [LATE CANCELLATION / NO-SHOW TERM].

Candidacy is determined by your provider at consultation. Not every client is a candidate for every
service.
```

**Short form** — ad primary text, GBP post, email. Use only where the long form is linked one click away:

```
[AMOUNT], credited toward your first treatment. Refunded in full if you're not a candidate. Credit valid
[WINDOW]; no cash value. Candidacy determined at consultation.
```

**If the consultation is complimentary**, the fee terms fall away but two obligations do not: the
§ 456.062 block is still required (a free service is the statute's first listed trigger), and the
candidacy line still belongs in the copy.

## 4. Staff script — the moment that matters

Front desk and provider, at the point of payment. **Say it before taking money, not after.**

> "The analysis is `[AMOUNT]`, and it comes off your first treatment — you've got `[WINDOW]` to use it.
> You'll leave today with a written plan in your name, and that's yours to keep either way. And if we
> look at your skin and decide you're not a candidate for anything we do, or you'd be better served by a
> physician, we refund the `[AMOUNT]` in full. You're not paying us to tell you no."

**Never say:** "non-refundable" as a blanket term, "no refunds," "store credit only," or "all sales
final." Each contradicts §2.3 and hands over the complaint.

**Document the decline.** Where a client is declined or referred out, record the date, the provider, the
reason category, and the refund date. A refund you cannot evidence is a refund you did not make.
