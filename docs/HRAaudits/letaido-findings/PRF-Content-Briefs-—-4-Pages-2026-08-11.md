### Why this page wins

`under eye filler` (17,000) and `tear trough filler` (7,900) are what people search when they have the *problem*. PRF is the answer many of them actually want — natural, autologous, no HA filler risk of migration or Tyndall effect. The page should meet them at the problem and route them to PRF. Nobody locally competes on any of it.

### Structure

1. **H1:** PRF Under Eyes in Punta Gorda — Natural Under-Eye Rejuvenation
2. **Opening (100 words):** what PRF under-eye treatment is, in plain language. Lead with the outcome (brighter, less hollow, less shadowed), not the science.
3. **H2: What causes dark circles and under-eye hollows** — targets `under eye bags treatment` + `dark circles under eyes treatment`. Volume loss, thin skin, pigment, genetics. Sets up why a biostimulator beats a filler here.
4. **H2: What is PRF?** — platelet-rich fibrin, drawn from your own blood, spun to concentrate platelets and fibrin. 100% autologous, no synthetic material. Slow release of growth factors over days.
5. **H2: PRF vs. under-eye filler (HA)** — the decision section. Honest table: HA filler = instant volume, risk of puffiness/migration/Tyndall blue tint in thin under-eye skin; PRF = gradual collagen building, no migration risk, needs a series. Targets `under eye filler` + `tear trough filler`.
6. **H2: PRF vs. PRP under the eyes** — targets `prp under eyes` (5,700) + `prf vs prp under eyes`. PRF spins slower with no anticoagulant → fibrin matrix → slower, longer growth-factor release. PRP is the older generation.
7. **H2: What to expect — the treatment, step by step** — blood draw, spin, injection, 30-45 min. Honest downtime: swelling/bruising 2-5 days.
8. **H2: Results timeline + before and after** — targets `prf under eyes before and after`. Real patient photos, consented. Series of 3, spaced 4-6 weeks; results build over 8-12 weeks.
9. **H2: Pricing** — targets `prf under eye cost`. Real numbers. Link to `/packages/prf-under-eye-series-of-3/`.
10. **H2: Is PRF under the eyes right for you?** — good candidates, and honestly who it is not for (severe fat herniation needs surgery; active infection; certain blood conditions).
11. **H2: FAQ** — is prf worth it · how long does it last · does it hurt · how many sessions · can I combine with Botox · what if I have had filler before
12. **CTA:** book a consultation, Punta Gorda / Port Charlotte / Charlotte County named.

**Length:** 2,000-2,500 words. **Internal links:** to `/services/prf-injections/`, `/services/prf-microneedling/`, `/compare/prf-vs-prp/`, the package page. **Schema:** MedicalProcedure + FAQPage.
