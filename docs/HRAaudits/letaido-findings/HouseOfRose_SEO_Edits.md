## 1. Homepage — houseofrosefl.com/

### Problem
The homepage ranks at **position 9.3** for "house of rose" — a brand-name query that should land at position 1. It's also getting impressions but not enough clicks from searches for "house of rose aesthetics" (25 clicks, 106 impressions, avg. position 2.1 — performing well) vs. the shorter "house of rose" query which is slipping.

### What to Change

#### Title Tag
**Current** *(assumed from site pattern — confirm in CMS)*
```
House of Rose Aesthetics | Medical Aesthetics Practice in Punta Gorda, FL
```

**Suggested**
```
House of Rose Aesthetics — Punta Gorda, FL | Skin, Injectables & Wellness
```
> Why: Brings specific services into the title for non-brand searches while keeping the brand name front-loaded. Under 60 characters for the brand portion.

---

#### Meta Description
**Current** *(assumed from footer text pattern)*
```
Consultation-led skin, injectable, facial, body, IV hydration, and wellness care in Punta Gorda, Florida.
```

**Suggested**
```
House of Rose Aesthetics in Punta Gorda, FL offers skin treatments, injectables, PRF, IV therapy & more. Consultation-led care. Book or walk in today.
```
> Why: Starts with the brand name + city (reinforces brand query match), lists key services searchers may be looking for, and has a clear CTA. 155 characters.

---

#### Internal Linking Action
Ensure every other page on the site links back to the homepage using **"House of Rose Aesthetics"** as the anchor text at least once (e.g. in the footer, breadcrumbs, or body copy). This reinforces the brand-name association in Google's eyes.
