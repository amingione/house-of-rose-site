## Revised recommendation

The first report's blog roadmap is still valid — but it is now clearly **step 3, not step 1**. Corrected sequence:

**Step 1 — Google Business Profile (highest ROI by far).** 447,400 monthly "near me" searches resolve through the local pack. Categories, services, photos, hours, and review velocity. No blog post competes with this.

**Step 2 — One service page per treatment,** built like Skin Spot and Sweet Spot build them: `/botox/`, `/dermal-fillers/`, `/microneedling/`, `/prf/`, `/iv-therapy/`, `/laser-hair-removal/`, `/facials/`, `/waxing/`. Target the "near me" phrasing in H1/title, with Punta Gorda / Port Charlotte / Charlotte County in the copy. This is the proven structure in this exact market — both ranking competitors do only this.

**Step 3 — The PRF cluster.** `prf under eyes` (11,000 / KD 0), `prf vs prp`, `what is prf`, `prf microneedling`, `prf hair restoration`. Zero competition locally, and it is your stated differentiator. Highest-leverage content on the list.

**Step 4 — A membership/package page.** Copy the Wax Pass model. Skin Spot already proved it ranks here.

**Step 5 — The 33 blog gaps** from [[seo-content/content-gap-analysis]], for authority over 6-12 months.

## Method notes

* All six named competitors resolved to domains via web search, then verified individually in Ahrefs (`mode="subdomains"`)

* Punta Gorda zero-volumes are real zeros, not scoping artifacts — cross-checked against non-zero controls (`massage punta gorda` 100, `botox punta gorda` 20) in the same call
