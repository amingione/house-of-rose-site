## 1. The 3-sentence answer

Permanent jewelry is a clasp-free bracelet, anklet, necklace, or ring that Aundrea custom-fits to your body and micro-welds closed in seconds — no clasp to catch, lose, or fumble, so it simply becomes part of you. At House of Rose Aesthetics in Punta Gorda, you choose a chain and metal (14k gold-filled, sterling silver, or solid gold), it's measured to your exact size, and a quick, flameless spark fuses the final link shut — the whole thing takes about **15–30 minutes** and starts **from $45**. It's called "permanent" because there's no clasp, but it's not forever-locked-on: it can be snipped off anytime with small scissors, so it's an easy, low-commitment way to mark a friendship, a milestone, a girls' day, or a bridal party.

## 2. How it works

**Plain English.** You pick a chain and a metal. Aundrea wraps it around your wrist (or ankle, neck, or finger), marks your exact fit, and trims it to length. She links the two ends with a tiny **jump ring**, slips a protective barrier between the chain and your skin, and uses a small handheld **micro-welder** to send one quick spark across the jump ring's opening — the metal melts together and re-solidifies in under a second, closing the ring seamlessly. There's no clasp, so the join is nearly invisible and as strong as the chain itself. You don't feel the weld on your skin.

**One level deeper (for the curious).** The tool is a **pulse-arc micro-welder** — the same family of jeweler's welders used for fine repair work. It fires a precisely controlled, millisecond burst of electrical energy that creates a tiny plasma arc between a tungsten electrode and the jump ring, melting and fusing the metal **without flame, solder, or filler material.** Many welders shield the weld with a puff of **argon gas** to keep the join clean and oxidation-free. The energy is contained at the jump ring — a protective sleeve/card sits between the chain and your skin, and eye protection is provided for the brief flash. Different metals and jump-ring gauges take different power settings (gold and silver weld at lower settings), which is why the piece is welded by a trained hand, not a kiosk. The result is a clasp-free, custom-length piece sized once, to you.

## 3. Who it's for / not for

**Who it's for.** Anyone who wants a low-maintenance, always-on piece they'll never have to clasp — friends and sisters marking a bond, mothers and daughters, bridal parties and bachelorettes, a self-gift for a milestone, or a group looking for a shared experience rather than another disposable favor. It suits people who lose clasps, can't work small clasps, or simply want jewelry that becomes part of their everyday look.

**Who it's not for / honest limits.** A short, honest candidacy list applies (general beauty-safety, not medical screening):

- **Pacemakers / implanted electronic cardiac devices** — the micro-welder produces electromagnetic energy, and the industry standard is to **decline the welding process** for anyone with a pacemaker or similar device. (We can still sell a clasped version — the *welding* is what's contraindicated, not the jewelry.)
- **Pregnancy** — commonly deferred: swelling (especially later in pregnancy) can change fit on a piece that can't be resized, and hormonal shifts can trigger new metal sensitivities. Not a safety emergency — a timing and comfort call.
- **Known metal allergies / sensitivities** — choose the metal accordingly (see §5); nickel-free 14k gold and gold-filled are the safest options, sterling silver is safe for most. A client with a strong, known reaction to a specific metal should skip that metal.
- **Sensitivity to bright/flashing light** — the weld produces a brief flash; eye protection is provided, but flag it.
- **Fit realities** — it's welded to one size. Significant weight change, swelling, or a growing child's wrist means it may need to be re-welded to a new length later. Set that expectation up front.
- **It can be cut off anytime** — so "commitment" is emotional, not literal. That's a *selling point*, not a caveat.

Final fit, metal, and suitability are confirmed by Aundrea at the appointment. Individual comfort and outcomes vary.

## 4. The visit

**Duration:** ~15–30 minutes for a single piece (Sanity). Group events run longer by headcount.

**Step by step:**
1. **Choose** — you pick your chain style and metal (14k gold-filled, sterling silver, or solid gold), and any charm or connector.
2. **Fit** — Aundrea wraps the chain to your wrist/ankle/neck/finger and marks your exact size, allowing for natural movement and comfort (not too tight).
3. **Prep** — she trims the chain, adds the jump ring, positions the seam, slips a protective barrier between chain and skin, and hands you eye protection.
4. **Weld** — one quick spark fuses the jump ring closed (under a second). You feel nothing on your skin.
5. **Reveal** — she checks the join, adds any charms, and you're done. It's ready to wear immediately — shower, sleep, swim.

**Sensation & downtime.** None to speak of — you don't feel the weld through the skin barrier, there's no needle, no aftercare, no downtime. You walk out wearing it.

**Care (everyday):**
- **Shower & swim freely** — gold-filled and solid gold handle water of any temperature and normal soaps/shampoos. Frequent chlorine or salt water can dull shine over time (most noticeable on **sterling silver**); a quick fresh-water rinse afterward helps.
- **Wipe** gently with a soft cloth to remove oils and lotion; a soft toothbrush with mild soapy water for detail.
- **Avoid** bleach and harsh cleaning chemicals; take it off the skin's-worth of caution around anything you'd protect fine jewelry from.

**Removal & re-weld.** It's designed to stay, but it is **not locked on for life** — you (or Aundrea) can snip it off in seconds with small scissors or clippers at any time; nothing about removal harms your skin. Save the piece and it can often be re-welded. (House of Rose re-weld / repair terms are a business decision — **pending Aundrea**; see §8.)

**Travel & medical notes (say these plainly on the page):**
- **Airport security / TSA** — most permanent jewelry is small and non-magnetic and typically does **not** set off detectors; you generally don't need to remove it.
- **MRI / surgery** — you **must remove it** before an MRI or many surgeries (metal + MRI magnets don't mix, and it can interfere with imaging). Snip it off beforehand; it can be re-welded afterward.

**Results "timeline."** There isn't one — it's an object, not a treatment. It looks its best from day one; a well-made piece simply *lasts* (see §5 for how long each metal holds up).

## 5. Comparisons *(honest — the choices a client actually weighs)*

### Permanent jewelry vs. traditional clasp jewelry
| | Permanent (welded) | Traditional (clasp) |
|---|---|---|
| Closure | No clasp — welded jump ring, seamless | Clasp you open/close |
| Fit | Custom-measured to *you*, once | One-size or adjustable |
| On/off | Stays on; snip to remove | Take on/off daily |
| Loss risk | Very low (nothing to unclasp) | Clasps fail, pieces get lost |
| Feel | "Part of you," forget it's there | A thing you put on |
| Best when | You want an always-on keepsake / shared moment | You want to swap and restyle often |

**When traditional wins:** if you like to change jewelry daily, layer differently by outfit, or don't want any commitment at all, a clasped piece is the better tool. Permanent jewelry trades flexibility for the seamless, always-on experience.

### Metal options — durability, tarnish, price tier *(general jewelry facts, not House of Rose claims)*
| Metal | What it is | Durability (continuous wear) | Tarnish | Hypoallergenic | Relative price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sterling silver** | 92.5% silver + copper | Durable for everyday wear; softer look | **Tarnishes** — reacts with sulfur in air; dulls faster with chlorine/salt; cleans easily | Safe for most; small % react to the copper | **Entry** |
| **14k gold-filled** | Thick layer of solid 14k **heat/pressure-bonded** to a core (not plating) | Looks identical to solid gold; roughly **2–5 years** of continuous permanent wear before any change | Does **not** tarnish under normal wear (gold layer thick enough to shield the core) | Nickel-free; real gold sits against skin — one of the safest options | **Mid** (the popular default) |
| **14k solid gold** | 58.3% gold **throughout** — no core to wear through | Best for 24/7 everyday wear; no base metal to ever expose | Effectively doesn't tarnish (very minor surface dullness over years at most) | Nickel-free 14k gold is the most reliably hypoallergenic | **Premium** (tiered by chain weight) |

**Honest framing:** gold-filled is the sweet spot for most permanent pieces — the solid-gold look and near-tarnish-free wear at a fraction of solid-gold cost — but it is *not* infinitely permanent (the gold layer can eventually wear on a continuously worn piece over years). Solid gold is the true "wear it forever" choice and priced accordingly. Sterling silver is the accessible entry point but wants a little more care (rinse after pool/ocean, occasional polish).

### Single piece vs. group / party booking
| | Single (one-on-one) | Group / party |
|---|---|---|
| Vibe | Quiet, personal, quick | Shared experience, the *reason* to book |
| Best for | A self-gift, a quick milestone, adding a charm | Bridal parties, bachelorettes, girls' days, mother-daughter, birthdays, corporate gifting |
| Time | 15–30 min | Scaled to headcount |
| Why choose it | Convenience | The moment — choosing chains together, the tiny spark, matching keepsakes everyone leaves with |

**When each wins:** book **single** when you just want the piece; book a **group event** when the point is the gathering — permanent jewelry is one of the few "activities" that leaves everyone with a lasting, personal keepsake instead of a forgotten favor. ## 9. FAQ bank *(answer-first, compliance-clean)*

1. **What is permanent jewelry?** It's a clasp-free bracelet, anklet, necklace, or ring welded to a custom fit — no clasp to open, so it stays on until you choose to remove it. At House of Rose it starts from $45 and takes about 15–30 minutes.
2. **Is it really permanent — can it come off?** It's "permanent" only in that there's no clasp. It can be snipped off in seconds with small scissors anytime, by you or by us — nothing about removal harms your skin. Many clients keep the piece and have it re-welded.
3. **Does the welding hurt? Can I feel it?** No. A protective barrier sits between the chain and your skin, and the weld happens at the jump ring in under a second — you don't feel it on your skin. You'll wear eye protection for the brief flash.
4. **How long does it take?** About 15–30 minutes for a single piece. Group events run longer depending on how many guests.
5. **Can I shower, swim, and sleep in it?** Yes. Gold-filled and solid gold handle water and everyday wear; rinse with fresh water after chlorine or salt water to keep the shine. Sterling silver benefits from an occasional gentle polish.
6. **Which metal should I choose?** Sterling silver is the accessible entry; 14k gold-filled is the popular default (looks like solid gold, resists tarnish, nickel-free); 14k solid gold is the premium, wear-forever option. Aundrea will help you match metal to budget and skin sensitivity at your visit.
7. **Will it tarnish?** Solid gold effectively won't, and quality 14k gold-filled resists tarnish under normal wear. Sterling silver can tarnish over time but cleans up easily. Individual wear varies.
8. **Is it safe for sensitive skin / metal allergies?** Nickel-free 14k gold and gold-filled are among the safest options, and sterling silver suits most people. If you have a known metal allergy, tell Aundrea so she can guide the metal choice — not every metal suits every client.
9. **Can I get it through airport security?** Usually yes — permanent jewelry is small and typically non-magnetic, so it generally doesn't set off detectors and doesn't need to be removed.
10. **What about an MRI or surgery?** You'll need to remove it beforehand — metal and MRI magnets don't mix, and it can interfere with imaging. Snip it off before your scan; it can be re-welded afterward.
11. **Can I get one if I have a pacemaker?** We don't perform the welding process for anyone with a pacemaker or similar implanted cardiac device, because welding produces electromagnetic energy. Talk to us about a clasped alternative instead.
12. **Can I get one while pregnant?** It's usually best to wait — swelling can change the fit on a piece that can't be resized, and pregnancy can bring new skin sensitivities. It's a comfort-and-timing call, not an emergency.
13. **Can I add charms or a second chain later?** Yes — charms and birthstone/diamond connectors can be added, and a second chain can be layered onto an existing weld at a later visit. It's a piece that grows with you.
14. **Can I host a group or party?** Yes — bridal parties, bachelorettes, girls' days, and mother-daughter sessions are some of the most popular reasons to book. Everyone chooses their chain and leaves with a matching, lasting keepsake. Contact us to arrange a group booking.
15. **What if it breaks or the weld pops?** Bring it back — a broken jump ring can usually be re-welded. (Re-weld and repair terms are confirmed at booking.)
