## 1. The 3-sentence answer

The Glo2Facial by Geneo is a non-invasive, no-downtime signature facial that does three things in one pass — gently exfoliates, floods the skin's surface with active infusions, and triggers the skin's own oxygenation from within — for an immediate, luminous "lit-from-within" glow. At House of Rose in Punta Gorda it's performed by Amber as our advanced-facial gateway (investment from $185), and it's the treatment most clients book before a wedding, reunion, photo session, or event because there's no peeling, redness, or recovery. It also works as a finishing layer added to microchanneling or microneedling to send you home glowing rather than flushed.

## 2. How it works

**Plain English.** The Geneo handpiece glides across your skin with a single-use OxyPod capsule and its matched gel. As the capsule reacts with the gel it fizzes into a blanket of tiny carbon-dioxide (CO₂) bubbles — this is the same gentle "Oxfoliation" step you feel as a soft tingle. Three things happen at once: (1) the fizzing action lightly buffs away dull surface cells (exfoliation); (2) with that top layer softened, the pod's active ingredients infuse more easily (infusion); and (3) the CO₂ at the surface prompts your body to send oxygen-rich blood up to the treated skin (oxygenation). The visit finishes with a lymphatic-style facial massage, so you leave plump, bright, and calm — no downtime.

**One level deeper (the Bohr effect).** The oxygenation isn't oxygen sprayed *onto* the skin — it's the skin oxygenating *itself*. The pod chemistry is a classic effervescent reaction: sodium bicarbonate + citric acid react in the gel to release CO₂ (3NaHCO₃ + C₆H₈O₇ → Na₃C₆H₅O₇ + 3CO₂ + 3H₂O, per Diaz et al., JCAD 2017). A local rise in CO₂ at the skin lowers local pH, which shifts the oxygen-hemoglobin dissociation curve to the right — the **Bohr effect** — so hemoglobin in the capillaries releases more oxygen into the surrounding tissue. In parallel, the light mechanical exfoliation transiently increases the skin's permeability, which helps the infused actives absorb, and boosts local circulation. This is why Geneo is described as a **non-invasive carboxytherapy facial**: it borrows carboxytherapy's CO₂-and-Bohr-effect logic without any needles or injections.

**On radiofrequency (important accuracy note).** The original geneO+ platform in the 2017 study paired OxyGeneo with a separate TriPollar RF applicator; current Geneo systems also offer an optional RF module. **House of Rose's standard Glo2Facial should be described as the three-in-one oxygenating facial (exfoliate + infuse + oxygenate) — do not imply RF, skin-tightening from RF, or "neocollagenesis" unless the studio confirms an RF module is actually part of the protocol.** Keep mechanism claims to what we actually deliver.

## 3. Who it's for / not for

**Great for:**
- Anyone wanting an **immediate glow with zero downtime** — the classic "event facial" before a wedding, reunion, photoshoot, or holiday.
- **First-timers and sensitive skin.** No aggressive suction, no acids required, comfortable — a gentle on-ramp to House of Rose's advanced-skin lane.
- **Dull, dry, tired, or dehydrated skin** wanting brightness, softness, and hydration.
- Clients who want the **feel-good spa experience** (massage, glow) rather than corrective downtime.
- As a **finish/add-on** after microchanneling or microneedling to calm and brighten.
- A broad range of skin tones — the underlying OxyGeneo study included Fitzpatrick I–VI.

**Honest limits — what it is not:**
- It is **not a collagen-induction / corrective treatment.** For deeper acne-scar texture, defined scars, stretch marks, or significant laxity, needling (microchanneling/microneedling) is the right tool — Glo2Facial complements those, it doesn't replace them.
- Results are **cumulative and gradual on texture and tone**; the standalone "wow" is the glow and hydration, which is immediate but temporary. A series builds and sustains the skin-quality changes.
- It does **not** replace medical treatment for any skin condition.

**Not a candidate / check with the provider first (candidacy is always the provider's call at consultation):**
- **Active dermatologic conditions in the treatment area** (active breakout flares, eczema/dermatitis, open lesions), active infection, or broken/sunburned skin.
- **Recent resurfacing** (recent peels, laser, ablative or invasive procedures) or recent injectables/filler in the area — spacing is the provider's decision.
- Known **allergies to skincare ingredients**; the provider selects the pod/gel to your skin.
- **Pregnancy / breastfeeding:** the manufacturer states Glo2Facial has not been formally tested on pregnant persons and advises consulting your physician; many practitioners consider the gentle, non-invasive facial suitable during pregnancy, but at House of Rose **the provider confirms candidacy and pod selection at consultation.** (Do not blanket-market it as "pregnancy-safe.")
- Vascular disorders in the treatment area, or a history of skin cancer in the area — flag for provider review.

*Not every client is a candidate for every service. Individual outcomes vary. Final candidacy and pod/serum selection are confirmed by the licensed provider at consultation and skin analysis.*

## 4. The visit

**Duration:** ~60 minutes (per Sanity + master menu).

**Step-by-step protocol (experience guidance, not a medical protocol):**
1. **Cleanse & skin analysis.** Amber reviews goals, skin type, and any recent treatments; selects the OxyPod/gel matched to your skin (hydration/rejuvenation vs. brightening lanes).
2. **Oxfoliation pass (exfoliate + infuse + oxygenate).** The Geneo handpiece with the single-use pod glides over the skin; you feel a light effervescent tingle and warmth as the CO₂ bubbles form. This is the three-in-one core.
3. **Infusion / mask step.** Actives are worked in; a soothing/hydrating mask or serum layer supports the glow.
4. **Facial + lymphatic-style massage.** A relaxing massage finishes the treatment for a calmer, de-puffed, luminous result.
5. **SPF & send-off.** Sunscreen applied; home-care guidance.

**Sensation:** comfortable — a mild tingle and gentle abrasion during the pass, warmth, then a relaxing massage. In the OxyGeneo study patients described minimal-to-no pain.

**Downtime:** **none.** No peeling, no forced redness, no recovery — this is the treatment's headline feature and why it's the "event facial." You can wear makeup and return to your day. (Never *guarantee* zero downtime for a specific person; say "typically no downtime.")

**Aftercare:** keep skin hydrated, wear SPF daily, avoid harsh actives/retinoids and heavy sun/heat for ~24–48 hours per the provider's guidance. Follow the take-home routine Amber recommends.

**Results timeline:**
- **Immediate:** glow, hydration, softness, brightness after the single visit — best seen 1–3 days out; ideal timing for an event is a day or two before.
- **Cumulative:** in the OxyGeneo clinical study (6 weekly sessions over ~2 months), evaluators and patients reported improvement in skin texture, brightness, and tone, with more modest change in fine lines/wrinkles. Texture/tone gains build over a series.

**Cadence:** the standalone glow is a lovely one-off or monthly maintenance facial. For visible skin-quality change, a **series of 3–6 sessions ~4–6 weeks apart** is the norm, then individualized maintenance. House of Rose offers Glo2Facial as **Single · Series of 3 (Renewal) · Series of 6 (Full Correction)** (see §8).

## 5. Comparisons (honest — vs. treatments we offer)

**Glo2Facial vs. hydrodermabrasion (Brandy's basic-facial lane).**
Both are gentle, exfoliating, no-downtime "glow" facials — the honest overlap is real. Hydrodermabrasion uses water/serum + light suction to exfoliate and hydrate; Glo2Facial uses the CO₂/Bohr-effect **oxygenation** step and active-pod infusion on top of light exfoliation, plus the finishing massage. **When hydrodermabrasion wins:** a relaxing, budget-friendly maintenance cleanse-and-glow, or a client who prefers Brandy's basic-facial experience; it's the everyday-glow entry. **When Glo2Facial wins:** the marquee event glow, the oxygenation/infusion experience, sensitive skin that dislikes suction, and — critically — as the **branded gateway into Amber's advanced lane** and the finishing layer for needling. Lane note: hydrodermabrasion sits with **Brandy (basic facials)**; Glo2Facial is **Amber (advanced)**. Recommend by goal and provider, never disparage either.

**Glo2Facial vs. dermaplaning.**
Different jobs. Dermaplaning is manual exfoliation with a blade — it removes peach fuzz + dead surface cells for instant smoothness and flawless makeup application. Glo2Facial exfoliates *and* infuses *and* oxygenates for glow and hydration. **When dermaplaning wins:** removing vellus hair, prepping for flawless foundation, ultra-smooth texture. **When Glo2Facial wins:** hydration, brightness, oxygenated glow, and a full-service experience. They're **complementary** — dermaplaning is often done first (as a prep add-on, +$45) to help the Glo2Facial infusion absorb.

**Glo2Facial vs. a needling treatment (microchanneling / microneedling).**
These are not the same category. Needling creates controlled micro-channels to trigger the skin's collagen-repair response — it's the **corrective, collagen-induction** tool for texture, fine lines, pore appearance, acne-scar and stretch-mark appearance, and it comes with some downtime (redness, later flaking). Glo2Facial is **surface-level**: glow, hydration, brightness, no channels, no downtime. **When needling wins:** you want to change the skin's structure/texture over a series. **When Glo2Facial wins:** you want a glow now with zero recovery, you're not ready for needling, or you want the between-needling maintenance facial. They pair beautifully (see §7).

**Glo2Facial as an add-on/finish layered onto microchanneling.**
This is a signature House of Rose combination. After a microchanneling or microneedling session, adding the **Glo2Facial Finish (+$95)** layers the oxygenating, infusing, calming step on top of the needling — clients leave glowing and soothed rather than only flushed, and the infusion supports comfort. It's the "signature experience" upsell and one of the strongest reasons to book at House of Rose over a plain-microneedling competitor.

## 9. FAQ bank (answer-first, compliance-clean)

1. **What is a Glo2Facial?** It's a non-invasive, no-downtime signature facial by Geneo that exfoliates, infuses active ingredients, and oxygenates the skin from within in one session — designed to reveal an immediate, luminous glow. At House of Rose it's performed by Amber and starts at $185.
2. **How does the "oxygenation" actually work?** As a single-use pod reacts with its gel it releases a layer of CO₂ bubbles at the skin; that local rise in CO₂ prompts your own blood to release more oxygen into the treated skin (the Bohr effect). Nothing is injected — it's your skin oxygenating itself.
3. **Is there any downtime?** Typically none. There's no peeling or forced redness — most clients return to their day and can wear makeup, which is why it's a favorite before events.
4. **Is it good before a wedding or photoshoot?** Yes — it's our go-to event glow. Book it a day or two before so skin looks its brightest for the big day; final timing is confirmed with your provider.
5. **Does it hurt?** No — most clients feel a light effervescent tingle and warmth during the pass, then a relaxing facial massage. Comfort is a hallmark of the treatment.
6. **How is it different from a regular facial or hydrodermabrasion?** A basic hydrating facial cleanses and exfoliates; the Glo2Facial adds the CO₂/Bohr-effect oxygenation step and active-pod infusion for extra brightness and glow. Hydrodermabrasion (in our basic-facial lane with Brandy) is a great everyday option; Glo2Facial is the advanced glow and our gateway into corrective care.
7. **How often should I get one?** Wonderful as a one-off or monthly glow. For visible texture and tone change, many clients do a series of 3–6 sessions about 4–6 weeks apart, then maintenance — the provider tailors this to your skin.
8. **Will it help with fine lines?** It hydrates and plumps for the appearance of smoother, more supple skin. For structural change in fine lines and texture, we'd pair or graduate to microchanneling/microneedling; the provider maps the plan.
9. **Can it be added to microneedling or microchanneling?** Yes — the Glo2Facial Finish (+$95) layers the oxygenating, infusing, calming step onto a needling session, so you leave glowing and soothed. It's one of our signature combinations.
10. **Is it safe for sensitive skin?** It's designed to be gentle — no aggressive suction, no required acids — and is a comfortable first step for sensitive skin and first-timers. Candidacy is confirmed by the provider.
11. **Can I get it if I'm pregnant or breastfeeding?** The manufacturer notes Glo2Facial hasn't been formally tested on pregnant persons and recommends checking with your doctor. Bring it up at consultation and we'll confirm candidacy and pod selection with you.
12. **Does it work on all skin tones?** It's a gentle, non-ablative treatment used across a broad range of skin tones (the underlying clinical study included Fitzpatrick types I–VI). Your provider confirms suitability.
13. **What areas can be treated?** The face, and it can be extended onto the neck and décolleté (+$95). Ask about hands/chest at consultation.
14. **When will I see results?** The glow and hydration are immediate — best seen 1–3 days after. Texture, brightness, and tone build over a series of sessions; individual outcomes vary.
15. **Can I wear makeup afterward?** Yes — with no downtime you can typically apply makeup right away; your provider will confirm based on any add-ons you chose.
