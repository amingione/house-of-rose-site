## 1. The 3-sentence answer

BioRePeel is a TCA-based "lunchtime" resurfacing peel that refreshes tone, texture, and radiance with little to no visible peeling and essentially no downtime — you can walk out and back into your day. Its two-phase (biphasic) formula pairs 35% trichloroacetic acid with salicylic and other acids, GABA, amino acids, and vitamins to exfoliate the surface while feeding the skin underneath. At House of Rose in Punta Gorda, BioRePeel is **provider-split**: **Brandy** (basic facials & peels lane) offers the **standalone** face peel and standalone series; **Amber** (advanced lane) offers it **only as a +$65 add-on onto an advanced service** (microchanneling/microneedling) and performs the advanced acne-scarring, body, and gold spot-treatment variants — with final candidacy confirmed by the provider at consultation, because not every client is a candidate and individual outcomes vary.

## 2. How it works

**Plain English.** Most chemical peels work by injuring the top layers of skin so hard that the skin flakes and sheds for days — effective, but with visible downtime. BioRePeel is engineered to get resurfacing and glow **without** that heavy peeling. It's a two-layer ("biphasic") formula: an oily outer layer shields the skin surface and carries the actives, while a water-based inner layer delivers the working ingredients (TCA plus supporting acids, amino acids, and vitamins). The result is exfoliation and stimulation with minimal to light flaking rather than the sheeting of a deep TCA peel. Many clients notice brighter, smoother, more even-looking skin within a few days, and it improves with a short series.

**One level deeper.** The face formula (BioRePeelCl3 FND) is built around **35% trichloroacetic acid (TCA)**, combined with **salicylic acid** (a beta-hydroxy acid that is oil-soluble and works into pores — useful for congestion and the appearance of acne), plus **lactobionic, tartaric, and citric acids**, **GABA (aminobutyric acid)**, the amino acids **glycine, proline, hydroxyproline, and arginine**, and **vitamins C and B2**. The **lipophilic (oil-loving) phase** carries actives and, per the manufacturer, supports fibroblast activity and cellular turnover — the biostimulating/revitalising side. The **hydrophilic (water-loving) phase** has stabilising and protective actions, reduces trans-epidermal water loss, and helps restore the skin's hydro-lipidic barrier. So the peel is described by its maker as having three actions at once — **peeling, biostimulating, and revitalising**. Application time is short (on the order of a few minutes, up to ~10 minutes per session) and it is designed to be repeated in a series. The **body/spot formula (Gold)** runs a higher TCA concentration and is intended for thicker skin and stubborn localized spots; House of Rose uses it as a targeted upgrade layered onto the standard face peel (the "Gold Spot Upgrade").

## 3. Who it's for / not for

**Good candidates.** Clients wanting a low-commitment, no-real-downtime refresh — dull, uneven, or congested skin; early photoaging and fine lines; the appearance of mild-to-moderate acne and post-acne marks; rough texture; and anyone who wants a "glow before an event" without days of flaking. It suits busy schedules and is a strong entry into corrective skincare. In the **advanced acne-scarring** variant it pairs with microneedling; in the **body** variant it addresses the appearance of stretch marks, back/chest texture, and crepey skin per zone.

**Not for / caution (provider confirms at consultation).** Common contraindications for TCA peels include pregnancy and breastfeeding (avoided due to limited safety data on the actives), recent isotretinoin (Accutane) use, aspirin/salicylate allergy (salicylic acid is present), active skin infection, open wounds, cold sores in an active flare, recent sunburn, and a history of keloid or abnormal scarring. Active eczema, psoriasis, or rosacea may be aggravated. Recent deep chemical peels, laser resurfacing, or other resurfacing in the treatment area, and certain topical actives (e.g., retinoids) may require a waiting period. Very deep, established "ice-pick" scars respond less to peeling alone and are better matched to a needling-led plan.

**Honest limits.** BioRePeel is a resurfacing and skin-quality tool, not a scar-removal or acne-cure procedure. It addresses **the appearance of** texture, tone, congestion, and post-acne marks — it does not treat or cure acne, and it does not erase scars. Deeper structural correction is a microneedling/microchanneling job (often a series), sometimes combined with the peel. Best results come from a short series plus sun protection; a single visit is a refresh, not a full correction.

## 4. The visit

**Protocol (standard face peel, ~45 min).**
1. Cleanse and degrease the skin; provider confirms candidacy and reviews recent product use and sun exposure.
2. BioRePeel is applied and gently worked over the treatment area for a short contact time (typically a few minutes, up to ~10).
3. The product is removed/neutralized per protocol; a soothing/hydrating step and SPF finish the visit.
4. In the **advanced acne-scarring** variant (~75 min), microneedling precedes/accompanies the peel so the actives reach through freshly opened channels — a certified induced-peel style protocol Amber performs for stubborn textural scarring. In the **Gold Spot Upgrade** (~60 min), the standard face peel is done and the higher-strength gold formula is layered onto stubborn spots. **Body** (~75 min, per zone) treats back, chest, hands, or stretch-mark areas.

**Sensation.** A tingling, warm, or mild stinging feeling during application is normal and usually brief; there is no needle pain in the standalone peel (the acne-scarring variant adds the sensation of microneedling, typically with numbing).

**Downtime.** This is the headline: little to none. Skin may look slightly pink/flushed for a few hours and can feel tight or mildly dry, with **light** flaking for some clients over the following days — not the heavy sheeting of a deep peel. Most people return to normal activities immediately. (No guaranteed downtime — reactions are individual.)

**Aftercare (typical norms — provider gives the final plan).** Daily broad-spectrum SPF 30+ and strict sun avoidance for several days; skip makeup for ~24 hours; avoid heat, heavy sweat, sauna, and strenuous exercise for ~72 hours; use gentle, non-active skincare and pause AHAs/BHAs/glycolics/retinoids for roughly 1–2 weeks; do not pick or force any flaking; keep skin hydrated.

**Results timeline.** Many clients notice brighter, smoother, more even-looking skin within a few days to two weeks, with collagen-driven improvement continuing to build over the following weeks (commonly described as peaking around 4–6 weeks). Results are individual.

**Cadence.** BioRePeel is designed as a **series**. Common professional cadence is a course of sessions spaced roughly 1–2 weeks apart for a resurfacing goal, or the House of Rose corrective series (see §8) spaced further apart when combined with needling; the provider sets intervals. Maintenance thereafter is individualized.

## 5. Comparisons (honest)

**BioRePeel vs light/superficial peels (Brandy's basic-facial lane — enzyme exfoliation, light glycolic/lactic peels).** Brandy's light peels and enzyme treatments are gentle, relaxing, maintenance-grade exfoliation — the right call for sensitive skin, a first-timer easing in, routine glow, or someone who wants a pampering facial with no active-acid intensity. BioRePeel is a **stronger, TCA-based corrective resurfacing** peel in Amber's advanced lane: it goes after texture, congestion, early photoaging, and the appearance of post-acne marks with more punch, while still keeping downtime minimal. **Light peel wins** when the goal is maintenance, calm, or the client isn't ready for TCA. **BioRePeel wins** when the client wants visible resurfacing/correction but can't accept the downtime of a traditional medium-depth peel.

**BioRePeel vs corrective microneedling/microchanneling.** Different jobs. Microneedling/microchanneling create controlled micro-channels to remodel from within — the tool of choice for **structural** texture and the appearance of deeper/rolling acne scars and stretch marks (see the `microneedling-corrective` and `microchanneling` services). BioRePeel resurfaces from the **surface down** — tone, glow, congestion, superficial texture. **Microneedling wins** for defined scar texture and deeper correction. **BioRePeel wins** for radiance, surface texture, and no-downtime refresh. They combine: the **advanced acne-scarring protocol** deliberately pairs them so the peel's actives penetrate through needling channels — often the strongest single answer for stubborn scarring.

**BioRePeel vs dermaplaning.** Dermaplaning physically removes vellus hair ("peach fuzz") and dead surface cells with a blade — instant smoothness and makeup glide, no acids, no downtime. It does not resurface at depth or address pigment/congestion. **Dermaplaning wins** for hair removal, event-ready smoothness, and product penetration prep. **BioRePeel wins** for real resurfacing, tone, and texture. Dermaplaning is frequently used as **prep** before a peel (available as a "+$45 Dermaplane Prep" add-on) — not a substitute.

**BioRePeel as a single visit vs a series.** A single BioRePeel is a genuine refresh — good before an event or to try the treatment. But most concerns (texture, tone, the appearance of acne marks, early photoaging) show their real change across a **series**, which is how the treatment is designed and how House of Rose prices it. Recommend the series for correction; the single visit for maintenance or a first try.

## 9. FAQ bank (answer-first, compliance-clean)

1. **What is BioRePeel?** A TCA-based resurfacing peel designed to refresh tone, texture, and radiance with little to no visible peeling and essentially no downtime. At House of Rose, **Brandy** offers the standalone BioRePeel facial; **Amber** offers it as a +$65 add-on to an advanced treatment and performs the advanced acne-scarring, body, and gold spot variants.
2. **Will I actually peel?** Usually only lightly, if at all — that's the point of BioRePeel. Some clients get mild flaking over a few days; many get none. It does not cause the heavy sheeting of a deep peel. Individual reactions vary.
3. **Is there downtime?** Generally none. Skin may look slightly pink for a few hours and feel tight or mildly dry. Most clients return to normal activities right away. No downtime is ever guaranteed.
4. **Does it hurt?** The standalone peel causes a brief tingling, warmth, or mild stinging during application — not needle pain. The advanced acne-scarring version adds microneedling, typically performed with numbing.
5. **How many sessions will I need?** BioRePeel is designed as a series. Many clients do a short course of sessions; your provider sets the number and intervals based on your concern and skin. A single visit works as a refresh or a first try.
6. **How often can I get a BioRePeel?** Sessions are typically spaced roughly 1–2 weeks apart for a resurfacing course, or further apart when combined with needling. Your provider confirms the schedule for you.
7. **Can BioRePeel help with acne or acne scars?** It's designed to support the appearance of congested, blemish-prone, and post-acne skin, and the advanced protocol pairs it with microneedling for the appearance of stubborn textural scarring. It does not treat or cure acne — for active breakouts, the Face Reality Acne Bootcamp is the dedicated path.
8. **Is it safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?** It's generally avoided during pregnancy and breastfeeding because there isn't enough safety data on the active ingredients. Your provider will confirm timing at consultation.
9. **Can I get it if I have sensitive skin, rosacea, or eczema?** Sometimes — but active rosacea, eczema, or psoriasis can be aggravated, so this is a provider decision. A gentler light peel or enzyme treatment may be the better first step. Not every client is a candidate.
10. **What's the difference between the Blue face peel and the Gold spot upgrade?** The standard face peel uses the face formula; the Gold Spot Upgrade layers a higher-strength gold formula onto stubborn localized spots after the standard peel. Your provider decides whether the gold upgrade is appropriate for you.
11. **How is BioRePeel different from a regular facial peel?** Brandy's light/enzyme peels are gentle maintenance exfoliation; BioRePeel is a stronger, TCA-based corrective resurfacing peel in Amber's advanced lane — more visible resurfacing, still minimal downtime.
12. **BioRePeel or microneedling — which do I need?** Microneedling remodels structural texture and the appearance of deeper scars; BioRePeel resurfaces tone, glow, and surface texture with no downtime. For stubborn scarring, the advanced protocol combines both. The provider matches the tool to your concern at consultation.
13. **What should I do after a BioRePeel?** Wear daily SPF 30+ and avoid sun; skip makeup for about a day; avoid heat, heavy sweat, and sauna for ~72 hours; pause strong actives (AHAs/BHAs/retinoids) for 1–2 weeks; don't pick any flaking; keep skin hydrated. Your provider gives the exact plan.
14. **Can I do BioRePeel on my body?** Yes — the BioRePeel Body variant treats areas like the back, chest, hands, and stretch-mark zones, priced per zone. Body skin is thicker, so a longer series is realistic.
15. **When will I see results?** Many clients notice brighter, smoother, more even-looking skin within a few days to two weeks, with improvement continuing to build over the following weeks. Outcomes are individual and depend on your concern and skin.
