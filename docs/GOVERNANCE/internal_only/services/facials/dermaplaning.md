## What the service is

Dermaplaning is surface exfoliation with a specialized blade. It removes accumulated dead skin cells
and fine vellus hair from the face during the same appointment. It is not a collagen-induction
service, acne treatment, or substitute for removing coarser facial hair.

Cutting vellus hair at the skin surface does not change the follicle, hair thickness, color, or growth
rate. Early regrowth can feel blunt because the tapered tip was cut.

## Questions worth answering

**Will peach fuzz grow back thicker or darker?**

No. Dermaplaning cuts hair at the skin surface and does not change the follicle. The hair's thickness,
color, and growth rate remain unchanged.

**How is Dermaplaning different from Facial Waxing?**

Dermaplaning removes fine vellus hair and surface buildup across the face with a specialized blade.
Facial Waxing uses wax and is priced by area for the brows, upper lip, and chin.
