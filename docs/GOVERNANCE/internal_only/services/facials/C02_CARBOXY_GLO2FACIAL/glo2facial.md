## What the service is

Glo2Facial is a Geneo facial with three parts:

1. A single-use OxyPod and paired Primer Gel move across the skin for surface exfoliation.
2. Their reaction creates carbon-dioxide bubbles at the skin's surface. Geneo describes that
   carbon-dioxide-rich environment as triggering the oxygenation step; oxygen is not blown onto the
   face from an external source.
3. The appointment continues with topical infusion and finishes with facial massage.

Keep this explanation at the service level. Do not turn the small cited study into a promised House of
Rose outcome or import a radiofrequency step that is not part of the current appointment description.

## Client decisions this page should answer

- **Glo2Facial or Dermaplaning:** both include surface exfoliation, but the tools and remaining steps
  differ. Dermaplaning uses a specialized blade to remove fine vellus hair and accumulated dead skin
  cells. Glo2Facial uses the OxyPod and Primer Gel, then continues with topical infusion and massage.
- **Glo2Facial or Microneedling:** these are separate services. Glo2Facial works at the surface;
  Microneedling creates controlled microchannels. Do not present either as the automatic next step.
- **Recovery:** House of Rose confirmed on 2026-08-06 that this exact procedure has no downtime. This
  is a procedure-specific owner-reviewed fact, not a reusable claim for other services.

## Questions worth answering

**What is included in the 60-minute appointment?**

Surface exfoliation with the OxyPod and Primer Gel, topical infusion, and facial massage.

**Is oxygen blown onto the skin?**

No. Geneo describes the OxyPod and Primer Gel reaction as creating a carbon-dioxide-rich environment
on the skin's surface that triggers the oxygenation step.

**How is it different from Dermaplaning?**

Dermaplaning uses a specialized blade across the facial surface and removes fine vellus hair.
Glo2Facial uses the OxyPod and Primer Gel and includes topical infusion and facial massage.
