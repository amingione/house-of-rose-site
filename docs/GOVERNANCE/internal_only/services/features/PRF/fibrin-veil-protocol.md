# TREATMENT PURPOSE

The Rose PRF Fibrin Veil is a two-phase autologous PRF treatment combining:

1. Provider-selected microneedling with the Procell Therapies device
2. Fresh liquid PRF applied topically during the section-by-section treatment process
3. A naturally polymerized PRF fibrin gel applied as a finishing veil or mask

The final fibrin veil is prepared from the client’s own PRF without skincare, graft material, bone material or other additives.

Do not refer to the facial product as “sticky bone.” Sticky bone is an oral-surgery preparation that includes bone-graft material. The appropriate terminology for this service is:

- PRF fibrin veil
- Autologous fibrin gel mask
- PRF finishing veil
- Naturally polymerized PRF mask

# PRF COLLECTION AND PREPARATION

Prepare the PRF according to the exact collection tubes, centrifuge and protocol used during certification.

Do not substitute centrifuge times, speeds or settings from another PRF system.

## CREATE TWO FUNCTIONAL PRF PORTIONS

### PORTION 1: WORKING LIQUID PRF

Used topically during the section-by-section needling process.

The working PRF should remain sufficiently liquid to:

- Spread in a thin layer
- Function as the approved treatment slick when permitted
- Be distributed immediately behind a completed needling section
- Avoid thick fibrin strands during active needling

### PORTION 2: FIBRIN VEIL PRF

Reserved for the final gel mask.

Place this portion in a sterile, closed, nonabsorbent container approved by the medical protocol and allow it to polymerize naturally.

Do not place the mask portion directly onto absorbent gauze because gauze can wick away the fluid component.

Do not add:

- Calcium chloride
- Thrombin
- Bone-graft material
- Hyaluronic acid
- Skincare products
- Serums
- Exosomes
- Growth-factor products
- Topical medications
- Gelling agents

Do not save, refrigerate or send unused PRF home with the client.

---

# FIBRIN VEIL CONSISTENCY SCALE

Use the following internal scale to standardize the final mask:

| Score | Appearance | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thin, watery and freely running | Working liquid PRF |
| 2 | Slightly syrupy but still migrating | Continue monitoring |
| 3 | Honey-like, spreadable and forming soft strands | Ideal fibrin veil |
| 4 | Soft cohesive clot that lifts as one piece | Too polymerized for a smooth facial mask |
| 5 | Dense or rubbery fibrin membrane | Do not use as the facial veil |

The desired facial-mask endpoint is Score 3.

The consistency shown in a traditional sticky-bone preparation is generally more polymerized than the ideal full-face veil. The facial veil should be applied immediately before it becomes a dense, cohesive clot.

Record the time from centrifuge completion to Score 3 during every pilot treatment.

# MICRONEEDLING PATHWAY

Use this pathway only when the selected device and medical protocol permit the intended topical PRF sequence.

## WHEN PRF IS PERMITTED AS THE TREATMENT SLICK

Work in controlled facial sections.

For each section:

1. Apply a thin, even layer of fresh liquid PRF.
2. Perform the provider-selected microneedling passes.
3. Reapply a thin layer of PRF as needed to maintain appropriate movement.
4. Avoid excessive pooling or dripping.
5. Complete the selected endpoint for that section.
6. Move to the next section.
7. Repeat until the treatment area is complete.

## WHEN THE DEVICE REQUIRES ITS OWN APPROVED GLIDE

For each section:

1. Apply the device-approved glide.
2. Complete the selected microneedling pass or passes.
3. Apply a thin layer of fresh liquid PRF immediately behind the completed section.
4. Move to the next section.
5. Repeat until the treatment area is complete.

Do not replace a required manufacturer glide unless the device manufacturer and medical protocol permit the substitution.

---

# MICROCHANNELING PATHWAY

Use this pathway only when the selected microchanneling manufacturer has approved the topical PRF combination in writing and the House of Rose medical director has approved the protocol.

Use the following section-by-section sequence:

1. Stamp the first treatment section.
2. Apply a thin layer of fresh liquid PRF to the stamped section.
3. Stamp the adjacent new section.
4. Apply fresh PRF to the new section.
5. Backroll across the previously stamped section and the newly stamped section, distributing the PRF evenly.
6. Continue stamping, applying PRF and backrolling in controlled overlapping sections.
7. Avoid excessive pressure, repeated unnecessary passes or visible pooling.
8. Continue until the full treatment area is complete.

The working pattern is:

**Stamp → Apply PRF → Stamp New Section → Apply PRF → Backroll Previous and New Sections → Continue**

Do not use this PRF sequence with a device whose required protocol prohibits immediate application of outside products.

# PRF FIBRIN VEIL APPLICATION

After completing all needling passes:

1. Inspect the treated skin for an appropriate and uniform endpoint.
2. Apply a final thin coat of remaining liquid PRF.
3. Confirm the reserved PRF portion has reached Score 3.
4. Apply the fibrin gel using a sterile applicator.
5. Spread it in a thin, even and continuous layer.
6. Avoid the eyelids, inside the orbital rim, nostrils, lips and hair-bearing areas.
7. Do not needle, stamp or backroll through the partially polymerized gel.
8. Do not cover the PRF with gauze, a sheet mask or a conventional skincare mask.
9. Leave the fibrin veil in place for approximately 10–15 minutes during the initial protocol-development phase.
10. Observe the client continuously for excessive swelling, burning, discomfort or unexpected skin response.
11. Gently remove excess gel using sterile nonwoven gauze and sterile saline.
12. Do not scrub or aggressively wipe the treated skin.
13. Leave only a very light residual film when permitted by the selected device’s post-treatment protocol.
14. Discard all remaining PRF as biohazardous waste.

Do not allow the client to leave wearing a thick, untreated layer of blood product.

# POST-TREATMENT INSTRUCTIONS

Advise the client to:

## FIRST 24 HOURS

- Do not apply makeup unless the device-specific instructions permit it.
- Do not touch the treated area unnecessarily.
- Avoid vigorous exercise and heavy sweating.
- Avoid saunas, steam rooms, hot yoga and very hot showers.
- Avoid swimming pools, hot tubs, lakes and ocean water.
- Avoid alcohol-based or fragranced products.
- Sleep on a clean pillowcase.
- Keep pets away from the treated face.
- Do not pick, rub or exfoliate the skin.

## DAYS 2–7

- Use only a gentle cleanser and approved moisturizer.
- Avoid retinoids, exfoliating acids, benzoyl peroxide and scrubs.
- Avoid facial devices, waxing and additional professional treatments.
- Avoid direct sun exposure.
- Use broad-spectrum sunscreen once permitted by the post-treatment instructions.
- Allow dryness, flaking or mild crusting to resolve naturally.

## CONTACT THE MEDICAL PROVIDER FOR

- Increasing pain
- Spreading or worsening redness
- Increasing warmth
- Pus or unusual drainage
- Fever
- Blistering
- Significant swelling
- Persistent bleeding
- Cold-sore symptoms
- Delayed healing
- Any unexpected or concerning reaction
