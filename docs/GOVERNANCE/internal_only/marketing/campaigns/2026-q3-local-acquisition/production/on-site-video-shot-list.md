# On-Site Video Capture and Short-Form Scripts

The supplied still photographs are sufficient for launch statics and motion-still cutdowns. This capture session replaces the motion-still versions with unmistakably human, local footage once staff are available.

## Session format

- **Duration:** 60–90 minutes.
- **Camera:** current phone rear camera, 4K/30 fps, standard lens, no beauty filter, no portrait-video blur.
- **Orientation:** capture every speaking segment vertically; capture B-roll vertically and horizontally.
- **Lighting:** use the studio’s practical lights plus window light. Avoid ring-light catchlights and over-smoothed skin.
- **Audio:** quiet room, phone 3–5 feet from the speaker, or a simple lavalier microphone.
- **Performance:** conversational delivery, one thought per take, no memorized sales cadence.
- **Consent:** obtain a signed release from every identifiable non-staff participant. Do not film treatment results without explicit before/after consent.

## B-roll checklist

1. Exterior establishing shot from the parking area.
2. Slow approach to the blue-and-gold entrance.
3. Hand opening the studio door.
4. Welcome console and House of Rose sign.
5. Hallway walk-through at normal speed.
6. Seating area with no client visible.
7. Skin-analysis device and screen with no identifiable client data.
8. Provider preparing a clean treatment tray.
9. Glo2Facial or Procell equipment and products, labels accurate and unobstructed.
10. Diana entering or preparing the injectables room; no needle close-up.
11. Amber Mingione, Licensed Esthetician, arranging current skin-treatment products.
12. Exterior closing shot with the sign centered.

## Delivery checklist

- Export 1080×1920 and 1080×1350 H.264 MP4 versions.
- Burn in accurate captions for all spoken words.
- Keep essential text inside the central safe zone so platform controls do not cover it.
- Use licensed platform-native music quietly under speech, or publish without music.
- Do not use fake testimonials, synthetic voice, generated people, skin-smoothing filters, or misleading result transitions.
