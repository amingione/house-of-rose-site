01 / What It Is
The Science, Simplified

What PRF Actually Is

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is prepared from a small sample of your own blood, spun down to concentrate your platelets, growth factors, and fibrin — the same proteins your body already uses to repair itself.

At House of Rose, topical PRF is prepared chairside and applied to the skin’s surface as part of an eligible microneedling treatment. It is not injected. Because it comes from your own blood, it is added to your plan the same day it is drawn.

Why We Layer It In

PRF was never meant to stand alone.

Applied on top of the treatments already doing the work — resurfacing, exfoliation, light-based therapy — PRF is designed to support your skin’s own collagen remodeling process, so the investment you’re already making has more raw material to build with. Think of it less as a stand-alone appointment and more as an amplifier you bring to the treatments already on your plan.

Ask Your Provider

Eligibility, timing, and which treatments PRF can be layered into are determined by your provider during consultation. Individual results vary.

02 / Pairing · Tone & Pigment
PRF + Lumecca IPL

Light Therapy, Layered With Regeneration

Lumecca is a broad-spectrum IPL (intense pulsed light) treatment available at House of Rose, used to address the visible appearance of uneven tone, sun damage, and redness-prone skin.

Because light-based treatments like Lumecca work by prompting a visible response in the skin, providers can choose to layer a topical PRF step into the same plan — designed to support the skin’s own renewal process while it’s already primed to respond. It’s a pairing built around timing: PRF meets your skin exactly when it’s doing the most work.

The Idea

Correct tone and pigment with Lumecca. Layer in topical PRF to support what comes next. Two treatments, one plan, working toward the same goal.

Ask Your Provider

Lumecca and PRF are two separate treatments performed as part of a personalized plan. Sequencing, spacing, and eligibility are determined by your provider. Individual results vary.

03 / Pairing · Texture & Scarring
PRF + Morpheus8

RF Microneedling, Layered With Regeneration

Morpheus8 is RF (radiofrequency) microneedling available at House of Rose, designed to address the visible appearance of texture, acne scarring, and stretch marks.

Because Morpheus8 creates a controlled network of micro-channels across the treatment area, providers can choose to layer a topical PRF step into the same visit — designed to support the skin’s own remodeling process while it’s already primed to respond. Ask your provider whether adding PRF to your Morpheus8 plan is right for you.

Address texture, scarring, and stretch marks with Morpheus8. Layer in topical PRF to support what your skin does next.
