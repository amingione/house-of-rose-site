## What the evidence can support

PRF is prepared without an anticoagulant and forms a fibrin matrix containing platelets and growth
factors. Research supports a biological rationale for gradual growth-factor release. The facial-
aesthetics evidence for combining PRF with needling remains limited and varies by preparation,
application, device, depth, treatment area, and study design.

Use mechanism studies for literacy, not as proof of a House of Rose result. Do not transfer findings
from injected PRF, another device, another topical material, or a different protocol to this service.
