## Direct answer

BioRePeel is a topical TCA-based chemical peel used at House of Rose for visible surface texture and
uneven tone. TCA means trichloroacetic acid in the BioRePeelCl3 face formulation; the product is applied
to the skin rather than injected. House of Rose currently lists five BioRePeel appointments with
different prices, appointment lengths, booking paths, and responsible providers.

## BioRePeel and Microneedling are different services

The standalone BioRePeel appointment is a topical chemical peel. Microneedling uses the Procell device
to create controlled microchannels. A provider decides whether either service is appropriate and
whether an eligible combination fits an individual plan; public copy must not prescribe a sequence or
claim that the peel is delivered through microneedling channels.

Use `/services/microneedling/` when a visitor needs the current Procell menu and the distinction between
the two services.
