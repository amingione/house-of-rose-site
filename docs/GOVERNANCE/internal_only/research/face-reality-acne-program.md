## Provider and medical boundary

Where Amber is named in this context, use **Amber Mingione, Licensed Esthetician**. Add **Face Reality
Certified Acne Specialist** when it explains her role in the program.

The current approved boundary is direct: this is a non-prescription esthetics program for the
appearance of recurring breakouts. Deep, painful, widespread, or actively scarring breakouts need
medical evaluation before an esthetics program is considered. Do not name or imply a medical
specialty, diagnose a condition, prescribe medication, or claim that the program replaces medical
care.

Place safeguards where the content creates the need:

- When copy describes program candidacy or the medical threshold, defer that decision to the
  appropriate licensed professional without turning the page into a clinical checklist.
- When copy makes an appearance or outcome claim, use only a supported claim and include concise
  individual-variation language.
- Before-and-after evidence may publish only with documented consent.
- Stating a current ordinary price does not by itself create a free, discounted, or credited offer.
  Never invent a statutory disclosure or an offer from the program price.

Do not publish a guaranteed result, success percentage, promised timeframe, disease-treatment claim,
or vendor marketing claim as House of Rose evidence. Do not direct clients from Acne Bootcamp into a
different service or product purchase as an automatic next step.
