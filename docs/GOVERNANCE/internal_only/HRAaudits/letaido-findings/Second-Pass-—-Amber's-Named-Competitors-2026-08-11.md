## Revised recommendation

The first report's blog roadmap is still valid — but it is now clearly **step 3, not step 1**. Corrected sequence:

**Step 1 — Google Business Profile (highest ROI by far).** 447,400 monthly "near me" searches resolve through the local pack. Categories, services, photos, hours, and review velocity. No blog post competes with this.

**Step 2 — One canonical page per verified current service.** Keep the treatment entity clear in the H1 and title; use the `localArea` page type for geographic search intent. Search volume does not authorize a page for a service House of Rose does not offer or the substitution of one branded system for another.

**Step 3 — The reviewed PRF cluster.** Keep `/services/prf/`, `/services/prf-injections/`, `/services/microneedling/`, and `/services/prf-under-eyes/` distinct, substantive, and internally linked. Do not revive unsupported PRF routes merely because a related keyword has volume.

**Step 4 — Reviewed journal coverage** from [[seo-content/content-gap-analysis]], limited to topics that have current service support and primary-source evidence.

## Method notes

* All six named competitors resolved to domains via web search, then verified individually in Ahrefs (`mode="subdomains"`)
* Geo-grid and "near me" volumes pulled via `keywords_explorer.keywords_overview`; 32 of 93 seeded terms returned **no data at all** (below Ahrefs' volume floor) — itself the finding
* `attrs.local` confirms local-pack intent on every "near me" term
* Punta Gorda zero-volumes are real zeros, not scoping artifacts — cross-checked against non-zero controls (`massage punta gorda` 100, `botox punta gorda` 20) in the same call
