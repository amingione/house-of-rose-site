# Product Packaging Design — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 08/01 · 2026-06-21 · retail line (skincare, candles, gift cards). Built from the visual system.*

## Structure & panels
- **Front:** monogram + product name (Cormorant) + descriptor (Montserrat) on ivory or burgundy;
  gold accents. Calm, gift-worthy.
- **Back:** on-voice description, usage ("designed to"), key ingredients, net weight, NAP/web.
- **Sides:** monogram repeat pattern (low opacity) or botanical line.

## Materials & finishes
Matte ivory or deep-burgundy cartons/jars; gold foil monogram; soft-touch; recyclable where possible.
Never glossy-clinical, never stark white.

## Copy
Warm, specific, compliant (no medical claims on cosmetics); "From $X" on shelf talkers, not the pack.

## Specs
Per-SKU dieline; CMYK + foil; legible ≥6pt regulatory text; print-ready.
