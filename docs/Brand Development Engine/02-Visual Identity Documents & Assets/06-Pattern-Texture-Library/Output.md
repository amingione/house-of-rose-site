# Pattern & Texture Library — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 02-Visual/06 · 2026-06-21 · Aligned to Phase 01 + color/logo.*

Patterns are **quiet luxury** — used as accents, never wallpaper. They make collateral feel
considered (the fashion-house monogram-paper effect) without ever going clinical or busy.

## The patterns
1. **Monogram repeat.** Tonal HR monogram tiled at low contrast (e.g., gold-on-burgundy at 8–12%
   opacity, or ink-on-ivory) — inside covers, gift-card backs, packaging interiors, email footers.
2. **Botanical line toss.** Sparse rose + baby's-breath + sage line sprigs, gold or ink, scattered
   with lots of space — section breaks, card corners, thank-you notes.
3. **Fine gold rule system.** Hairline gold rules + a small ✦/✧ or rose tick as separators (already
   used on the waxing menu) — menus, headers, dividers.
4. **Greige paper texture.** Subtle warm-paper grain on ivory/greige grounds for tactility (print +
   digital backgrounds), very low intensity.

## Usage rules
- One pattern per surface, low opacity, lots of negative space. Patterns **support** type and the
  mark — never compete.
- Monogram repeat only on "interior/luxury" moments (inside, backs, linings) — not behind primary copy.
- Botanicals stay sparse; if it reads "busy," reduce by half.

## Don'ts
No dense florals, no drop-shadows, no multi-color patterns, no pattern behind body text, no pattern
on the gold gradient logo.
