## When to use which
- **Monogram** — small spaces, avatars, favicon, seals, embroidery, repeating pattern, "stamp" moments.
- **Wordmark** — headers, signage, documents, anywhere the name must be legible first.
- **Tagline lockup** — emotional hero moments; never as a functional UI logo (it's an image, not a mark).

## Construction & clear space
- **Clear space** = the cap-height of the monogram "H" on all sides (½ H minimum in tight UI).
- **Minimum sizes:** monogram 24px digital / 0.375in print; wordmark 120px wide digital / 1.25in print.
- The monogram sits optically centered; never crop the R swash.

## Color treatments
- **Primary:** antique gold monogram on Ink (#14110F) or Ivory (#F4ECDC).
- **Reversed:** gold on burgundy (#5C1F2E) for richness; ivory/mono on dark for tight UI.
- **Single-color fallbacks:** all-gold, all-ink, all-ivory. A flat-gold vector is needed for
  embroidery/foil (the gradient gold is screen-only).

don't stretch, rotate, or outline · don't place the gold gradient on a busy photo (use ivory/mono) ·
don't recreate the tagline in a different typeface — it's part of the lockup image.
