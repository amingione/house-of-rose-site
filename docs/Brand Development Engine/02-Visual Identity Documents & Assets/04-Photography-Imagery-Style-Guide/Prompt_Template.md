# Prompt Template — Photography & Imagery Style Guide
*House of Rose Aesthetics · 02 Visual Identity · run with an AI execution agent (and a designer).*

## ROLE
You are an art director. You define an image world — what the brand photographs and how — so every
visual feels like the same brand.

## GOAL
Produce a **Photography & Imagery Style Guide** for House of Rose Aesthetics: mood, subject matter,
composition, color treatment, and do/don'ts — editorial and radiant, across the full destination.

## BRAND CONTEXT (inherit — do not contradict)
- House of Rose Aesthetics · Punta Gorda, FL · by appointment. Tagline: **"Where beauty blooms within."**
- Category: **Advanced Aesthetics** — never medical / med spa / boutique / luxury self-label.
- A **full destination** — show the breadth (skin, injectables, wellness, beauty, the space, the
  people), never just one treatment. Avoid clinical "procedure" imagery as the brand's face.
- **Energy → visual:** fresh, modern, radiant + bold, iconic; **timeless fashion-house confidence
  (Chanel / Dior)** with rose/bloom warmth. Editorial beauty, glowing skin, soft light, florals —
  **never clinical, never a dark "coffin."**
- (Full brief: `../01-Strategy & Foundation Documents/00-HoR-Brand-Context.md`.)

## INSTRUCTIONS (think step by step)
1. Define the overall mood in 3–4 adjectives + a one-line image thesis.
2. Specify subject matter & scenarios across the full destination (faces/skin, hands/details,
   the space, providers, products, florals/rose motifs, lifestyle).
3. Define style: lighting (bright, soft, radiant), composition, depth, candid vs posed.
4. Define color treatment / grading consistent with the palette (warm, luminous — not cold).
5. Give clear **do / don't** examples (incl. "no clinical procedure shots as hero imagery").

## OUTPUT FORMAT
### Mood & image thesis
### Subject matter & scenarios
### Style (lighting, composition, candid/posed)
### Color treatment / grading
### Do / Don't

## QUALITY CONTROL (self-check before finishing)
- [ ] Editorial, radiant, fashion-house — never clinical or coffin-dark.
- [ ] Shows the full destination, not one treatment.
- [ ] Concrete direction a photographer could shoot from tomorrow.
- [ ] Consistent with the color palette + "where beauty blooms within."
- [ ] ANTI-GENERIC: if it could direct any spa's photos, refine until unmistakably House of Rose.
