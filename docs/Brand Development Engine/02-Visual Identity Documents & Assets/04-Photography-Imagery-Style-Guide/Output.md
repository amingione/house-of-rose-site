# Photography & Imagery Style Guide — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 02-Visual/04 · 2026-06-21 · extends the tagline lockup's mood. Aligned to Phase 01.*

## The mood (from the brand image)
Soft natural light, warm greige and ivory grounds, the deep burgundy rose, ivory baby's breath,
antique gold. Editorial and romantic — like a fashion-house campaign, not a clinic brochure. **Never
clinical, never a coffin:** no harsh fluorescent white, no cold blue, no flat black voids, no
gloved-hands-and-syringes hero shots.

## What we photograph (full destination, not one treatment)
- **The experience & space** — the suite, textures, florals, the welcome, hands offering tea.
- **Real results, honestly** — natural, individual; consented before/after with even, soft lighting.
- **People** — calm, confident clients of varied ages; providers as warm experts (not posed
  clinical stock).
- **Details** — gold monogram moments, product still lifes, botanicals, the rose recurring as motif.
- **All lanes** — skin/facials, regeneration, injectables (tasteful, never graphic), wellness/IV
  lounge, beauty (waxing/lashes), retail.

## Lighting & treatment
- Soft, directional **natural light**; warm white balance (~4800–5400K feel).
- Gentle shadows, low-to-mid contrast; airy, not blown out.
- **Color grade:** warm, slightly desaturated; lift toward ivory/greige; protect burgundy + gold
  richness. No cold teal, no clinical over-bright.
- Composition: generous negative space (type and the gold mark need room); rule-of-thirds; editorial
  framing.

## Do / Don't
**Do:** natural skin texture, candid calm, florals, warm neutrals, real people, tasteful restraint.
**Don't:** stocky "spa with cucumber slices," syringe close-ups as heroes, cold operatory rooms,
heavy retouching that erases identity, neon/gradient filters, busy backgrounds behind the gold mark.

## Illustration / graphic imagery
Sparing line-botanicals (rose, baby's breath, sage stem) in gold or ink; never cartoonish. The rose
is the recurring emblem.
