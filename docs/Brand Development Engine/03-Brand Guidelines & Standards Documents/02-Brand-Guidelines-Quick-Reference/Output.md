## Who we are
**House of Rose Aesthetics** · Punta Gorda, FL · **Advanced Aesthetics** (the only category — never
medical / med spa / boutique / luxury self-label). A **full destination**, not one treatment.
Tagline: **"Where beauty blooms within."**

## Logo
Antique-gold **HR monogram** (small spaces/seals) · **wordmark** (name must read) · **tagline
lockup** (emotional heroes). Clear space = cap-height of "H". Gold on ink/ivory/burgundy. Never
stretch, recolor, or add effects.

## Colors
Burgundy `#5C1F2E` · Gold `#C9A24B` · Ivory `#F4ECDC` · Ink `#14110F` · Greige `#C7BBA9`.
**60/30/10:** light ground / depth / gold+botanical. Gold = precious accent, never big fills.

## Type
Headings **Cormorant Garamond** · eyebrows/buttons **Montserrat** (tracked caps) · body **EB
Garamond**. Sentence case. Large, airy.

## Voice
Confident not loud · warm + expert · specific over superlative. Pricing always **"From $X"**. No
guarantees; defer clinical specifics to licensed providers.

## Top do's & don'ts
✅ Represent the whole house · warm editorial imagery · whitespace · honest "From $X."
🚫 Don't self-label luxury/medical · don't tunnel on PRF/microchanneling · never clinical/coffin
tone · no hype/urgency/discount energy · no flat numbers.
