  always look like yourself.

## 2. Strategy foundation *(full text: Phase 01)*
- **Mission:** give SW Florida a place where beauty is built the right way — expertise, restraint,
  care that compounds.
- **Vision:** the region's most trusted name in advanced aesthetics — the full destination.
- **Values:** Results Built Right · Expertise Worn Lightly · Honesty as the Standard · Quiet
  Refinement · The Client, Cared For.
- **Positioning (keystone):** *The full Advanced Aesthetics destination of Southwest Florida — where
  results are built the right way, and you always look like yourself.* Wedge: full house · method
  (Rose Method™) · honesty · intimacy. Regeneration = signature depth, not identity.
- **Personas:** Refined Regular (Diane) · Investment-Minded Professional (Marisol) · Milestone
  Glow-Seeker (Hannah) · Quiet Maintainer (Mark).
- **Archetype:** Sovereign (lead) + Caregiver (warmth) + Sage (educates).
- **Promise:** honest care, natural results, always you.

## 3. Verbal identity *(full text: 01-09 Voice)*
- **Voice:** confident not loud · warm + expert · specific over superlative · sensory but disciplined.
- **Mechanics:** sentence case; "From $X" pricing always; sparing 🌹; "you," not "the patient."
- **Compliance:** no guarantees ("designed to/supports/many clients see"); defer specifics to licensed
  providers; individual results.
- **Never:** hype/urgency, discount energy, clichés, overclaiming, single-lane tunnel.

## 4. Visual identity *(full specs: Phase 02)*
- **Logo:** gold HR monogram · wordmark · tagline lockup. Clear space = "H" cap-height.
- **Color:** Burgundy `#5C1F2E` · Gold `#C9A24B` · Ivory `#F4ECDC` · Ink `#14110F` · Greige `#C7BBA9`.
  60/30/10 light/depth/gold.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond (display) · Montserrat (eyebrow/UI, tracked caps) · EB Garamond (body).
- **Photography:** warm, editorial, natural light; all lanes; never clinical/coffin/syringe-hero.
- **System:** fine gold line icons · quiet monogram/botanical patterns · Astro+Tailwind component kit.

## 5. Applications
Website, memberships/plans pages, service menus, social, email, print collateral, retail packaging,
signage — each built from the component library and the 60/30/10 + eyebrow→heading pattern. Examples
in Visual Identity Standards (03-04).

## 6. Do's & Don'ts (master)
**Do:** represent the whole house · lead with warm light + whitespace · keep gold precious · "From
$X" · honest candidacy language · let clients call it luxury.
**Don't:** self-label luxury/medical/boutique · tunnel on PRF/microchanneling · go clinical/coffin ·
hype/urgency/discount · flat prices · stretch/recolor the logo · busy patterns behind copy.
