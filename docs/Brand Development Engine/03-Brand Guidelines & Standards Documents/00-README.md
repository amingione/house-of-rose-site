# 03 · Brand Guidelines & Standards — Prompt Template Library

The **governance layer** of the House of Rose AI Brand Factory. These templates **compile** the
locked outputs of Strategy (phase 01) and Visual Identity (phase 02) into enforceable standards —
the documents a team member, vendor, or new hire follows to stay on-brand.

> Run these **after** phases 01 and 02 are finalized. They don't invent new positioning or visuals
> — they organize and govern what's already decided.

## Documents in this phase

1. Comprehensive Brand Book (the master guidelines) · 2. Brand Guidelines Quick Reference (1-page) ·
3. Logo Usage Guidelines (standalone) · 4. Visual Identity Standards

## The rules baked into every template

- Category = **Advanced Aesthetics**; **full destination**; **"Where beauty blooms within."**
- Energy = fresh, modern, radiant + bold, iconic; timeless fashion-house confidence — **never
  clinical, never a coffin.**
- Compile faithfully from phases 01 + 02; no new or contradictory decisions.
- Concrete, governable rules + the anti-generic check.
