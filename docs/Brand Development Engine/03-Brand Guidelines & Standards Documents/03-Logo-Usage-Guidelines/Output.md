# Logo Usage Guidelines — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 03/03 · 2026-06-21 · standalone, vendor-shareable. Built from the real marks (02-01).*

## Approved versions & when to use
- **HR Monogram (gold).** Avatars, favicon, seals, embroidery, pattern, small "stamp" moments.
- **Wordmark "HOUSE OF ROSE AESTHETICS."** Headers, signage, documents — anywhere the name must read.
- **Tagline lockup ("Where beauty blooms within" over the rose image).** Emotional hero moments,
  covers, campaign opens. Not a functional UI logo.

## Clear space & minimum sizes
- **Clear space:** the cap-height of the monogram "H" on all four sides (½ H in tight UI).
- **Minimum sizes:** monogram 24px / 0.375in · wordmark 120px wide / 1.25in. Below these, use the monogram.

## Backgrounds & color treatments
- Gold monogram on **Ink**, **Ivory**, or **Burgundy** (preferred grounds).
- On photography: place on a calm, darker or ivory area; never on a busy/multicolor zone — switch to
  a one-color ivory or ink mark.
- One-color fallbacks (foil/embroidery/fax-grade): flat gold, all-ink, or all-ivory.

## Acceptable vs unacceptable
**Acceptable:** approved colorways, proportional scaling, on-brand grounds, ample clear space.
**Unacceptable:** ❌ stretch/distort · ❌ recolor outside palette · ❌ rotate/skew · ❌ add
shadows/glows/outlines beyond the existing dimensional gold · ❌ crop the R swash · ❌ gradient gold
on busy photos · ❌ re-typeset the tagline · ❌ box the mark or crowd it.
