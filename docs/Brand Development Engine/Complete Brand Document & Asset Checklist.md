# Complete Brand Document & Asset Checklist

## 📋 Strategy & Foundation Documents

- [ ] **Mission Statement Document** — Why the company exists
- [ ] **Vision Statement Document** — Future state and goals
- [ ] **Brand Values Framework** — Core beliefs and principles
- [ ] **Target Audience Personas** (3–5 detailed personas)
  - Demographics, psychographics, pain points, buying behaviors
- [ ] **Competitive Analysis Report** — Competitor landscape and gaps
- [ ] **Unique Value Proposition (UVP) Statement** — What makes you different
- [ ] **Brand Positioning Statement** — Market position and differentiation
- [ ] **Brand Archetype Document** — Personality and character definition
- [ ] **Voice & Tone Guidelines** — Communication style with examples
- [ ] **Brand Promise Statement** — What you guarantee customers
- [ ] **Brand Story Document** — Company origin and why you do what you do

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity Documents & Assets

- [ ] **Logo Files** (all formats)
  - Primary logo (color, black & white, grayscale)
  - Logo variations (icon, horizontal, vertical, lockup)
  - EPS, AI, PNG, SVG versions
- [ ] **Color Palette Document** 
  - Primary colors with hex, RGB, CMYK, Pantone codes
  - Secondary colors with same specs
  - Accent colors for CTAs and highlights
  - Color usage guidelines
- [ ] **Typography System Document**
  - Primary font family with sizes and weights
  - Secondary font family
  - Heading hierarchy (H1–H6 specs)
  - Body text sizing and line-height
  - Font licensing details
- [ ] **Photography & Imagery Style Guide**
  - Visual mood board (reference images)
  - Photography style guidelines (candid vs. posed, bright vs. dark)
  - Subject matter and scenarios
  - Color treatment rules
  - Illustration style (if applicable)
- [ ] **Icon Set** (if needed)
  - All icons in multiple sizes
  - Consistent style and weight
  - SVG files for scalability
- [ ] **Pattern & Texture Library** (if applicable)
  - Repeating design elements
  - Usage guidelines
- [ ] **Design System/Component Library** (for digital products)
  - Buttons, forms, cards, modals, etc.
  - Component variations and states

---

## 📖 Brand Guidelines & Standards Documents

- [ ] **Comprehensive Brand Guidelines/Brand Book** (50–100 pages)
  - All of the above combined
  - Do's and don'ts
  - Logo usage rules (minimum size, clear space, acceptable misuses)
  - Color usage examples
  - Typography in context
  - Photography examples
  - Real-world applications
  - Update log and version history

- [ ] **Brand Guidelines Quick Reference** (1-page or digital version)
  - Abbreviated version for quick lookups

- [ ] **Logo Usage Guidelines** (standalone document)
  - Clear space requirements
  - Minimum sizes for print and digital
  - Acceptable and unacceptable alterations
  - Background requirements

- [ ] **Visual Identity Standards Document**
  - All visual assets and how they work together
  - Spacing, sizing, and alignment rules

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with strategy** — Don't skip the thinking documents. Everything else flows from them.
2. **Use a design system** — Tools like Figma make it easy to manage all assets and keep them updated.
3. **Version control** — Always know which is the "official" version of each document.
4. **Collaborate** — Use shared drives (Google Drive, Dropbox, Notion) so everyone has access.
5. **Make it living** — Review and update these documents regularly, especially during brand refreshes.
6. **Distribute wisely** — Share strategy docs with leadership, visual assets with designers, messaging with marketing.

---

**Total estimate: 40–80 documents depending on complexity and scope.** Start with the essentials in Strategy and Visual Identity, then expand from there! 🚀
