# Brand Onboarding Guide — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 09/01 · 2026-06-21 · for new team members. From Story (01-11) + Voice (01-09).*

## Welcome — who we are
House of Rose is Punta Gorda's full **Advanced Aesthetics** destination — skin, regeneration,
injectables, wellness, and beauty under one standard. Our tagline is **"Where beauty blooms within."**
We build results the right way, and we're honest above all.

## Our story & values (the short version)
Beauty is built, not bought. Read the Brand Story (01-11). Live the five values: Results Built Right ·
Expertise Worn Lightly · Honesty as the Standard · Quiet Refinement · The Client, Cared For.

## How to represent the brand
- We're **Advanced Aesthetics** — never call us "med spa," "medical," "boutique," or "luxury." Let
  clients *feel* the luxury; we don't claim it.
- We're a **full house** — mention the whole range, not just one popular treatment.
- **Honesty first:** "From $X" pricing, real candidacy, "we'd rather decline than oversell."
- Defer clinical specifics to licensed providers; never guarantee results.

## Voice in a sentence
Confident, not loud. Warm and expert. Specific over superlative. (See examples in Voice 01-09.)
