# Employee Handbook — Brand Culture Section — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 09/04 · 2026-06-21 · from Values (01-03) + Story (01-11) + Promise (01-10).*

## Values → behaviors
- **Results Built Right** → recommend honestly in phases; never push the over-done look.
- **Expertise Worn Lightly** → explain the *why* in plain language; never condescend.
- **Honesty as the Standard** → "From $X," real candidacy, decline when right.
- **Quiet Refinement** → calm, considered, unhurried; the space and tone say "destination, not deal."
- **The Client, Cared For** → discretion, follow-through, treat each person as an individual.

## Client-experience standards (luxury, felt)
Warm welcome; unhurried consults; education before treatment; aftercare follow-up; privacy always.
The elevated feeling is something the client experiences — our job is to make it real, not to claim it.

## Culture ↔ promise
Every visit must keep the brand promise — *honest care, natural results, always you.* If a visit
fails the promise, we've failed the brand. We win by trust, not volume.
