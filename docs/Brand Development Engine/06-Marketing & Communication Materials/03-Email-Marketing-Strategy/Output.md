# Email Marketing Strategy — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 06/03 · 2026-06-21 · segmentation, automations, schedule, KPIs. No discounting.*

## Segments
- **New lead** (consult requested, not booked) · **First-visit** client · **Active member** ·
  **Active non-member** (repeat, no membership) · **Lapsed** (no visit 4–6mo) · **Plan/series** in
  progress · **Bridal/event** (date-driven).

## Automations
- **Welcome (3 emails)** → consultation. *(see 05-03)*
- **Post-visit** (24–48h): thank you, aftercare, what's next, rebooking.
- **Series/Plan nurture:** spaced touches matching the Rose Method cadence (provider-set intervals).
- **Membership nurture:** value of belonging → invite to join after 2nd–3rd visit.
- **Rebooking reminder:** by service cadence (e.g., facials ~monthly), gentle.
- **Win-back (lapsed):** "we've kept your place" → easy rebook (no guilt, no discount).
- **Bridal/event countdown:** date-anchored plan reminders.

## Schedule
1 value newsletter/month + automations triggered by behavior. Avoid over-mailing (≤ ~4 touches/mo
per person across streams).

## Content rules
Value/access-led, never discount-led; "From $X"; compliant; on-voice. Personalize by segment + lane.

## KPIs
Open, click, consult-booked, rebooking rate, membership conversion, list growth (quality), churn of
lapsed recovered. Review monthly; prune disengaged.
