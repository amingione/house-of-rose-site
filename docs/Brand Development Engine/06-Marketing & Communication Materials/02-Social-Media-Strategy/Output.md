# Social Media Strategy — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 06/02 · 2026-06-21 · platforms, pillars, cadence, engagement, hashtags.*

## Platforms & rationale
- **Instagram (primary):** where personas 1–3 are; visual, local discovery, Reels reach. Feed +
  Stories + Reels.
- **TikTok (growth):** education + reach for Marisol/Hannah; provider-led, tasteful.
- **Facebook (local trust):** older SW FL segment, community, reviews, events.
- **LinkedIn (light):** brand credibility, partnerships, hiring.
- (Pinterest optional for bridal/skin inspiration.)

## Content pillars per platform
All map to the 4 brand pillars (full house · method · honesty · care). IG: balanced mix. TikTok:
education-forward. FB: community + proof + local. LinkedIn: standard + people.

## Cadence & timing
IG 4–5×/week (mix feed + Reels) + daily-ish Stories · TikTok 3×/week · FB 3×/week · LinkedIn 1×/week.
Post around local peak (lunch + evening ET). Batch weekly from the calendar.

## Engagement guidelines
Respond on-voice (warm, expert, never defensive); answer questions with "designed to / your provider
confirms"; thank reviewers; DM booking → push to consultation. No public medical advice; no
arguing; escalate negative/compliance to owner.

## Hashtag strategy
Branded (#HouseOfRoseAesthetics #WhereBeautyBloomsWithin) + local (#PuntaGorda #PortCharlotte
#CharlotteCountyFL #SWFlorida) + category/service (rotate). ≤8, relevant, no spammy stacks.

## KPIs
Reach/saves/shares, profile→link taps, DM→consult, follower quality (local), and booked
consultations attributed to social.
