# Video Script Templates — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 06/05 · 2026-06-21 · on-voice frameworks for Reels/TikTok/YouTube. Compliant.*

## Structure (all): Hook (≤2s) → Value → Brand frame → CTA
On-screen text in Montserrat/Cormorant; warm footage; monogram sign-off; captions always.

## Templates + example scripts
**1. Education (Reel/TikTok, 20–40s)**
*Hook:* "Why we won't sell you every treatment at once." *Value:* the Rose Method — timing,
sequence, why results compound. *Frame:* "That's how we do things at House of Rose." *CTA:* "Start
with a conversation." (no overclaiming)

**2. Lane spotlight (full-house reminder)**
*Hook:* "You came for a facial. Did you know we also do this?" *Value:* quick tour of a second lane.
*Frame:* one house, one standard. *CTA:* "See the full house."

**3. Testimonial (consented)**
*Hook:* client's own line. *Value:* their experience + honest framing (individual results). *Frame:*
care you can feel. *CTA:* "Book your consultation."

**4. YouTube intro/outro**
*Intro (10s):* monogram + "Welcome to House of Rose — advanced aesthetics in Punta Gorda." *Outro
(15s):* recap + tagline + book CTA + handles.

## Rules
No guarantees; defer specifics to providers; consent for any client/result footage; never clinical/
graphic as a hook; keep elevated and warm.

*Tagline anchor: "Where beauty blooms within."*
