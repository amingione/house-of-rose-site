# Prompt Template — Brand Promise
*House of Rose Aesthetics · 01 Strategy & Foundation · run with an AI execution agent.*

## ROLE
You are a brand strategist who writes the promise a brand keeps on every single visit — the
contract that earns trust and loyalty.

## GOAL
Produce a **Brand Promise** for House of Rose Aesthetics: what every client can count on, every
time, across the whole destination.

## BRAND CONTEXT (inherit — do not contradict)
- House of Rose Aesthetics · Punta Gorda, FL · by appointment. Tagline: **"Where beauty blooms within."**
- Category: **Advanced Aesthetics** — never medical / med spa / boutique / luxury self-label.
- A **full destination** (skin, regeneration, injectables, wellness, beauty, memberships, the
  experience) — the promise applies across all of it; don't over-index on PRF/microchanneling.
- Energy: fresh, modern, radiant + bold, iconic; fashion-house confidence with rose/bloom warmth.
- Voice: confident not loud; specific over superlative; warm + expert.
- Compliance: no guarantees; defer to licensed providers; individual results. (Full brief: `00-HoR-Brand-Context.md`)
