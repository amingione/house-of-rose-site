# Brand Archetype — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 01-Strategy/08 template · 2026-06-21 · aligned to Positioning keystone (01-07).*

## Primary archetype — The Sovereign (Ruler)
House of Rose leads with **The Sovereign**: refinement, quality, and quiet authority. The Sovereign
stands for elevated standards and gives clients a feeling of control — here, control over how one
ages, on one's own terms. It explains the restraint, the premium pricing, the "destination not a
deal" posture, and the calm confidence of the voice — the timeless fashion-house assurance, never
clinical, never a coffin.

**Sovereign expressions:** curated (not crowded) menus · "From $X" pricing that signals value, not
bargains · considered, unhurried language · a sanctuary environment · membership tiers framed as
access and belonging.

## Secondary archetype — The Caregiver
Underneath the refinement is genuine **Caregiver** warmth: restoration, nurturing, protection. It
shows up as education before treatment, honesty about candidacy, follow-through between visits, and
a philosophy of *restoring* rather than altering. The Caregiver keeps the Sovereign from feeling
cold or aloof.

**Caregiver expressions:** "we'll guide you from there" · aftercare and home protocols · honest
"this isn't right for you yet" conversations · the language of care, ritual, and restoration.

## A note of the Sage
A supporting thread of **The Sage** runs through the brand — House of Rose educates relentlessly
(the Waxing Guide, the Rose Method™, the *why* behind every protocol across every lane). This builds
the trust the Sovereign and Caregiver depend on.

## The blend, in one sentence
> **A refined authority that restores you — advanced and exacting, yet warm and honest.**

## Archetype guardrails
- Lead Sovereign, soften with Caregiver. Never tip into cold elitism (pure Sovereign) **or** soft,
  unconfident fussiness (pure Caregiver).
- Avoid archetypes that don't fit: the Jester (no gimmicks), the Everyman (not "for everyone /
  cheapest"), the Outlaw (no shock or rule-breaking).
- When in doubt: *would a refined, caring expert say or do this?* If not, cut it.

*Tagline anchor: "Where beauty blooms within."*
