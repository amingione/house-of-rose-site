# Target Audience Personas — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 01-Strategy/04 template · 2026-06-21 · aligned to Positioning keystone (01-07).*

House of Rose serves a discerning Southwest Florida clientele — primarily women 35–70, with a
meaningful share of affluent retirees and seasonal residents, plus younger professionals and a quiet
but real male segment. Four personas capture the core. Together they span the full house — skin,
regeneration, injectables, wellness, and beauty — not a single treatment.

## Persona 1 — "The Refined Regular" · Diane, 58
**Snapshot:** Established Punta Gorda / Isles resident or snowbird. Comfortable, image-aware,
time-rich. Has done aesthetics before and knows what she doesn't want: anything obvious.
**Goals:** Look rested and current for her age; maintain, not transform; a provider she trusts.
**Fears:** Looking "done," frozen, or overfilled; being upsold; an inexperienced injector.
**Wins her:** Restraint, regenerative and injectable options for natural volume, a named-provider
relationship, discretion. *Heart of the membership and regeneration-plan business.*
**Where she is:** Word of mouth, Instagram, local search, community circles.

## Persona 2 — "The Investment-Minded Professional" · Marisol, 42
**Snapshot:** Works or runs a business in Charlotte County; busy; results-driven; researches first.
**Goals:** Visible improvement in skin quality (texture, tone, firmness) with minimal downtime;
efficiency; a clear plan.
**Fears:** Wasting money on one-off treatments that don't add up; downtime she can't afford.
**Wins her:** The Rose Method's structured, compounding logic; honest ROI framing; flexible
scheduling; clear before-and-after expectations.
**Where she is:** Google, AI search, Instagram saves, referrals.

## Persona 3 — "The Milestone Glow-Seeker" · Hannah, 31
**Snapshot:** Bride, new mom, or celebrating something. Event-driven, social, budget-conscious but
willing to invest for the moment.
**Goals:** Camera-ready, glowing skin by a date; a guided countdown; a little indulgence.
**Fears:** Trying something risky too close to the event; not knowing where to start.
**Wins her:** The Bridal Bloom / Countdown plan, dermaplaning + Glo2Facial, waxing and beauty
services, warm guidance, an entry membership.
**Where she is:** Instagram, TikTok, bridal networks, friend referrals.

## Persona 4 — "The Quiet Maintainer" · Mark, 55 *(secondary)*
**Snapshot:** Professional or retiree who wants to look healthy and sharp without anyone knowing.
**Goals:** Subtle anti-aging, hair restoration (PRF), grooming (waxing) — handled discreetly.
**Fears:** Anything that feels feminine, fussy, or conspicuous.
**Wins him:** Plain talk, privacy, efficiency, natural results, by-appointment quiet.

### Across all personas
They want to look like *themselves*, they distrust the over-done, and they'll pay for expertise and
trust. The brand speaks to that — never to bargain hunters or trend chasers.

*Tagline anchor: "Where beauty blooms within."*
