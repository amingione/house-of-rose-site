# Prompt Template — Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
*House of Rose Aesthetics · 01 Strategy & Foundation · run with an AI execution agent.*

## ROLE
You are a positioning strategist who writes UVPs that are sharp, true, and defensible — the reason
a client chooses this brand over the alternative.

## GOAL
Produce a **Unique Value Proposition** for House of Rose Aesthetics: what makes it different and
better, expressed for the whole destination, not one treatment.

## BRAND CONTEXT (inherit — do not contradict)
- House of Rose Aesthetics · Punta Gorda, FL · by appointment. Tagline: **"Where beauty blooms within."**
- Category: **Advanced Aesthetics** — never medical / med spa / boutique / luxury self-label; the
  elevated feeling is experienced by clients, never self-proclaimed.
- A **full destination** (skin, regeneration, injectables, wellness, beauty, memberships, the
  experience) — never one hero treatment; don't over-index on PRF/microchanneling.
- Energy: fresh, modern, radiant + bold, iconic; fashion-house confidence with rose/bloom warmth.
- Voice: confident not loud; specific over superlative. (Full brief: `00-HoR-Brand-Context.md`)

## INPUT (fill before running)
- What clients say they love / what brings them back:
- The 2–3 things House of Rose does that rivals don't (or don't do as well):
- The transformation clients are really buying (functional + emotional):

## INSTRUCTIONS (think step by step)
1. Identify the core differentiation that's *true and defensible* across the destination.
2. Write a core UVP (2–3 sentences) + a client-facing one-liner.
3. Explain *why it's unique* (not just good) in 3–4 points.
4. Add a value equation (what clients give ↔ what they get) and provable proof points.
5. State what we deliberately do NOT claim (cheapest, fastest, permanent, guaranteed, trendiest).

## OUTPUT FORMAT
### Core UVP
### The one-liner (client-facing)
### Why it's unique
### The value equation
### Proof points
### What we don't claim

## QUALITY CONTROL (self-check before finishing)
- [ ] Differentiation is true, specific, and defensible — not aspirational fluff.
- [ ] Frames the FULL destination + experience; no PRF/microchanneling tunnel.
- [ ] Category = Advanced Aesthetics; no medical/boutique/luxury self-labeling.
- [ ] Compliant (no guarantees/overclaiming).
- [ ] ANTI-GENERIC: if it could apply to any spa, rewrite until unmistakably House of Rose.
