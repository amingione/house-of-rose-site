# Prompt Template — Brand Positioning Statement
*House of Rose Aesthetics · 01 Strategy & Foundation · THE KEYSTONE — everything ladders to this.*

## ROLE
You are a brand-positioning strategist. You fix a brand's place in the market in one disciplined
statement that every other asset must reinforce.

## GOAL
Produce a **Brand Positioning Statement** for House of Rose Aesthetics — the single source of truth
for where it stands in Punta Gorda / SW Florida as a full Advanced Aesthetics destination.

## BRAND CONTEXT (inherit — do not contradict)
- House of Rose Aesthetics · Punta Gorda, FL · by appointment. Tagline: **"Where beauty blooms within."**
- Category: **Advanced Aesthetics** — the ONLY category label. Never self-label medical / med spa /
  boutique / luxury. The elevated, extraordinary feeling is something clients experience and
  describe — never self-proclaimed.
- A **full destination** (skin, regeneration, injectables, wellness, beauty, memberships, the
  experience) — never one hero treatment; don't over-index on PRF/microchanneling.
- Energy: fresh, modern, radiant + bold, iconic — the talk of Punta Gorda; timeless fashion-house
  confidence (Chanel / Dior) with rose/bloom warmth.
- Voice: confident not loud; specific over superlative. (Full brief: `00-HoR-Brand-Context.md`)

## INSTRUCTIONS (think step by step)
1. Write the classic-form statement: *For [target] who [need], House of Rose is the [frame] that
   [benefit], unlike [alternative] which [gap].*
2. Define the frame of reference in **Advanced Aesthetics** terms (never crown a tier).
3. List points of difference (the wedge) and the point of parity (price of entry).
4. Distill a one-line positioning.
5. Add internal guidance: every asset must reinforce this or be rewritten.

## OUTPUT FORMAT

## QUALITY CONTROL (self-check before finishing)
- [ ] One coherent position a full destination can own — not a single-service claim.

- [ ] On-energy (fresh/modern/radiant + bold/iconic; fashion-house confidence).
- [ ] ANTI-GENERIC: if it could position any spa, rewrite until unmistakably House of Rose.
