# Competitive Analysis Report — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 01-Strategy/05 · 2026-06-21 · aligned to Positioning keystone (01-07). Source: competitor_analysis/ + Pricing & Service Strategy.*

*Market: Punta Gorda · Port Charlotte · Charlotte County, with Cape Coral / Fort Myers / Naples as
the broader set.*

## Landscape (three tiers)
1. **Day spas / esthetics** (e.g., Spago Day Spa) — relaxation-led; lower price, lower clinical
   credibility; anchors the price floor.
2. **Skin & injectable practices** — the competitive core: facials, peels, microneedling, some
   injectables (Skin Sanctuary, C's Medi Spa, Sweet Spot MediSpa, Skin Spot Laser Club).
3. **Physician / Naples high-end** (Kent V. Hasen MD, Jewell, Azul) — polished, higher price;
   aspirational reference, not daily competition.

## Key players & where House of Rose wins
| Competitor | Location | Strength | Where House of Rose wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skin Sanctuary** | Punta Gorda | "Best of Charlotte" reputation, strong facials | Full house under one standard + the Rose Method + regenerative depth + honest pricing |
| **Spago Day Spa** | Punta Gorda | Affordable, broad spa menu | We don't compete on price — advanced, curated, premium experience |
| **C's Medi Spa** | Port Charlotte | Broad menu, body sculpting | Curated, expert-led restraint over breadth-for-its-own-sake |
| **Sweet Spot MediSpa** | Punta Gorda / Ft. Myers | Injectables, promotions | No discount/Groupon energy — trust + natural results |
| **Skin Spot Laser Club** | Cape Coral | Member pricing, lasers | Local convenience + regenerative specialization + full-house integration |
| **European Wax Center** | National | Wax Pass model, retail | Advanced-aesthetics waxing membership with no local equal |

## Whitespace House of Rose owns
- **A full destination under one standard.** Skin, regeneration, injectables, wellness, and beauty —
  unusually integrated for this market. The primary wedge.
- **A named method.** The Rose Method™ sells structured transformation, not one-offs.
- **Honesty-led positioning.** "From $X" pricing, candid candidacy, no fear-selling — a trust posture
  promotion-driven players can't credibly claim.
- **Regenerative depth (signature).** CARE-certified PRF across forms — real differentiation, framed
  as proof of expertise, not the brand's whole identity.
- **An elevated waxing membership + retail attach** — EWC's model, refined, no local equal.

## Threats / watch-items
Skin Sanctuary's brand equity + reviews (counter with our own reviews + breadth) · Naples brands
drifting south · injectable/GLP-1 price compression · new entrants copying "natural/PRF" messaging
(stay ahead via the *method* + the *full house* + proof).

## Strategic takeaway
Don't compete on price or menu sprawl. Compete on **a full house under one standard, a named method,
honesty, and intimacy** — with regenerative expertise as signature depth. These are the things
discounts and copycats can't quickly buy.
