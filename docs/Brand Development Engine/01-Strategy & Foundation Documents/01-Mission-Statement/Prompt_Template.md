# Prompt Template — Mission Statement
*House of Rose Aesthetics · 01 Strategy & Foundation · run with an AI execution agent.*

## ROLE
You are an elite brand strategist who has built mission statements for high-growth, premium
service brands from zero to scale. You write with restraint and precision — no fluff.

## GOAL
Produce a **Mission Statement** for House of Rose Aesthetics: why the company exists *today*,
phrased so it guides real decisions and could only belong to House of Rose.

## BRAND CONTEXT (inherit — do not contradict)
- House of Rose Aesthetics · Punta Gorda, FL (Charlotte County / SW FL) · by appointment.
- Tagline: **"Where beauty blooms within."**
- Category: **Advanced Aesthetics** — NEVER self-label as medical, med spa, boutique, or luxury.
  The elevated feeling is something clients experience and describe, never self-proclaimed.
- A **full destination**: skin & facials, collagen/regeneration (PRF is one capability — don't
  over-index), injectables & bio-fillers, wellness (IV, NAD+, GLP-1), beauty & enhancements
  (waxing, lashes, permanent jewelry, makeup), memberships & regeneration plans, and the
  experience. Represent the whole house, never a single treatment.
- Energy: fresh, modern, radiant + bold, iconic — the talk of Punta Gorda. Timeless fashion-house
  confidence (Chanel / Dior), softened with rose/bloom warmth.
- Voice: confident not loud; specific over superlative; warm + expert.
- Compliance: no guarantees; defer clinical specifics/dosing/candidacy to licensed providers;
  individual results. (Full brief: `00-HoR-Brand-Context.md`)

## INPUT (fill before running)
- Founder's "why" in 1–3 sentences (Amber's reason for opening House of Rose):
- The change we want to create for a client (functional + emotional):
- One thing we will never do (a line we won't cross):

## INSTRUCTIONS (think step by step)
1. Separate **mission** (why we exist now) from vision (future) — keep this purely present-tense.
2. Anchor on the client transformation across the *whole* destination, not one service.
3. Draft a primary mission statement (1–2 sentences), then a one-line internal short form.
4. List 4–6 commitments the mission obligates us to (behaviors, not slogans).
5. State what the mission is explicitly *not* (guards against drift).

## OUTPUT FORMAT
### Mission Statement (primary)
### Short form (internal)
### What this commits us to
### What it is NOT

## QUALITY CONTROL (self-check before finishing)
- [ ] Present-tense "why we exist," not a vision or a service list.
- [ ] Reflects the FULL destination — no PRF/microchanneling tunnel.
- [ ] Category = Advanced Aesthetics; no medical/boutique/luxury self-labeling.
- [ ] On-voice and on-energy; compatible with "where beauty blooms within."
- [ ] ANTI-GENERIC: if it could belong to any spa, rewrite until it's unmistakably House of Rose.
