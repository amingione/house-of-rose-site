# Prompt Template — Brand Story
*House of Rose Aesthetics · 01 Strategy & Foundation · run with an AI execution agent.*

## ROLE
You are a brand storyteller who writes origin narratives that make people feel something and
remember the brand — without clichés.

## GOAL
Produce a **Brand Story** for House of Rose Aesthetics: the narrative that ties the brand together
(long form, short form, one-liner, and reusable story beats), centered on the full destination and
the "bloom within" idea.

## BRAND CONTEXT (inherit — do not contradict)
- House of Rose Aesthetics · Punta Gorda, FL · by appointment. Tagline: **"Where beauty blooms within."**
- Category: **Advanced Aesthetics** — never medical / med spa / boutique / luxury self-label; the
  elevated feeling is experienced and described by clients, never self-proclaimed.
- A **full destination** (skin, regeneration, injectables, wellness, beauty, memberships, the
  experience) — tell the story of the *house*, never a single treatment; don't over-index on
  PRF/microchanneling.
- Energy: fresh, modern, radiant + bold, iconic — the talk of Punta Gorda; timeless fashion-house
  confidence (Chanel / Dior) with rose/bloom warmth. A grand opening of something extraordinary —
  never a coffin, never clinical.
- Voice: confident not loud; specific over superlative; sensory but disciplined. (Full brief: `00-HoR-Brand-Context.md`)

## INSTRUCTIONS (think step by step)
1. Open with the tension/problem (aesthetics done in pieces, the over-done look, the ordinary).
2. Turn to the belief/founding conviction, then the house we built (the full destination, one
   standard) and the "bloom within" idea (beauty drawn out, not bolted on).
3. Land on the payoff: how clients feel — and close on the tagline.
4. Provide: long form (story), short form (about-page length), one-line story, and 5 reusable
   story beats for campaigns/About page.
5. Avoid clichés ("turn back the clock," "fountain of youth") and any single-treatment framing.
