# House of Rose — Brand Context (the inherited brief)

Every prompt template in this phase inherits this context. Any AI executing a template must
honor it and must not contradict it. This is the guardrail that keeps the brand consistent and
keeps AI from drifting (e.g., tunneling on one treatment, or self-proclaiming "luxury").

---

## The brand at a glance

- **Name:** House of Rose Aesthetics
- **Where:** 525 E. Olympia Ave, Suite 9 · Punta Gorda, FL 33950 · serving Charlotte County & SW Florida
- **Tagline:** **"Where beauty blooms within."**
- **Category / positioning:** **Advanced Aesthetics.** This is the only category label we use.
- **Model:** By appointment; multi-provider house with one standard.

## Non-negotiable positioning rules

1. **We are "Advanced Aesthetics."** Never self-label as *medical*, *med spa*, *boutique*, or
   *luxury*. The elevated, extraordinary feeling is something clients **experience and describe** —
   never something we crown ourselves with.
2. **We are a FULL destination — not a single treatment.** House of Rose offers the complete
   spectrum:
   - **Skin & facials** — facials, Glo2Facial, dermaplaning, peels, LED, acne programs
   - **Collagen & regeneration** — microchanneling, PRF (one capability among many — *do not over-index on it*)
   - **Injectables & bio-fillers** — neurotoxins, dermal fillers, PRF/EZ Gel
   - **Wellness** — IV hydration lounge, NAD+, GLP-1 programs
   - **Beauty & enhancements** — waxing, lashes, permanent jewelry, makeup
   - **Memberships, packages & regeneration plans** — the ongoing relationship
   - **The experience** — the space, the people, the feeling
   > Critical: AI keeps tunneling on microchanneling/PRF. **Represent the whole house.** If a
   > deliverable reads like a PRF clinic, it has failed.
3. **Energy:** fresh, modern, radiant **and** bold, iconic — the talk of Punta Gorda. Carry the
   confidence of a **timeless fashion house (Chanel / Dior)**, softened with the warmth and
   romance of the rose/bloom. A grand opening of something extraordinary — never clinical, never a
   coffin.

## Voice

Confident, not loud · warm and expert · specific over superlative · sensory but disciplined.
Sentence case, no hype, no urgency gimmicks. Pricing always reads "From $X" or a range — never a
bare flat number.

## Compliance (always)

No guarantees ("designed to," "supports," "many clients see"). Defer clinical specifics, dosing,
intervals, and candidacy to the licensed provider. No uniform before/after promises; emphasize
individual results. House of Rose offers advanced aesthetic services, including injectable and
wellness treatments performed by licensed providers.

## The anti-generic rule (paste into every template)

> *If this output could apply to any generic business — or any generic spa — it is WRONG. Refine
> until it is unmistakably House of Rose: specific, differentiated, and defensible.*
