# 01 · Strategy Core — House of Rose Aesthetics

The foundational strategy layer of the House of Rose brand. Everything downstream — visual
identity, messaging, web copy, campaigns, packaging, the in-suite experience — should trace
back to the eleven documents in this folder. If a decision can't be justified against these,
it's off-brand.

| # | Document | What it answers |
|---|----------|-----------------|
| 01 | Mission Statement | Why we exist, today |
| 02 | Vision Statement | Where we're going |
| 03 | Brand Values Framework | What we stand for and how it shows up |
| 04 | Target Audience Personas | Who we serve |
| 05 | Competitive Analysis Report | Who else is in the market and where the whitespace is |
| 06 | Unique Value Proposition | Why a client chooses us over the alternative |
| 07 | Brand Positioning Statement | The single sentence that fixes our place in the market |
| 08 | Brand Archetype | The personality the brand embodies |
| 09 | Voice & Tone Guidelines | How we sound, everywhere |
| 10 | Brand Promise | What every client can count on |
| 11 | Brand Story | The narrative that ties it together |

---

**Brand:** House of Rose Aesthetics
**Location:** 525 E. Olympia Ave, Suite 9 · Punta Gorda, FL 33950 · serving Charlotte County & Southwest Florida
**Model:** Advanced aesthetics — by appointment (the elevated feeling is earned in the suite, not a self-proclaimed tier)
**Signature philosophy:** The Rose Method™ — *not everything at once; everything at the right time.*

*Sources & companions in this folder:* `regeneration-plans.md`, the pricing & service strategy
doc, `competitor_analysis/`, and `SEO-AEO-PLAYBOOK.md`.

*Compliance note:* House of Rose offers advanced aesthetic services (including injectable and
wellness treatments performed by licensed providers). All brand language avoids
guarantees, defers clinical decisions to licensed providers and client candidacy, and never
overstates outcomes. This applies to every document built from this strategy core.
