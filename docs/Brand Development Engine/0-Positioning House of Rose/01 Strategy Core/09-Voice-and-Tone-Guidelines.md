# Voice & Tone Guidelines

## Voice (constant) vs. Tone (situational)

**Voice** is who we are — it never changes. **Tone** flexes with context (a consult vs. a
post-treatment text vs. an Instagram caption). The voice below holds everywhere.

## The House of Rose voice

> Confident, not loud. Warm and expert. Specific over superlative. Sensory but disciplined.

- **Confident, not loud.** State results plainly; let restraint signal prestige. No exclamation
  pile-ups, no urgency.
- **Warm and expert.** Clinical credibility delivered with hospitality. We're the knowledgeable
  friend who happens to be a pro.
- **Specific over superlative.** "Visibly firmer, more even skin over a series" beats "amazing
  results."
- **Sensory but disciplined.** Evoke the experience and the transformation without purple prose.

## Sound like this

- "Where beauty blooms within." *(brand tagline)*
- "Not everything at once. Everything at the right time."
- "Looking like yourself — refreshed, never overdone."
- "We'll never sell you something you don't need."
- "Care you can feel from the moment you walk in."

## Never sound like this

- Hype or pressure: "Act now!", "Limited spots!", "Don't miss out!"
- Discount energy: "cheap," "deal," "blowout," "lowest prices."
- Clichés: "turn back the clock," "fountain of youth," "snatched," "BBL glow up."
- Overclaiming: "permanent," "guaranteed," "erases," "eliminates."
- Clinical coldness or jargon-as-status.

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone dial |
|---------|-----------|
| Website / service pages | Polished, assured, outcome-led |
| Consultation & in-suite | Warm, educational, unhurried |
| Post-treatment / aftercare | Caring, clear, reassuring |
| Instagram / social | Aspirational but human; a touch more relaxed, still refined |
| Email / membership | Personal, gracious, value-led (access, not discounts) |

## Mechanics

- **Sentences:** short and confident; vary rhythm; let some breathe.
- **Capitalization:** sentence case. Never ALL CAPS for emphasis, never Title Case In Body Copy.
- **Pricing:** always "From $X" or a range — never bare flat numbers (more aspirational, and our
  standing convention).
- **Emojis:** sparingly, if at all — a single 🌹 is the most we reach for.
- **"You," not "the patient."** Clients are guests, not cases.

## The compliance overlay (non-negotiable)

- No guarantees. Use "designed to," "supports," "many clients see," "helps."
- Defer clinical specifics, dosing, and candidacy to the licensed provider.
- No uniform before/after promises; emphasize individual, realistic outcomes.

## The one-line test

*Confident, kind, specific, and honest — would a refined expert say it exactly this way?* If not,
revise.
