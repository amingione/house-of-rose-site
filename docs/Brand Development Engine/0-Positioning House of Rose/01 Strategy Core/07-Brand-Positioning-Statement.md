# Brand Positioning Statement

## The classic-form statement

**For** discerning women and men in Southwest Florida who want to look refreshed and naturally
youthful — never "done" — **House of Rose Aesthetics** is the **advanced aesthetics studio** that
**builds lasting results from your own biology through a structured, well-paced method, delivered
with clinical expertise and honest, hospitality-grade care** — so the experience *feels* elevated
without ever having to call itself that.

**Unlike** conventional practices and day spas that sell one-off treatments and chase the over-done
look, **House of Rose** treats beauty as something built the right way, over time — so results
compound and look unmistakably like you.

## The frame of reference

We compete in **advanced aesthetics** in Charlotte County — more results-driven than a relaxation
day spa, more personal and considered than a promotion-driven practice, and at a more intimate
scale than the Naples injector brands. We don't crown ourselves with a tier. The elevated feeling
is something clients *experience* in the suite and describe in their own words — earned through the
space, the care, and the results, never self-proclaimed.

## Points of difference (the wedge)

1. Regenerative-first (PRF / collagen induction, CARE-certified).
2. A named method — the Rose Method™ — not just a service list.
3. Honesty and transparency as the premium.
4. Intimate, named-provider, by-appointment care.

## Point of parity (the price of entry)

Credible, safe, expertly delivered advanced aesthetic services — table stakes we
clearly meet, so the differentiators can carry the decision.

## The positioning in one line

> **Regenerative aesthetics, built the right way — results that look unmistakably like you.**

## Internal guidance

Every brand expression should reinforce *regenerative + method + honesty + intimacy*. If a piece of
work could just as easily belong to a discount practice or a generic spa, it isn't positioned —
rewrite it.
