# Competitive Analysis Report

*Market: Punta Gorda · Port Charlotte · Charlotte County, with Cape Coral / Fort Myers / Naples as
the broader competitive set. See `competitor_analysis/` and the Pricing & Service Strategy doc for
source pricing.*

## The landscape at a glance

The local market splits into three tiers:

1. **Day spas / esthetics** — relaxation-led facials and waxing (e.g., Spago Day Spa). Lower price,
   lower clinical credibility.
2. **Skin & injectable practices** — the competitive core: facials, peels, microneedling, some
   injectables (Skin Sanctuary, C's Medi Spa, Sweet Spot MediSpa, Skin Spot Laser Club).
3. **Physician / Naples high-end** — injector-led, higher price, polished brands (Kent V. Hasen MD,
   Jewell, Azul) — aspirational reference, not direct daily competition.

## Whitespace House of Rose owns

- **True regenerative aesthetics.** CARE-certified PRF across every delivery form (microchanneling,
  under-eye, EZ Gel bio-filler, hair, body) — no local competitor matches the depth.
- **A named method.** The Rose Method™ gives clients a reason to commit to a *journey*, not a
  one-off. Competitors sell services; we sell structured transformation.
- **Honesty-led positioning.** "From / starting at" pricing, candor about candidacy, no
  fear-selling — a trust posture the promotion-driven players can't credibly claim.
- **An advanced-aesthetics waxing membership + retail attach.** EWC's model, elevated, with no
  local competitor offering it.
- **Multi-lane house under one standard.** Advanced aesthetics + injectables + wellness + beauty,
  unusually integrated for the area.

## Threats / watch-items

- Skin Sanctuary's brand equity and reviews — counter with regenerative differentiation + reviews
  of our own.
- Naples high-end brands drifting south on price/marketing.
- Compounded-GLP-1 and injectables price compression market-wide.
- New entrants copying the "natural/PRF" message — stay ahead via the *method* and proof.

## Strategic takeaway

Do not compete on price or menu breadth. Compete on **regenerative expertise, a named method,
honesty, and intimacy** — the things money and discounts can't quickly buy.
