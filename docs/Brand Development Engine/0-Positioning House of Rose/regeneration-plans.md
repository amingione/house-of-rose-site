## 1 · The Strategic Reframe

The original flyer had one good instinct and three quiet problems.

**The instinct (keep it):** *Skin does not transform in moments — it transforms through timing,
consistency, and structure.* That single line is the brand's whole differentiator. It reframes
aesthetics from transactions to architecture, and it justifies a premium that à-la-carte facials
never can. We lead with it everywhere.

**The problems (fix them):**

1. **One plan, one price band ($5,000–$7,000).** A single high number with nothing beneath it
   gives a first-time client no on-ramp and no reason to believe the figure. There was no entry
   point and no ceiling — just a wall.
2. **A thin treatment story.** Only ProCell microchanneling and PRF were named, when the studio
   offers a deep regenerative arsenal (Glo2Facial, BioRePeel, PRF in five delivery forms, EZ Gel
   bio-filler, LightStim LED, IV therapy, ProCell MD home protocol). The plan should *showcase the
   depth*, not hide it.
3. **"Not a membership" with no recurring structure.** Saying what it *isn't* left a hole where
   lifetime value should be. A plan ends; the relationship shouldn't. There was nothing to graduate
   into.

**The fix — a three-part architecture:**

| Layer | What it is | Job to be done |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| **The Entry** | Consultation or Internal Hydration Session | Low-friction first step; the diagnostic that designs the plan |
| **The Plans** | Outcome-anchored Regeneration Plans (a ladder + specialized journeys) | The transformation — structured, sequenced, 3–6 months |
| **The Rose Circle** | Continuity membership in three tiers | The relationship after results — maintenance, access, belonging |

This keeps the prestige of "this is a plan, not a punch card" while building the recurring revenue
a membership was meant to create — just earned through results first, access second.

## 2 · The Rose Method™ — The Philosophy That Sells Everything

Use this as the connective narrative across the website, the consultation, and every plan page.

> **Most aesthetic care happens in isolation — a facial here, an injectable there.**
> The Rose Method is different. We design your skin's transformation the way it actually
> happens: in phases, in sequence, at the right time. We rebuild from a foundation of
> healthy skin, stimulate your own collagen intentionally, and refine — never "done," always
> *more like you.* Not everything at once. Everything at the right time.

**The four phases** (the spine every plan is built on):

1. **Reveal** — clear, resurface, and prepare the canvas (Glo2Facial, BioRePeel, dermaplaning, LED).
2. **Regenerate** — stimulate collagen and tissue from within (ProCell microchanneling, PRF in its
   many forms).
3. **Restore** — replace what time has taken, naturally where possible (EZ Gel bio-filler, PRF
   under-eye, refined neurotoxin, dermal filler).
4. **Sustain** — protect the investment (IV therapy, ProCell MD home protocol, The Rose Circle).

Every plan below is simply a different *path* through these four phases, weighted to the client's
goal. That's the system the client buys into.

### ✦ THE RENEWAL PLAN™ — *the foundation*
**Phase weighting: Reveal → light Regenerate · ~3 months · Investment from $1,950 (monthly options available)**

**Ideal outcome.** Skin that looks lit-from-within: smoother texture, more even tone, a visible

**Suggested rhythm (experience guidance, not medical protocol).** A Glo2Facial to open, then
BioRePeel roughly every 3–4 weeks with LED finishes, dermaplaning timed to life events, home care
from day one. Your provider sets exact intervals.

**Premium positioning.** This isn't a facial package — it's the first chapter of a longer story.
Most clients who complete Renewal are perfect candidates to step up into Regeneration, with their
foundation already built.

## 4 · Specialized Plans — Beyond the Ladder

Not every transformation is the anti-aging arc. These outcome-specific plans use the same Rose Method
structure and let the studio meet clients exactly where their goal lives.

### ✦ THE CLARITY PLAN™ — *clear skin, for good*
**~12 weeks · $899 program (The Clarity Bootcamp) + curated home care · monthly options available**

For acne and congestion. Built on the Face Reality Clarity Bootcamp — acne-typing consultation, six
biweekly in-studio sessions (customized peels, extractions, LED), and a home routine adjusted at every
visit. Drug-free, antibiotic-free, built on the program that gets over 90% of *committed* clients
clear. *Add the Back Treatment for body breakouts before boating and event season.* Graduates into
maintenance Acne Peels and a Rose Circle tier. **Outcomes are individual; candidacy is confirmed at
the acne-typing consultation.**

### ✦ THE COUNTDOWN PLAN™ — *event & bridal*
**Timeline-based: 6 / 3 / 1 month + week-of · investment by timeline**

A reverse-engineered glow path to a wedding or milestone date:

- **6 months out** — BioRePeel series and/or microchanneling to build genuine skin quality.
- **3 months out** — refinement: Glo2Facial cadence, any volume or neurotoxin well ahead of the date.
- **1 month out** — Glo2Facial, lock the routine, no new aggressive treatments.
- **Week of** — dermaplaning for flawless, photograph-ready skin; LED to calm.
- **Bridal-party add-on** — Glo2Facials and permanent jewelry for the whole party.

The luxury angle: we never gamble a wedding on a last-minute treatment. We *plan backward* from the
date so the skin peaks on the day. Final timing is set with your provider so nothing is introduced too
close to the event.

### ✦ THE RESTORED PLAN™ — *hair*
**PRF Hair Restoration series · ~3–4 sessions + maintenance · investment from $2,400**

For early-to-moderate thinning. A series of PRF scalp treatments — your own platelet-rich fibrin

effective as a committed series; candidacy and cadence confirmed at consultation.

### ✦ THE BODY REGENERATION PLAN™ — *scars, stretch marks, crepey skin*
**PRF Body and/or BioRePeel Body, series of 3 · priced per area, from $1,800/area · monthly options**

The liquid-gold protocol applied where the body needs it — stretch marks, surgical and acne scars,
crepey arms, knees, and décolleté. Priced by treatment area because larger zones take longer and use
more of your PRF. Series of three recommended for progressive remodeling.

### ✦ THE INNER GLOW PLAN™ — *wellness-led*
**IV Hydration series + optional GLP-1 program · from $129/visit · GLP-1 from $349/month**

Regeneration from the inside: a structured IV journey (classic hydration, Myers', beauty drips with
glutathione and biotin, NAD+) with vitamin-shot add-ons — pairs beautifully with any skin plan or
stands alone. For metabolic goals, the supervised GLP-1 program adds weekly check-ins, nutrition
guidance, and body-composition tracking. *Medical evaluation required to begin GLP-1; this is
structured clinical care, not a prescription and a goodbye.*

**What triggers each step:**

## 7 · Why It Works — Retention & Business Logic *(internal)*

**Why clients stay (retention logic):**

- **Visible, cumulative results.** Regenerative work compounds; stopping means watching it fade. The
  structure makes continuity the rational choice, not an upsell.
- **Belonging and status.** Member evenings, concierge access, and the "En Pleine Rose" ceiling create
  an identity worth keeping. Leaving feels like a loss of standing, not just a cancelled subscription.
- **Rhythm without pressure.** Banked credits and provider-set cadence keep clients returning on a
  natural schedule.

**How it lifts the business (directional, ethical framing — no guaranteed figures):**

- **Higher average ticket.** Plans convert single-service buyers into multi-month, multi-treatment
  commitments anchored on outcomes rather than price.
- **Predictable recurring revenue.** The Rose Circle turns post-plan clients into monthly members,
  smoothing the calendar and the cash flow.
- **Stronger lifetime value.** The Entry → Plan → Membership → tier-up pathway is a designed ladder of
  deepening relationship, each step raising retention and the next-purchase rate.
- **Prestige preserved.** Because value is framed as access, curation, and results — never discounting —
  growth doesn't cheapen the brand.

## 8 · Compliance & Voice Notes *(internal — keep on every public surface)*

- **No guarantees.** Use "designed to," "supports," "many clients see," "helps reveal." Never
  "permanent," "will eliminate," or "guaranteed."
- **Provider authority.** Final treatment recommendations, combinations, dosing, and intervals depend
  on the provider's clinical judgment, client candidacy, contraindications, and local regulations.
- **Candidacy is real.** Not every client is a candidate for every service; the consultation determines
  suitability. State it wherever a plan is sold.
- **No uniform before/after promises.** Emphasize personalization and realistic, individual outcomes.
- **Pricing language.** Always "investment," "from," "starting at," with monthly options framed as
  *support for consistency*, not financing pressure.
- **Tone.** Confident, not loud. Specific over superlative. Sensory but disciplined. The brand is a
  destination, never a deal.
