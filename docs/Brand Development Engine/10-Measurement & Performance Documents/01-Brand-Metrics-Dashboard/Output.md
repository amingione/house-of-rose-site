# Brand Metrics Dashboard — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 10/01 · 2026-06-21 · KPIs + methodology. Can power a Cowork artifact.*

## KPI set
- **Awareness:** local search impressions/rank (GSC), AI-overview presence, social reach, branded
  search volume.
- **Perception:** review rating + volume (Google), sentiment, NPS (10-03).
- **Engagement:** site sessions, key page views (services/plans/memberships), social saves/shares,
  email open/click, DM→consult.
- **Business impact:** consultations booked, booking rate, new clients, **membership conversion**,
  **retention/repeat-visit rate**, average ticket / "From $X" uplift, plan completion.

## Sources & cadence
GA4 · Google Search Console · Google Business Profile (reviews) · booking/POS system · social
insights · email platform. Weekly pulse + monthly review + quarterly brand review (10-02).

## Dashboard layout
KPI cards (awareness/perception/engagement/business) → trend lines → lane mix → funnel
(reach→consult→client→member). Targets/benchmarks per KPI; flag red/yellow/green.

## Targets (set baselines first 90 days)
Establish baselines, then target MoM growth in consults, membership conversion, retention. Keep
quality (local relevance) over vanity reach.
