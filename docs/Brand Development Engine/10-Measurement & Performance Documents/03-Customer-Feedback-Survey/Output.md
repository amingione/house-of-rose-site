# Customer Feedback Survey — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 10/03 · 2026-06-21 · awareness, perception, NPS, preference. On-voice, unbiased.*

## Question sets
**NPS:** "How likely are you to recommend House of Rose to a friend? (0–10)" + "What's the main
reason?"
**Perception:** "In a few words, how would you describe House of Rose?" · "Did you feel cared for
and given honest guidance? (1–5)" · "Did you leave looking like yourself? (1–5)."
**Awareness/discovery:** "How did you first hear about us?" (referral / Google / AI search / social /
walk-by / other).
**Preference:** "What made you choose us over other options?" (open) · "Which lanes are you
interested in next?" (multi: skin/regen/injectables/wellness/beauty).
**Experience:** "Anything that would have made your visit better?" (open).

## Design & delivery
≤6 questions, ~2 min; post-visit email (24–48h) + QR in-suite; smart routing (low NPS → service
recovery). On-voice, neutral wording.

## Reading results
Track NPS trend, perception themes (do clients say "honest / natural / cared for"?), discovery mix,
lane interest (cross-sell signal). Feed the brand audit (10-02) + dashboard (10-01).
