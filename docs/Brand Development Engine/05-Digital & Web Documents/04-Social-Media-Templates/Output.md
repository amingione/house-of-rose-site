# Social Media Templates — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 05/04 · 2026-06-21 · layouts + caption frameworks. On-brand, full destination.*

## Visual system (all formats)
Ivory/greige grounds, burgundy + gold accents, Cormorant headline, Montserrat tracked eyebrow,
warm editorial photography, gold monogram sign-off, sparse botanicals. Never clinical, never busy.

## Formats
- **IG feed (1080×1350):** image-led; eyebrow + short Cormorant line; monogram corner. Variants:
  education, lane spotlight, result (consented), quote, behind-the-house.
- **Stories/Reels cover (1080×1920):** big Cormorant headline on ivory or over-image lower-third;
  one CTA ("Book," "Learn"); monogram.
- **Carousel:** cover (hook) → 3–5 value cards → CTA card. For "how the Rose Method works," "5 lanes."
- **LinkedIn (1200×627):** more editorial/professional; brand standard, partnerships, hiring.
- **TikTok:** vertical, captioned, hook in first 2s; provider-led education, tasteful.

## Caption frameworks
- **Education:** Hook → 1 insight → "here's how we think about it" → soft CTA. (no overclaiming)
- **Lane spotlight:** "Did you know House of Rose also does ___?" → reinforce full house → CTA.
- **Proof (consented):** client moment → honest framing (individual results) → CTA.
- **Brand/values:** restraint, honesty, the experience → tagline sign-off.
- **CTA patterns:** "Start with a conversation." · "Book your consultation." · "See the full house."

## Hashtag approach (local-first)
Mix branded (#HouseOfRoseAesthetics #WhereBeautyBloomsWithin) + local (#PuntaGorda #CharlotteCountyFL
#SWFlorida) + category (#AdvancedAesthetics #Microchanneling #PRF #Facials) — rotate, keep ≤8,
relevant.

## Do / Don't
Do: warm imagery, whitespace, full-house variety, consented proof. Don't: discount energy, hype,
clinical/syringe shots as heroes, single-treatment monotony, stocky spa clichés.
