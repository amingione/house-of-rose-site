# 04 · Messaging & Communication — Prompt Template Library

The **verbal layer** of the House of Rose AI Brand Factory. These templates turn the strategy into
the words the brand actually uses — framework, pitches, about copy, service descriptions, FAQ, and
press kit.

> Run these **after** Strategy (phase 01) is locked — especially Positioning (07) and Voice & Tone
> (09). Every message must ladder back to the positioning and sound like the brand.

## Documents in this phase

1. Messaging Framework (tagline + pillars + proof) · 2. Elevator Pitch (multiple versions) ·
3. About Us Copy (50/100/200-word) · 4. Service Descriptions (every offering) · 5. Testimonials &
Case Studies · 6. FAQ · 7. Media / Press Kit

## The rules baked into every template

- Voice = **confident not loud, specific over superlative, warm + expert.**
- Tagline = **"Where beauty blooms within."**
- Category = **Advanced Aesthetics**; **full destination** (don't tunnel on PRF/microchanneling).
- Energy = fresh, modern, radiant + bold, iconic; fashion-house confidence.
- Compliance (no guarantees, defer to providers, individual results) + the anti-generic rule.
