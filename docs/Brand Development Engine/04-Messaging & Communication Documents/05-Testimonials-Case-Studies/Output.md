# Testimonials & Case Studies — House of Rose Aesthetics
*Generated via 04/05 · 2026-06-21 · system + formats. Maps to Sanity `caseStudy`. Consent-based; nothing fabricated.*

## Collection & consent framework
- **Ask at the right moment:** after a series result or a milestone visit, in person or via a short
  follow-up. Make it easy (1–2 questions + optional photo).
- **Consent is required and recorded** before any quote, name, or photo is used — including how it
  may be used (web, social, print) and for how long. Honor withdrawal.
- **Before/after:** same angle, distance, lighting; no filters; note timeframe + that results vary.

## Editing standards
Tighten for clarity, keep the client's own voice, never invent or embellish. No medical claims in a
client's mouth. Remove identifying details if requested.

## Formats
**Testimonial (short):** quote · first name + lane · optional photo.
> "I look like myself, just more rested. They told me what I *didn't* need — that's why I trust them."
> — *Diane, regeneration plan*

**Case study (`caseStudy` doc):** Situation (goal, honestly framed) → Experience (the plan / Rose
Method) → Result (individual, designed-to language, timeframe) → optional consented before/after →
soft CTA. Maps to `caseStudy`: `title`, `client` (first name/anon), `lane`, `summary`, `body`,
`beforeAfter[]`, `consentOnFile` (bool), `seo`.
