 Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF)
What is PRF?
Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is a next-generation regenerative treatment created from a small sample
of your own blood. Unlike PRP, PRF is processed without anticoagulants, allowing a natural fibrin
matrix to slowly release platelets, growth factors, and white blood cells over time. This supports
your body's natural healing process and stimulates collagen production for healthier, more
youthful-looking skin.
Benefits of PRF
- 100% natural using your own blood
- Stimulates collagen and elastin production
- Improves skin texture, tone, and elasticity
- Softens fine lines and wrinkles
- Enhances skin quality naturally over time
- Little to no downtime
- Long-lasting regenerative effects
- Safe for patients seeking a natural alternative to synthetic products

PRF Hair Restoration
PRF injections deliver concentrated growth factors directly to the scalp to support healthier hair
follicles, reduce shedding, and encourage thicker, stronger hair growth. A series of treatments is
typically recommended for best results.
What to Expect
A small blood sample is drawn and placed in a specialized centrifuge to separate the PRF. The
PRF is then injected into the treatment area or used with microneedling. Treatments usually take
45–60 minutes. While some improvement may be seen after the first treatment, optimal results are
generally achieved with a series of 3–4 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart.
