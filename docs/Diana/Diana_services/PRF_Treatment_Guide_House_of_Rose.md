# PRF Treatment Guide House of Rose

## Understanding PRF Treatments

### What is PRF?

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is created from a small sample of your own blood. It contains platelets,
fibrin, white blood cells and growth factors that stimulate collagen, improve healing and rejuvenate
tissue naturally. Because no anticoagulants are used, PRF releases growth factors gradually over
time.

| Injectable PRF | PRF Microchanneling |
|---|---|
| Injected beneath the skin | Applied during microneedling/microchanneling |
| Best for under-eyes, facial rejuvenation, and hair restoration | Best for acne scars, pores, texture, fine lines, pigmentation, and overall skin quality |
| Stimulates collagen below the skin | Stimulates collagen throughout the skin surface |
| Targets volume loss and tissue quality | Targets skin texture and radiance |
| Little downtime | 24–72 hours of redness is common |

### Why choose PRF?

PRF uses your body's own regenerative cells to stimulate collagen and elastin, improve skin quality,
support healthier hair follicles, soften fine lines, and create natural-looking rejuvenation without
synthetic fillers.
