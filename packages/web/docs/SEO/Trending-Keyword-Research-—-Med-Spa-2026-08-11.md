## Headline

Of **640** qualifying med spa keywords, **27 are trending** at +25% or better over the last six complete months (Feb–Jul 2026). Average growth across them is **+65.5%**.

But only **12 of the 27 are real trends.** The rest are summer seasonality. Every keyword below carries a year-over-year check alongside the 6-month number — see "How to read this" before acting on any row.

`houseofrosefl.com` ranks for **zero** organic keywords (re-verified this run), so all 27 are net-new opportunities rather than positions to defend.

## How to read this

The 6-month growth number is what most keyword tools show you, and on its own it is misleading in aesthetics. A Jan→Jul window compares winter to summer, and demand for laser, body contouring and pigmentation treatments swings hard with the season.

So each row also carries **YoY**: the same three recent months against the same three months a year earlier. That cancels seasonality out.

* **Durable** — 6-month growth is positive *and* YoY is +15% or better. Real, structural demand growth. Act on these.
* **Seasonal** — 6-month growth looks strong but YoY is flat or negative. The term is rising because it's July, not because the market is growing. Still worth targeting, but time the content and don't expect the growth rate to hold.

The clearest example is **melasma treatment**: +65.8% over six months, 17,000 searches a month — and −1.1% YoY. Demand is not growing at all; it peaks every summer with sun exposure. Publish it in autumn to rank for next summer, not now.

## Implementation use

1. Keep one canonical, substantive page for each verified service. Use the current IV, Lumecca, PRF, dermal-filler, and concern routes instead of creating keyword variants.
2. Use search language only where it accurately describes the verified service. A high-volume adjacent brand or treatment is not a synonym.
3. Treat Google Business Profile and current local entity data as separate authenticated work. Do not simulate that work with geo-stuffed service pages.
4. Reconnect current GSC and bot-analytics evidence before using the old "not ranking" observation to judge the revised site.
5. Create a new page only after the service exists in current commerce truth, fits a documented page type, has supported claims and imagery, and can be linked without cannibalizing a canonical route.

## Method and limits

Discovery ran against Ahrefs Keywords Explorer. Two constraints shaped the approach:

* The workspace Ahrefs plan is **Lite**, and the keyword-*ideas* endpoint hit its row quota partway through this run (resets **2026-08-15**). Automatic semantic expansion was therefore unavailable, so the candidate set was constructed manually — 89 med spa services and conditions crossed with 19 intent modifiers, plus comparison and long-tail terms, for 1,811 candidates.
* Growth-rate fields are disabled on the Lite plan, so growth was **computed from raw monthly search-volume history** rather than read from Ahrefs. 640 keywords each got a real volume series; growth uses 3-month averaging (avg of the last 3 complete months vs the 3 months six months prior), which is the same method the Ahrefs UI uses.

1,377 of 1,811 candidates returned data; 640 passed volume ≥100 and KD ≤40; all 640 got history; 27 cleared +25% growth. No estimated or interpolated figures — every number traces to an Ahrefs response.

The manual candidate set is the main limitation: it covers the aesthetics service vocabulary thoroughly but will miss emerging terms nobody has named yet. Worth re-running after 2026-08-15 with automatic expansion enabled to catch those.
