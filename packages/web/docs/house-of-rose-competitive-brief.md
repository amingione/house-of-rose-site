## How to read this document

Every factual claim carries the URL it came from. Claims are labeled one of two ways:

- **Verified** — read directly off a live page, quoted or transcribed. Default; unlabeled claims are verified.
- **INFERENCE** — my reasoning from verified evidence. Always labeled inline.

Where a competitor does not publish a price, this brief says **"not published."** No competitor price in this document is estimated.

# PHASE 1 — The market, pinned down

## The city is Punta Gorda, not Fort Myers

House of Rose Aesthetics is at **525 E Olympia Ave, Unit 9, Punta Gorda, FL 33950**, phone **(844) 941-7673**, open **Monday–Friday 9 AM–5 PM**, with walk-ins accepted "when the schedule allows." Source: <https://houseofrosefl.com/>

The site describes its catchment in its own words: **"Punta Gorda, Florida — Serving Charlotte County and Southwest Florida"** and **"A focused practice in Southwest Florida."** Source: <https://houseofrosefl.com/>

This matters for the whole brief. Punta Gorda is in **Charlotte County**, roughly 25 miles north of Fort Myers, which is in Lee County. The initial hypothesis that this was a Fort Myers-area business was directionally right about the region and wrong about the market. **The correction is strategically load-bearing**, because as Phase 2 shows, the businesses that dominate Punta Gorda search results are mostly headquartered in Lee County and are renting the geography through landing pages. That is the single biggest exploitable fact in this document.

## Site architecture — genuinely strong, and ahead of the business

House of Rose has content infrastructure most of its competitors lack entirely. All linked from <https://houseofrosefl.com/>:

- **`/cost/`** — nine treatment cost guides (Morpheus8, Forma RF, IPL Photofacial, PRF Microneedling, PRF Injections, Microneedling, Botox, BioRePeel, Dermal Fillers)
- **`/compare/`** — treatment comparison hub
- **`/concerns/`** — concern-led entry pages
- **`/areas/`** — service-area pages
- **`/skin-analysis/`** — multi-spectrum imaging
- **`/results/`**, **`/faq/`**, **`/blog/`**, **`/experience/`**, **`/shop/`** (with Jane Iredale), **`/rent-a-room/`**

Clean canonicals, full Open Graph, `index, follow`, `max-image-preview:large`, Google Search Console verification present. Source: page head of <https://houseofrosefl.com/> and <https://houseofrosefl.com/cost/>

**And here is the self-inflicted wound.** The cost guides publish no costs. The Botox cost guide is a well-written 600-word page titled *"How Much Does Botox Cost in Punta Gorda?"* whose meta description promises *"How Botox pricing works in Punta Gorda — per-unit dosing, areas treated, and units needed"* — and it contains **not one dollar figure.** It closes with *"Your unit count and investment are confirmed at your consultation."* Source: <https://houseofrosefl.com/cost/botox-cost-punta-gorda/>

The `/cost/` index sets the same expectation and does not meet it: *"Straight answers on how treatments at House of Rose are priced… Your exact pricing is confirmed at your consultation."* Source: <https://houseofrosefl.com/cost/>

A page built to rank for "how much does Botox cost" that never answers the question will lose the click to Google's own AI Overview, which — as Phase 5 documents — is already answering that exact question on this exact SERP. The architecture is right. The payload is missing.
