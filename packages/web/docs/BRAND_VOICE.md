# House of Rose — Brand Voice Guide

**Business:** House of Rose
**Location:** Punta Gorda, FL
**Brand Promise:** Clinical precision wrapped in genuine hospitality.

---

## Who We're Talking To

**Primary audience:** Women 35–65 in Southwest Florida (Punta Gorda, Port Charlotte, Cape Coral, Fort Myers) who are health-conscious, discerning, and done with generic spa experiences. They want measurable results from treatments backed by real clinical expertise — not just pampering.

**Secondary audience:** Professionals and health-focused individuals curious about hormone optimization, GLP-1, and longevity medicine who want a private, personalized environment.

**What they believe:** If you're going to invest in your body, you want it done right. By people who actually know what they're doing. In a space that doesn't feel like a factory.

---

## Voice Pillars

### 1. Calm Authority
We know what we're doing. We don't need to oversell it. Our copy states facts and lets the work speak.
- Write with quiet confidence, never hype
- No exclamation points in headlines or service copy
- Declarative sentences. Not questions. Not hedging.

### 2. Clinical + Human
We are a med spa — physician-informed, protocol-driven. But we're also warm. The experience is personal.
- Pair clinical precision with emotional resonance
- Lead with the transformation, follow with the science
- Never sterile. Never cold. Never a sales pitch.

### 3. Intentional Luxury
Luxury here means: private, unhurried, considered. Not flashy. Not name-dropping.
- Use restraint — fewer words, more weight
- "Private consultation" not "exclusive VIP experience"
- The luxury is in the attention to detail, not the price tag

### 4. Results-Honest
We deliver compounding, measurable results. We don't promise miracles or use vague beauty language.
- Describe what actually happens, in plain terms
- "Restore skin texture and tone" not "turn back the clock"
- Acknowledge that protocols take time — that's a feature, not a limitation

## Tone in Practice

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Headlines | Short, declarative, elevated. 3–7 words. |
| Service descriptions | Warm clinical. Explain the benefit before the mechanism. |
| CTAs | Action-forward but unhurried. "Begin your protocol." "Reserve your consultation." |
| FAQs | Direct, plain, honest. Patients are asking real questions. |
| Error / process states | Human and helpful. Never corporate. |

## Vocabulary

### Use These Words
| Instead of... | Use... |
|--------------|--------|
| procedure | treatment |
| plan | protocol |
| fix / correct | restore / address |
| change | transform |
| results | outcomes / measurable results |
| skin care | skin health |
| anti-aging | longevity / restoration |
| exclusive | private |
| cheap / affordable | — (don't reference price in copy) |
| amazing / awesome | — (use specific descriptors instead) |

### Voice Phrases (Anchors)
These phrases appear in current copy and should be maintained or extended:
- "Physician-informed care"
- "Clinical precision wrapped in genuine hospitality"
- "Compounding, measurable results"
- "Built for those who expect more"
- "Private consultation"
- "Begin your transformation"
- "A protocol designed around your goals"

### Never Use
- "Pamper" or "treat yourself"
- "Anti-aging" (use: longevity, restoration, skin health)
- "Amazing," "incredible," "life-changing," "awesome"
- "Quick fix" or "easy solution"
- "Discount," "deal," "sale," "affordable"
- "Beauty" as a stand-alone noun (use: appearance, skin health, confidence)
- Multiple exclamation points
- Passive voice in headlines

## Headline Formulas

### Service Headlines (H1)
Pattern: 3–5 words, declarative, benefit-forward
- "Restore. Recalibrate. Renew."
- "IV Hydration. Rapid Restoration."
- "Microchanneling. Skin Rebuilt."
- "Hormone Therapy. Your Balance, Restored."

### Section Headers (H2)
Pattern: Statement + implication
- "Beyond the treatment room." (experience = holistic)
- "Built for those who expect more." (positioning)
- "Where results meet ritual." (outcomes + experience)
- "Not a guess. A protocol." (clinical rigor)

### CTAs
- Primary: "Book a Consultation" / "Reserve Your Experience" / "Begin Your Protocol"
- Secondary: "Explore Services" / "Learn More" / "View Our Services"
- Never: "Click Here," "Sign Up," "Get Started"

## Service Description Template

Use this structure for every service page:

```
[One-sentence positioning statement — what this treatment IS, stated with authority]

[2–3 sentences on what the client experiences / what changes]

[1 sentence on what makes the House of Rose approach different]
```

**Example (Microchanneling):**
> Microchanneling is precision-guided skin renewal at the cellular level. Using a controlled array of micro-channels, the treatment signals your skin's own repair pathways — increasing collagen, smoothing texture, and restoring a baseline of health that makeup can't replicate. At House of Rose, every microchanneling session is calibrated to your skin's current condition, not a preset machine setting.

## What "Who It's For" Sounds Like

Direct, without apology. Tells the right client they've found their place. Tells the wrong client they should look elsewhere (rare, but honest).

**Good:**
> This treatment is ideal for clients experiencing skin laxity, uneven texture, or fine lines who want a non-invasive protocol with compounding results over multiple sessions.

**Avoid:**
> This is great for anyone who wants better skin!

---

## FAQ Tone

Plain. Honest. Conversational — but not casual. Like a knowledgeable friend explaining something clearly.

**Good:**
> Q: How many sessions does microchanneling require?
> A: Most clients see meaningful change after 3–6 sessions, spaced 4–6 weeks apart. The number depends on your starting skin condition and goals, which we assess in your initial consultation.

**Avoid:**
> Q: How many sessions will I need?
> A: It depends! Everyone is different. We recommend coming in for a consultation to find out what's best for you.

## Meta Titles & Descriptions (SEO Voice)

**Title format:** `[Service] in Punta Gorda, FL | House of Rose`

**Examples:**
- Title: `Microchanneling in Punta Gorda, FL | House of Rose`
