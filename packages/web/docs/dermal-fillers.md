## What is safe to explain

- All five listed products are manufactured hyaluronic-acid gels.
- The reviewed public service names the lips, cheeks, and folds as the selected areas a client may
  discuss.
- Dermal fillers address a different question from Botox or Daxxify: the current filler page discusses
  selected areas of lost volume, while the neurotoxin page discusses lines related to facial movement.
- Product choice is not a client prerequisite. Diana Morrison, RN makes treatment-planning decisions
  after reviewing the client and the area of concern.
- Hyaluronic-acid filler and PRF Bio-Filler are separate current appointment paths. The current menu
  does not establish that PRF Bio-Filler is EZ-Gel. Do not use those names interchangeably and do not
  create an EZ-Gel route.

## Provider and claims boundary

Where Diana is named, use **Diana Morrison, RN**. Do not substitute a job title for the RN licence
type. Do not imply that Joshua Shaw, MD performs treatments, is on site, or holds a specialty not
verified in the binding rules.

Place safeguards where the content creates the need:

- When copy discusses product choice, amount, placement, or candidacy, make clear that Diana Morrison,
  RN makes those decisions under the written physician protocol and medical direction.
- When copy makes a result claim, include concise individual-variation language supported by the
  claim's source. A product name, current price, duration, or provider attribution is not itself a
  result claim.
- Use candidacy or contraindication information only when it answers a real client safety question and
  the exact fact has been clinically reviewed. Do not turn a general clinical checklist into website
  copy.
- Before-and-after evidence may publish only with documented consent.

Do not publish guarantees, permanence, disease-treatment language, unsupported clearance claims,
discount framing, or an assumption that one product is the default for a body area. Do not create a
sales sequence that directs clients from fillers into neurotoxins, PRF, facials, products, or another
purchase.
