## What the evidence can support

PRF is prepared from a client's own blood without an anticoagulant. Centrifugation produces a fibrin
matrix containing platelets and growth factors. Facial-aesthetics literature provides a rationale for
gradual changes in skin quality and selected under-eye concerns, but the evidence base remains limited
by small studies, varied preparations, and short follow-up.

Use the narrowest source-supported statement needed for the page. A laboratory mechanism is not a
clinical result, and a result reported for one preparation, placement, or treatment area cannot be
transferred to another.
