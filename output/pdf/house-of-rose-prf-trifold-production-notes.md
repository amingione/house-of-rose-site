# House of Rose PRF Tri-Fold - Production Notes

## Print setup

- Final format: two-page, two-sided US Letter (11 x 8.5 inches), landscape.
- Print at 100% / Actual Size. Do not use Fit to Page.
- For duplex printing in landscape orientation, select Flip on Short Edge.
- The outside spread is page 1. The inside spread is page 2.
- The outside panel order is: narrow fold-in flap, back panel, front cover.
- With the outside spread facing you, fold the narrow left flap inward first, then fold the right front-cover panel over it.
- Run one physical proof before ordering or printing a full quantity. Confirm panel alignment, printer margins, QR scanning, and paper grain with the selected printer.
- Suggested stock: 80-100 lb text or a similar smooth, uncoated or silk brochure stock that accepts a clean fold without cracking.
